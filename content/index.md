## 📖 Previously, in [[Yartar]] — [[Session 76 - Var - 2026-10-26|Session 76]] (2026-10-26)

**[[Var Borgal|Var]] disguised himself as [[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] and dismissed the anti-magic knight guarding the prison block — and it worked.** We **freed everyone in the cells, children and adults**, took **a necrotic wave from a trap** on the ladder out, lost no one, and **left them at the nearest bell tower**. Sneaking back to the inn to heal, we woke **in shackles with a female [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mage]] and her escort in our room**: she **read Var's mind for everything on Cardon and [[Goldenfields]], including the ward mission [[Naxene Drathkala|Naxene]] wiped from our memories**, and came away with **the ward locations**. Her offer, which we had no choice but to take: **get her what's inside an anti-magic knight and she'll let Var live**, with no deadline, though we assume they're watching. In the morning [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] **told [[Naxene Drathkala|Naxene]] what happened and everything about Var's note**, and we **gave [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]]'s body to a [[Harpers|Harper]] at the [[Through and Through Detective Agency]]**. At the docks **[[Jarlaxle|Jarlaxle Baenre]] traded Var the location of Cardon and [[Doctor Hadol]] for a like-for-like favor**, and we ended the session **sneaking into what we think is Cardon's hideout northeast of [[Yartar]]**, past **lamps run on technology instead of fire**, looking at **copper cylinders that may store power**.
