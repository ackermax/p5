## 📖 Previously, in Flint Rock and Yartar — [[Session 70 - Var - 2026-08-31|Session 70]] (2026-08-31)

We got the hundred-pound giant's head back to the [[Ignitamis]] and flew to [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]], where nothing looked wrong at all until we climbed to the top and found **incense burning** — and **moving the altar opened a passage down to the relic**. Then the **spirits of the Elk clan came out and asked us what our intentions were**, and told us the relic wasn't ours without the blessing of **[[The Golden Stag]]**; we made camp, **saw the Stag passing that evening**, approached, and it **asked each of us in turn what we had done to restore nature's balance.** We answered, and it **found us wanting — but far enough along** to be worth blessing, **because the breaking of [[The Ordening]] is itself a disruption of that balance** — so in the morning we went back under the altar and the spirits **gave us the relic and their own blessing with it: the tip of a mithral spear**, only the tip. **Relic #5, and the first one anybody has handed us since the Sky Ponies.** Then we flew to **[[Yartar]]** — the city half our threads point at — and got **stopped outside the walls by a magical ward**, sent to the guard tower, and **searched for dangerous items in the guardhouse** by **[[Oversword Vaelia Thann]]**, who told us the Waterbaron here is **[[Nestra Ruthiol]]**. **We passed the magic checks and walked in**, and **left the airship with an older gnome who's filling the tank and making repairs** — which is our oil problem and our repair list both being solved by the same stranger, who never gave us a name.
