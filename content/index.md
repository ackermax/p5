## 📖 Previously, in [[Yartar]] — [[Session 74 - Var - 2026-10-12|Session 74]] (2026-10-12)

We pushed east through the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] dungeon to where **flesh golems were burning bodies**, destroyed them, then doubled back through rooms we'd passed and **found five beads of force.** Heading north we cut through **more cultists** and found **a book showing that the young people brought here had their blood extracted for some sort of ritual — the oldest of them is 15.** At the top of the dungeon a hooded figure turned out to be **[[Rulf Cloudfang|Rulf]]**, leader of [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s old troupe and now **the current leader of the Cult of Cyric**, and the truth came out: **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] was being groomed to lead the cult after him, Jaak was only ever wanted for his blood to finish the ritual, and the ritual is meant to bring [[Cyric]] back.** We thought we'd killed him, but when Jaak went to check, **the body dissipated as if it had never been there**, and **Rulf stabbed [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] to death as he vanished into the ether.** Among Ilin's belongings were **a picture of Jaak's troupe and his notes on the cult and his investigation.** **We're carrying his body, and still searching the dungeon for any children left alive.**
