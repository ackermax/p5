## 📖 Previously, in Everlund and the Stone Stand Mound — [[Session 69 - Var - 2026-08-24|Session 69]] (2026-08-24)

We finally walked into [[Moongleam Tower]] with the ghost job finished, and [[Krowen Valharrow]] paid us in something better than treasure — **access**: the [[Harpers|Harper teleportation-circle network]], with a circle of our own going into the [[Phandalin|bastion]] once we gather the materials, and **[[Droop]] installed as our resident mage** to attend it. **[[Everlund]], [[Waterdeep]], [[Other Locations#Mirabar|Mirabar]], [[Yartar]], our bastion and "others" are connected; [[Other Locations#Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] is closed off because of **Neverember**, and [[Other Locations#Baldur's Gate|Baldur's Gate]] isn't on it** — **500 gp of diamond dust moves six of us, and the attendant has to be there.** The network is a Harper secret that **only Bright Candles and High Harpers** are told about, which is why the audience he'd been holding since S66 turned out to be a **promotion: [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] are now [[Harpers|Bright Candles]]** — **and that was the whole audience**, so [[Sister Garaele]]'s promised [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]] contact at the tower is **still unaccounted for**. We shopped with [[Clint]], then went to the [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Stone Stand]] — the Blue Bear mound we'd flagged *avoid* — **on foot**, because the mound is off the circle network and the [[Ignitamis]] **didn't have the oil to fly there and still make the leg after it**. [[Var Borgal|Var]] paid respects to the dead on arrival and **got back a vision of a tribe that killed anyone who came near**, plus the certainty that reaching for the relic would wake them. So we didn't go in together: **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] and [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard|Sir Joseph]] snuck in alone**, located the relic by spell, and dug while **the tribe's ghosts rose out of the graves and came for them**. They got it up, grabbed it, and **dimension-doored clear of the burial ground** — **that is where the session ends**, with our **fourth relic** in hand: **a giant's skull weighing a hundred pounds**, and no idea yet whether the Blue Bear dead let us keep it.
