# Ojin Voninsdottir

> **Race / Role:** Ulfgardian — **leader of the Black Raven tribe** at **[[Ulfgardian Mounds#Raven Rock|Raven Rock]]**
> **Disposition toward party:** Willing to deal, **on her terms**. She gave us a job, not a welcome.
> **First met:** [[Session 79 - Var - 2026-11-16|Session 79]] at [[Ulfgardian Mounds#Raven Rock|Raven Rock]]

## What We Know

- Known to us **by reputation** long before S79: **cold, heartless, old**, and **do NOT kill her**. *(From the pre-import notes; see [[Minor Contacts#Ulfgardian Chieftains|Minor Contacts]].)* The advice was always that **she has a problem in the mountains — help her, get a relic.**
- **Met S79.** We started with the Black Ravens when we reached Raven Rock, and **spoke with Ojin as their leader.**
- **Her people really don't like magic.** We found that out **after [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] used a spell to communicate with them.**
- **The Black Ravens and the Gray Wolves are pacted: fight one, fight both.** *(Var: "a pact to fight alongside the grey wolves if we get into a fight with them.")* The Gray Wolves are the other tribe at Raven Rock; **Syken Nightblaze** is trying to claim their chieftainship.
- **The Gray Wolves' price for not attacking us is [[Interitus]]'s head.** **We read that as his tribe having turned on him** (see his note).

## What She Wants

- **The monster at the top of the mountain killed.** **Great white wings, and it kills deer.** She **sent us up the mountain to fight it** (S79). Presumably the relic is the reward; **that hasn't been stated.**

## Relationships

- Leads the **Black Ravens**, who share [[Ulfgardian Mounds#Raven Rock|Raven Rock]], the oldest mound, with the **Gray Wolves**
- **Pacted to the Gray Wolves: fight one, fight both** *(S79)*
- The Gray Wolves are **[[Interitus]]'s tribe**, and **we believe they've turned on him**

## Notable Moments

- **S79:** the mountain job. **We traded the splint armor made in our [[Phandalin|bastion]] for hides** to survive the cold of the climb. Whether we traded with Ojin's people specifically isn't recorded.

## Open Questions

- **What's actually on the mountain?** Everything pointed to **a dragon**, but the cave held **kobolds riding frost wolves**. Var's question: **would a white dragon really be here, given how much they usually love their solitude?**
- **Does killing it earn the Black Ravens' relic, or both of Raven Rock's relics?**
- **How much did Jaak's spell cost us with her?**
