# Interitus

> **Also known as:** The Black Wolf
> **Race:** **Human — werewolf**
> **Affiliations:** **[[Force Grey]]**; [[Ulfgardian Mounds|wolf guardian of the gray wolf tribe]]
> **Disposition:** Friendly (to [[Harshnag]]'s allies)

## Who He Is

A werewolf and **Force Grey member**, a wolf guardian belonging to the **gray wolf tribe** of the [[Ulfgardian Mounds]] tradition. **First met [[Harshnag]] in battle, lost, then they spoke and became traveling partners.** Currently in [[Kryptgarden Forest]].

To approach him: **speak Harshnag's name.**

## What He Wanted From Us

He **asked us to kill the necromancer** in [[Kryptgarden Forest]]. We did — **before even meeting him**. The necromancer turned out to be running the [[The Death Knight Ritual]] for the [[Cult of Cyric]]. Interitus's tip was high-value intelligence.

## What He's Told Us About The Mounds

- **Grandfather Tree** — Interitus **asked us not to go**. This is notable; the [[Ulfgardian Mounds#Grandfather Tree|Grandfather Tree]] is generally regarded as sacred. He may have a reason; respect it for now.
- Multiple oracles among the mound tribes **demand sacrifices of giant relics to answer questions** — including the question of how to fix [[The Ordening]]

## Messages

- **To [[Harshnag]]:** "Next time I'll be the one who wins our duel"
