# Countess Sansuri

> **Race:** Cloud Giant
> **Title:** **Mistress of the Ninefold Veil**
> **Disposition:** Hostile

## What She's Done

- **Probably ordered the cloud giant attack on Nightstone** (per longstanding rumor)
- **Writing demanding letters to [[Zephyros]]** — pulling him out of his isolationist tower

## The Sansuri-Zephyros Letter (Recovered)

> Sansuri's words, addressed to Zephyros — recovered by the party.

> "You have ignored my summons long enough. Do not think the winds conceal you from me — I know your tower still drifts among the northern skies. I have no time for your isolationist musings or your endless observations of clouds and omens.
>
> **The time for theory has passed; the time for action is at hand. I am on the verge of something, and if my calculations are correct, this discovery will change everything we thought possible. Power beyond our reach is stirring — and I intend to grasp it.**
>
> I will not commit details to parchment, not when so many eyes and spells seek to pry. You will have to come and see for yourself. Bring your wisdom, your artifacts, and whatever sense of purpose remains within that ancient mind of yours.
>
> This is the final time I will ask politely. The world is shifting, and those who linger behind will be left among the dust and bones of forgotten mages."

— *Countess Sansuri, Mistress of the Ninefold Veil*

## What This Tells Us

- Sansuri is pursuing **"power beyond our reach"** that is **"stirring"** — likely tied to [[The Ordening]]'s collapse and the [[The Second Sundering|age of mortals]]
- She wants Zephyros's *artifacts* — he has something specific she lacks
- **She is paranoid about prying eyes** — won't write specifics. Suggests Harpers or rivals are surveilling her.
- "The world is shifting" — explicit recognition the cosmic order is changing
- Her threat is real: "those who linger behind will be left among the dust and bones"

## Affiliation

- Follows **[[Memnor]]** (the "Masked Ones") — evil-aligned cloud giant faction
- Compare with [[Mulara and Nimbolo]] — also cloud giants holding a Memnor statue but with a more scholarly bent

## Open Questions

- **What is she on the verge of?** Some link to [[Hekaton]]'s disappearance? An artifact tied to the [[The Ordening|Ordening]]? A path to ascension during the age of mortals?
- Is she allied with the [[Cult of the Dragon]] or operating parallel to it?
- Will Zephyros actually come, or has he gone deeper into hiding?
