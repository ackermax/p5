## What We Know

- The name came up while we were learning that **the apparitions at the mound are aasimar** — see [[The Everlund Apparitions]].
- Described as **the last of the aasimar at "this base"** — implying the mound was one site among others, and that the aasimar there are gone.
- [[Jaak Greye]] used **his own aasimar heritage** (plus charisma) to make the spectrals in the main chamber disperse without a fight. Whether Altariar was among them is unrecorded.

## Relationships

- Connected to [[Aasimar Temple|the Aasimar Temple]] — the aasimar temple/mausoleum
- Potentially relevant to [[Jaak Greye]] and [[Jaaks Celestial War]] — the first aasimar other than Jaak this campaign has put in front of us
