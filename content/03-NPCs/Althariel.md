# Althariel

> **Race / Class:** **Aasimar**
> **Role:** **The last of the aasimar at this base.** The one who **brought [[The Angel|the Angel]] here** and sealed it — and who died for it.
> **Disposition toward party:** **Grateful.** He thanked us and armed [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]].
> **First heard of:** [[Session 67 - Jaak - 2026-08-10|Session 67]] | **Met (as a spirit):** [[Session 68 - Var - 2026-08-17|Session 68]]

> [!info] Spelling
> S67 recorded him as **"Altariar"**; in S68 both players wrote him as **"Althariel"** *(Var also once as "Altharial")*. **The vault spells it Althariel**, with **Altariar** and **Altharial** kept as aliases so older links and the players' own session notes still resolve.

## What We Know

- **He brought [[The Angel|the Angel]] here years ago** and it was sealed beneath the site — the whole [[Aasimar Temple]] is, in effect, its cell.
- **He and his kin were killed** — by the thing he'd imprisoned. **They became the ghosts** haunting the forest, the ones the lumberjacks saw: sad, sunken-eyed, wings shedding feathers.
- **His spirit appeared to us after we destroyed the Angel**, in the place where it had been locked up, and **thanked us.**
- **He knew answers** — and gave them freely. He is the source of everything below.

## What He Told Us *(S68)*

**On [[Jaak Greye]]:**
- Jaak is a **celestial** (which we knew), of the **"Thier"** blood *(spelling unconfirmed)*, and **came from Celestia.**
- **Jaak has the [[Blood of the Ancients|blood of the ancients]].**
- **So does [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]].**
- **That blood makes a person strong enough to be an avatar — a vessel of the gods.**

**On the gods:**
- **[[Bhaal]]**, god of murder, **tried to use a person of ancient blood as a vessel to inhabit.**
- **[[Cyric]]** is **the most dangerous god** — a **human who ascended**, domain of **lies and greed**, a trickster, who **almost destroyed the world by killing [[Mystra]]**, at which point **all magic ceased to exist.**

**On where to go next:**
- **A group of aasimar at the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]] may be able to help Jaak.**
- **The [[Order of the Gauntlet]] was created by aasimar.**

**And he gave [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] the [[Items and References|Sword of Heavens]]**, then **remade the portal** and sent us back to the tomb.

## Relationships

- **[[The Angel]]** — his prisoner, and his killer
- **[[Aasimar Temple]]** — his base; "**this base**" implies **there were others**
- **[[Jaak Greye]]** — recognized his blood, named his origin, and armed him
- **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]** — named her as sharing the ancient bloodline
- **[[Order of the Gauntlet]]** — founded by his people

## Open Questions

- **Where are the other bases?** He was the last aasimar *at this one*.
- **Why did he bring the Angel here** instead of destroying it? Who was he taking it *from*?
- **How did he know about Jaak and Estelle's blood on sight** — is it visible to aasimar, or did he know of them already?
- **Who is "the Lord"** the Angel promised would return? Did Althariel know, and did we forget to ask?
- Does he connect to [[Jaaks Celestial War|Jaak's fragmentary memories of the celestial war]] — the chariot, the stray attack, the faceless figures watching him fall?
