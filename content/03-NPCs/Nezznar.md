# Nezznar

> **Title:** "The Spyder"
> **Race:** Drow
> **Faction:** **Loyalists of Lolth** ([[Society of the Drow]])
> **Status:** **Dead** (party arc)

## Who He Was

Nezznar was the puppet-master behind [[The Red Brands]] in [[Phandalin]]. **[[Iarno Albrek|Glasstaff]] answered to him.** A note from him was found on Glasstaff's desk during the manor raid.

## Vyerith

Also a drow, present with Nezznar at **Cragmaw Castle** when the party rescued [[Gundren Rockseeker]]. Same faction (Loyalists of Lolth). **Also dead.**

## What Remains

- The **Society of the Drow / Loyalists of Lolth** still exist as a faction
- Nezznar's network extended further than Phandalin — the underground he was operating in connects to [[Old Owl Well]]'s Underdark passages and beyond
- "Koleen overheard conversation with drow. She heard someone coming red brands, **one of the drow appeared here out of nowhere**" — drow have ways of moving silently into and out of the area
