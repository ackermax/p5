# Arnzan Vashk

> **Race / Role:** Ulfgardian human — **chieftain of the Sky Pony tribe** at **[[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]]**
> **Disposition toward party:** **Ally** — we cured him and he gave us his relic freely
> **First met:** [[Session 66 - Gotrek - 2026-08-03|Session 66]] at [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]]

## What We Know

- Long known to us **by reputation only** as the **dying chieftain of One Stone** — the mound where the approach was always "help him with his illness, get the relic."
- **He was genuinely dying.** [[Jaak Greye]] examined him and diagnosed **necrotizing fasciitis** *(Gotrek's notes record it simply as "a bad disease needing Greater Restoration")*. Jaak judged **Greater Restoration** a guaranteed cure and **Lesser Restoration** a serious risk of critical failure.
- **Cured in [[Everlund]] (S66)** — we flew him there aboard the [[Ignitamis]] and paid **1,600 gp** to a hired mage for *greater restoration*. It worked.
- He **asked why we'd done it**; the party deferred the conversation to a feast and the next morning. Back at One Stone he **gave us the mound's relic willingly** once asked — see [[Gather Ulfgardian Relics]].
- He told us about **[[Ituvalak]]** — described as "**children of the forest**." *(Context not recorded; see Open Questions.)*

## Relationships

- Chieftain of the **Sky Ponies**, the Ulfgardians who surrounded us at [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]] and then **fought the [[Black Earth]] alongside us** once [[Jaak Greye]] convinced them we were healers
- Hostile to the **[[Black Earth]]**, who were at One Stone claiming the relic as theirs
- Our first real **Ulfgardian alliance** — potentially a door into the other mounds and the oracles

## Notable Moments

- **S66:** the language barrier broke when Jaak used **Major Image** to *show* himself healing the Sky Pony leader — it landed perfectly and turned a three-way fight into an alliance. Jaak later cast **tongues** to actually talk, and the tribesmen brought us to Vashk.

## Open Questions

- **What is [[Ituvalak]]**, and why did he raise it? "Children of the forest" — a people, a place, or something else?
- We **never told him we wanted his relic** before curing him (a deliberate choice). He gave it anyway and was happy to — does he expect anything back?
- Can he **vouch for us with the other mounds** or with the **oracles**?
