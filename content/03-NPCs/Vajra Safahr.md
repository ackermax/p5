# Vajra Safahr

> **Title:** **The Blackstaff** (7th holder of the title)
> **Location:** [[Waterdeep]] — leader of Blackstaff Academy
> **Disposition:** Ally (presumed); regarded skeptically by other archmages

## Profile

- The **7th Blackstaff**
- **Not well regarded by the archmages** — they don't believe she's powerful enough to lead the academy
- **[[Naxene Drathkala]] likes her** — this is meaningful since Naxene is a working wizard who'd know
- **Member of Blackstaff Academy along with [[Laeral Silverhand]]**
- Closer to the Weave than typical

## What She Can Do For Us

- **Guide us to the cloud giant relic in the Black Tower** ([[Harrick Stonequill]] found it)
- Likely give intel on the **age of mortals / [[The Second Sundering]]** and what's happening to the Weave

## Lineage Of The Blackstaff

- **Khelben Arunsun** was a previous Blackstaff and **husband to [[Laeral Silverhand]]**
- Khelben **sacrificed his life to defeat something powerful** — died twice
- He and **Ualair the Silent One** gave their lives to **restore something to what it was before** (in Highmoor, a city that was lost)
- **The Blackstaff seat was vacant for 60 years** before Vajra
- **[[Droop]]** is her apprentice — *though he was at [[Moongleam Tower]] in [[Everlund]], not the Academy, in S66*
