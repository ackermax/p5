# Stonadar

> **Race:** Stone Giant
> **Location:** Settled around the [[Ice Peak]]

Loner stone giant who traveled with [[Master Miros]] and eventually settled near the Ice Peak. Belonged to one of the two [[Stone Giant Factions]] (rockspeakers / dreamwalkers) — which one is worth confirming, given [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s rune connection.
