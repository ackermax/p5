# Skeemo Weirdbottle

> **Race / Class:** Goblin
> **Role:** Owner of **Weirdbottle Concoctions** (potion shop, [[Waterdeep]])
> **Disposition toward party:** Friendly facade — actual loyalties under investigation
> **First met:** [[Session 56 - Var - 2026-05-18]] at his shop
> **Status (S59):** **Taken into custody by the [[Harpers]].** The [[Council of the Underworld]] has posted a **10,000 gp bounty to rescue him** (raised by [[Davil Starsong]]).

## Captured By The Harpers (Session 59)

At the [[Council of the Underworld]] meeting, **[[Davil Starsong]]** (leader of the [[Black Network|Zhentarim]] *and* the [[Doom Raiders]], Skeemo's old band) revealed the **Harpers have taken Skeemo** and suspected a "third party" set it up — **that third party was us.** Davil **posted a 10,000 gp bounty to spring him.**

- The Harper investigation we were running (S56–58) evidently **paid off** — they moved on Skeemo.
- **We're on the opposite side of the Council's bounty.** Action item: **tell the Harpers ASAP** that the underworld is coming for their prisoner (and may know a third party was involved).
- Skeemo is now a **bargaining chip / interrogation source** — he knows the cult's "anointed location" and the Enhanced Torpor plan.

## What We Know

- Owns **Weirdbottle Concoctions** in [[Waterdeep]] — *"Potions and Elixirs, Remedies & Tonics! For All Ailments & Afflictions!"*
- **Former member of the [[Doom Raiders]]** — a retired adventuring band the [[Harpers]] consider were "not decent people" and who built ties to the **[[Black Network|Zhentarim]]**
- **[[Harpers]] believe he is currently working with the Zhentarim** and want evidence to make a move on him
- Has been **commissioned by the [[Cult of Cyric]]** to brew **Enhanced Torpor**

## Our Session With Him

- We sold him some potions
- Promised to sell him a **larger potion** in a few days once he gathered the gold
- He offered **3,000 gp for our Oil of Etherealness** — 50% above typical market price; **highly motivated to acquire it**
- We **agreed not to sell it to him**, per Harper guidance
- [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] **snooped his back rooms** and recovered the Cult letter and a wanted poster for [[Var Borgal]]

## The Recovered Letter

From the [[Cult of Cyric]] / "[[The Circle]]" to Skeemo, asking him to refine a brew called **Enhanced Torpor**:

> **To Skeemo Weirdbottle, Keeper of Curious Brews,**
>
> The Circle turns, and the hour we spoke of draws near.
>
> Your mixture—*Enhanced Torpor*—is required at once. The refinement you promised, the one that stills both body and will, must be delivered to the anointed location without delay. You already know the place. No written names. No mistakes.
>
> The plans proceed as foretold. Every moment lost invites eyes we do not welcome.
>
> Ensure the dosage is… sufficient. There can be no resistance this time.
>
> You will be compensated as agreed, with favor beyond coin should this unfold as intended. Fail us, and the Circle will remember.
>
> Come unseen. Leave nothing behind but silence.
>
> **— The Cult of C**

## What This Tells Us

- The cult was **previously frustrated** — "no resistance this time" suggests an earlier dosing failed
- Skeemo **already promised this refinement** to them — he is a willing supplier, not coerced
- **A specific location** is the drop point — known to him, not us
- "Stills both body and will" — they're trying to neutralize a target without killing them. **Capture, not kill.**
