# Klauth

> **Also known as:** The Great Dragon of the North
> **Role:** Patron / quest-giver
> **Disposition:** Helpful (so far)

## What Happened

Klauth **gave us an airship**. He's been watching us, and wants to help fix the [[The Ordening|Ordening]]. Asked us to visit him after we succeed.

## What This Means

- An ancient red dragon is watching our every move. Comforting.
- His motives almost certainly extend past "helping" — old reds don't do altruism. He benefits from a stable Ordening somehow, or from us specifically.
- He's positioned himself as an ally **before** asking anything in return. That's the play.

- Name: **Ignitamis**
- **Tools needed for repair:** Tinker's tools, Woodcarver's tools, Carpenter's tools, Tailoring tools
