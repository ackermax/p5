# Countess Mulara & Count Nimbolo

> **Race:** Cloud Giants
> **Occupation:** **Cartographers** living in a **flying castle**
> **Disposition:** Neutral / scholarly so far

## What They Have

A **statue of [[Memnor]]**, the first of the cloud giants.

## What They're Doing

Searching for ancient giant relics from their flying castle. Cartographer-scientists; their work is mapping. Notably **less militant** in framing than [[Countess Sansuri]] — they hold [[Memnor]] iconography but don't behave like the Masked Ones.

## Recent Contact (Session 56)

- [[Var Borgal]] **messaged them** that we could not deliver the relic at this moment
- He offered that **once we resolve the broader situation, we may meet to either return it or share what we learned about it**
- **They agreed** and returned to their cartographical work
- This is a low-friction relationship to maintain — they're patient, scholarly, not chasing us
