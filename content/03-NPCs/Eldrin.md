# Eldrin

> **Relation:** [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s **brother**
> **Status:** **Alive — in hiding**
> **First contacted:** In the **dreamrealm**, by [[Gotrek Runehammer]] (last session)

## Identity

For most of the campaign, Gotrek's brother was unnamed in our notes — referenced only as the man who was hunting **[[Thane Kayalithica]]**. As of last session he is confirmed: **his name is Eldrin**, and Gotrek encountered him in the dreamrealm.

## What Gotrek Learned

- Eldrin is **in hiding** — survived the hunt
- He sent Gotrek to the **[[Cradle of the Stonegiants]]** (where Gotrek originated)
- He shared what he has learned about **[[Thane Kayalithica]]**'s plan (see her note)
- He apparently has the ability to reach Gotrek through the dreamrealm — implying **dreamweaver / Dreamwalker** affinity, or that he's working with someone who has it

## Open Questions

- Where, physically, is Eldrin hiding?
- How did he survive his hunt for Kayalithica?
- Is his ability to reach Gotrek through dreams *his own* gift, or a borrowed channel?
- What happened to him between his original hunt and now?
- The earlier framing was "the brothers are on opposite sides of the oldest stone giant schism." With Eldrin now reaching Gotrek through the **dreamrealm**, that framing may need revision — or Eldrin has shifted sides.
