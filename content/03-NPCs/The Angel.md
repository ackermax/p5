# The Angel

> **Race / Class:** Unknown — a **caged, one-eyed monster**. The only name we have is the one its tooltip gave: **"Angel."**
> **Role:** The thing the [[Althariel|aasimar]] imprisoned here — and the thing that killed them
> **Disposition toward party:** Hostile. Destroyed by the party.
> **First met:** [[Session 68 - Var - 2026-08-17|Session 68]], through the portal at [[Aasimar Temple|the Aasimar Temple]]

> [!warning] "Angel" is what the DM's tooltip called it — not a description
> It did not look like an angel. It looked like a **one-eyed monster in a cage**. Treat the name as a label, not a species.

## What We Know

- **[[Althariel]] brought it here years ago** and it was **sealed behind the altar**, reachable only by opening the portal with all four **keystones**.
- It **killed Althariel and his kin** — the aasimar of this base. Their deaths are why the site is haunted.
- It spoke of **needing to cleanse our world**, then **broke out of its cage** and attacked.
- **It controlled the plane it was held in** — an effect hit the party **every round** — and **summoned flying swords** to fight alongside it.
- As it went down it said **"the Lord" will return to cleanse our world.** It has a master, and the master is still out there.
- **Destroyed** after a very tough fight *(S68)*.

## The Ghosts' Version

The **mournful ghosts** at the site told us they **angered something very powerful from a different dimension**, and that it is **sending probes into ours to consume the plane**. The Angel reads as one of those probes — see [[Aasimar Temple]] and [[The Everlund Apparitions]].

## Open Questions

- **Who is "the Lord"?** A god? The thing from the other dimension the ghosts angered? The same power both times, or two?
- **How many other probes are there** — and are the other aasimar "bases" holding them?
- **Why did Althariel bring it here** rather than destroy it?
- Does any of this touch [[Jaaks Celestial War|Jaak's celestial war]]? Angels, a cosmic conflict, and aasimar dead all in one room is hard to ignore.
