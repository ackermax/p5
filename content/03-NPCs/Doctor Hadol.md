# Doctor Hadol

> **Race / Class:** Unknown. A **doctor**, and an inventor
> **Role:** **Building technology to rival magic.** **Works for [[Cardon Malefic]].** In the dungeon he was **with a [[Cult of Cyric]] mage in a red hood**, who seems to have **hired him as a contractor of sorts**
> **Disposition toward party:** **Hostile.** He interrupted us mid-conversation with [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] and fought us
> **First met:** [[Session 73 - Var - 2026-10-05|Session 73]] in the **dungeon under the house on [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin's list]]**, [[Yartar]]

## What We Know

- **He's trying to build technology that rivals magic.**
- **He works for [[Cardon Malefic]]**, [[Var Borgal|Var]]'s old lord.
- **He was down there with a mage in a red hood.** The mage seems to have **hired him as a contractor of sorts** to help with whatever they're doing in that dungeon. **We think the mage is [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of Cyric]]** (see [[Reckoning With The Cult Of Cyric]]).
- **He walked in on us** just after we freed and healed [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] in the torture room, before Ilin could tell us much.
- **He escaped with a technological dimension door.** It was a device, not a spell. **We knocked out the red-hooded mage instead**, but got nothing out of him, and **he was killed when he tried to attack [[Var Borgal|Var]] during the dealmaking.** **So the one man who could tell us what Hadol was hired for is dead.**

## Session 75 — The Knight Was A Gift

**Papers off the corpse of the Great Mother** say **[[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] and "the doctor" — Hadol *(confirmed)* — gave the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] the anti-magic knight as a "gift"**, to help **expand the cult's reach.** **It's the thing guarding the prison block** in the dungeon. So the S73 picture — **Hadol as a hired contractor** — is only half of it: **Cardon's side is also *giving* the cult things.**

## Session 76 — Northeast Of The City

**[[Jarlaxle|Jarlaxle Baenre]] told [[Var Borgal|Var]] that Cardon and Hadol are northeast of [[Yartar]].** In what we think is [[Other Locations#Cardon's Hideout (working name)|Cardon's hideout]] we found **lamps run on some sort of technology** and **copper cylinders that look like they store power.** **Whether those are Hadol's work is not recorded, but it's what he builds.**

## ⭐ Session 77 — Four Hadols, All Flesh Blobs

- **The alarm brought an anti-magic knight, Hadol, and [[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]].** **We took out the knight and the doctor**, and **when we killed him, Hadol became a flesh-like blob**, and so did Cardon.
- **Later, upstairs: a single anti-magic knight and three more Hadols.** **We killed the knight and two of the Hadols; both turned into flesh blobs.**
- **We knocked the third one out**, and **were able to tell it was a flesh blob as well.** **It's the only one left alive, and we have it. What we do with it is undecided.**
- **Downstairs we'd found tubes of Cardons in various states of completion.** **No Hadols in tubes are recorded.**

> **So every Hadol we met here was a flesh blob, and there are several of him.** **We assume there must be an original Hadol somewhere, but we don't know where yet.** Which Hadol escaped us in S73 is not recorded. The **knocked-out one is our first chance to question one of these copies.**
