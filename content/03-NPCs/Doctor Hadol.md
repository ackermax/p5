# Doctor Hadol

> **Race / Class:** Unknown. A **doctor**, and an inventor
> **Role:** **Building technology to rival magic.** **Works for [[Cardon Malefic]].** In the dungeon he was **with a [[Cult of Cyric]] mage in a red hood**, who seems to have **hired him as a contractor of sorts**
> **Disposition toward party:** **Hostile.** He interrupted us mid-conversation with [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] and fought us
> **First met:** [[Session 73 - Var - 2026-10-05|Session 73]] in the **dungeon under the house on [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin's list]]**, [[Yartar]]

## What We Know

- **He's trying to build technology that rivals magic.**
- **He works for [[Cardon Malefic]]**, [[Var Borgal|Var]]'s old lord.
- **He was down there with a mage in a red hood.** The mage seems to have **hired him as a contractor of sorts** to help with whatever they're doing in that dungeon. **We think the mage is [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of Cyric]]** (see [[Reckoning With The Cult Of Cyric]]).
- **He walked in on us** just after we freed and healed [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] in the torture room, before Ilin could tell us much.
- **He escaped with a technological dimension door.** It was a device, not a spell. **We knocked out the red-hooded mage instead**, but got nothing out of him, and **he was killed when he tried to attack [[Var Borgal|Var]] during the dealmaking.** **So the one man who could tell us what Hadol was hired for is dead.**

## What They Want

- **Technology to rival magic.** Beyond that, unknown.
- Whatever the cult hired him to help with down here. **We don't know what that job is.**

## Relationships
