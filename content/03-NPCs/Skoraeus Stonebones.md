# Skoraeus Stonebones

> **Role:** Stone giant patron and spell-source for [[Gotrek Runehammer]]
> **Status (updated):** **Demigod, son of Amand. Not on this plane.**
> **Servants:** Those who represent his will are called **Danes**.

## Significance To Gotrek

[[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s runes are written in an **old stone giant dialect** referencing Skoraeus. **All stone giants know about him.** Gotrek's heritage — or whatever wrote those runes onto him — is tied directly to the stone giant tradition.

The **leader of the cleft where Gotrek was raised was regarded as a Dreamwalker.** Open question: was that leader a **Dane** — a servant of Skoraeus's will?

## Lore

Skoraeus divided the means by which stone giants connect to **aman**:
- **[[Stone Giant Factions|Rockspeakers]]** — speak directly into the stones (dwarves can do the same)
- **[[Stone Giant Factions|Dreamwalkers]]** — peer not into the earth but into the **dreams of the land**

This division split the stone giants into clans and triggered a war. Gotrek's runes carry the **Dreamwalker symbol**.
