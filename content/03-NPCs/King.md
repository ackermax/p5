# King

> **Type:** Golden Retriever
> **Disposition:** Loyal good boy
> **Status:** **With the party** (rescued)

## Backstory

King's **family was killed by "large two-legs."** He joined the party for a while early on, showing us where the **banshee** ([[Agatha]]) was, north of [[Other Locations|Conyberry]].

## The Selling And Rescue

A regrettable chapter:

1. The **Order of the Kraken** ended up with King and **sold him to the gambling den** in [[Waterdeep]] for 35 gp
2. [[The Waterdeep Wazoo Issue 496|The Wazoo]]'s "FOUND!" ad reported a dog named King at the **[[Bloody Fist Tavern]]** — that's how we picked up the trail
3. We went to the Bloody Fist and found the **Order of the Kraken** still there — they admitted they'd sold King to the gambling den **the day before we arrived**
4. We tracked him to the gambling den and **rescued him**

## Why He Matters

- Genuinely good boy
- Experienced tracker of supernatural targets ([[Agatha]])
- Living reminder for the party not to lose track of their dog
