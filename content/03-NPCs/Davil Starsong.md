# Davil Starsong

> **Race / Class:** Elf (flamboyant)
> **Role:** **Leader of the [[Black Network|Zhentarim]] in [[Waterdeep]]** *and* **head of the [[Doom Raiders]]**
> **Disposition toward party:** Unaware of us — we attended his meeting in disguise. Underworld power-broker, not an ally.
> **First met:** [[Session 59 - Var - 2026-06-08]] at the [[Council of the Underworld]] meeting (disguised, via [[Thomas]])

## Appearance

A **flamboyant elf**. Carried himself as the chair of the room — he ran the Council meeting.

## What We Know

- **Leads the [[Black Network|Zhentarim]] presence in [[Waterdeep]]** — the city's Zhent boss
- **Also the head / lead adventurer of the [[Doom Raiders]]** — the retired band the [[Harpers]] flagged as "not decent people"
- This single fact links **[[Skeemo Weirdbottle]]'s two affiliations** (former Doom Raider *and* suspected Zhent asset) under **one man** — Davil is plausibly Skeemo's old captain *and* current handler
- **Chaired the [[Council of the Underworld]] meeting** (S59), opening it himself
- Reported **"hindering activities"** disrupting underworld operations — explicitly mentioned **[[Other Locations|Nightstone]]** (which the party was involved in)
- Knows **[[Skeemo Weirdbottle]] was taken by the [[Harpers]]**; suspected a "third party" was involved (it was us) and questioned the other bosses about it
- **Put up a 10,000 gp bounty to rescue Skeemo** from the Harpers
- **Accepted [[Cardon Malefic]]'s offer** to escort him to [[Yartar]] — pledged **[[Black Network|Zhentarim]] men** for the job

## What He Wants

- Protect Zhentarim operations on the Sword Coast and root out who's "hindering" them (i.e., us, the Harpers)
- **Recover Skeemo** — a former Doom Raider and active Zhent supplier
- Profit from / leverage the new eastern "operation" everyone's flocking to — mercenaries and money pouring in

## Relationships

- Boss of the **[[Black Network|Zhentarim]]** in Waterdeep
- Leader of the **[[Doom Raiders]]**
- Almost certainly **[[Skeemo Weirdbottle]]'s** old captain / current handler
- Sits on the **[[Council of the Underworld]]** alongside [[Thomas]], [[Xanathars Guild|Xanathar]], and [[Jarlaxle]]
- Now **partnered with [[Cardon Malefic]]** on the Yartar escort

## Open Questions

- Does he know the [[Harpers]] specifically took Skeemo, or just "a third party"?
- Is he coordinating the Zhent escort to Yartar *because* of the [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] connection, or just for the gold?
- How tight is his grip on the surviving Doom Raiders — can he reactivate the band?
- He's a direct enemy of the [[Harpers]] — does that make Davil the closest thing to a face for the Zhent threat we've been tracking?
