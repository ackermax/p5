# Oghma

> **Role:** God of knowledge — patron of [[Other Locations|Candlekeep]]
> **Status:** Active deity

> **Spelling note:** Our source notes capture the name as **"Augma"** (phonetic — "OG-ma"). Same deity. Canonical spelling is **Oghma**.

The biggest religion at [[Other Locations|Candlekeep]]. Worth knowing for any research trip — [[Harrick Stonequill]] is a Candlekeep member, the **Aldith Tresendar / Talon** lore is housed there, and the [[Quest For The Spellbook|Bowgentle spellbook]] research path runs through these archives.
