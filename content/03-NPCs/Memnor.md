# Memnor

> **Title:** The Masked Lord
> **Role:** Cloud giant deity; mythological progenitor of the cloud giants
> **Alignment:** Evil (canonically)

> **Spelling note:** In our source notes, Memnor appears as both **"Memnar"** (referenced as the deity the Masked Ones follow) and **"Memnore"** (referenced as the first of the cloud giants). These are the same entity — different phonetic renderings. The vault uses **Memnor** as the canonical spelling.

## Lore

Member of the **Ordening pantheon** alongside [[Skoraeus Stonebones]] (stone giants), Stronmaus (storm giants), Surtur (fire giants), Thrym (frost giants), and Grolantor (hill giants).

- **The Masked Lord** — Memnor's worshippers are called the **Masked Ones**
- **The First Cloud Giant** — Memnor is the mythological progenitor of the cloud giants; the statue [[Mulara and Nimbolo]] hold of "the first of the cloud giants" is a statue of him
- **Pride, manipulation, mental prowess** — Memnor's portfolio leans toward deception and trickery; "masked" is doctrinal as much as visual

## Followers In Play

- **The Masked Ones** — evil-aligned cloud giants
- **[[Countess Sansuri]]** — almost certainly aligned here, given her likely role in the Nightstone attack
- [[Mulara and Nimbolo]] — holding a statue of Memnor, but their alignment is unclear; could be devotional, could be scholarly/cartographic

## Counterpart

Non-evil cloud giants in our notes follow **[[Stronmaus]]** — god of joy/sky/clouds/weather (canonically the storm giant god, but worshipped by non-evil cloud giants in our campaign).
