# Laeral Silverhand

> **Title:** **Open Lord of Waterdeep**

The Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]]. Top of the political food chain in the city.

Note: notes spell it "Laryl Silverhand" — Forgotten Realms canonical is **Laeral Silverhand**. Probably the same person.

## Audience With Her (Session 62)

The party met Laeral in person. What she shared:

- **Cult activity is widespread on the Sword Coast** — she named the **[[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]]**, the **[[Cult of the Dragon]]**, a **Cult of the Abyss**, and "some other cults." *(We asked specifically about the Cult of C and got **no further information** from her.)*
- **[[The Ordening]] "may have been more of a magic trick than an actual phenomenon to keep the giants at bay."** A major reframe of the campaign spine — see [[The Ordening]]. Treat as a lead, not settled fact.
- **[[Ilin Cotton]] helped against the cult and kept a residence in [[Waterdeep]], at the dockyard.** A new place to investigate in the city.
- For **[[Malkyn Serpentwind]]'s curse**, she pointed us to **[[Blackstaff Academy]]** to ask around for help.
- Context that clarified the **[[Stone of Golorr]]** / [[Commander Hawkwinter]] / [[Xanathars Guild|Xanathar]] situation.
