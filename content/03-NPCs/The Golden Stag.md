# The Golden Stag

> **What:** A **golden stag** encountered at **[[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]]** — the power whose **blessing the Elk tribe's spirits required** before they would give up their relic
> **Disposition toward party:** **Blessed us** — while saying plainly that it **found us wanting**
> **First met:** [[Session 70 - Var - 2026-08-31|Session 70]] at [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]]

## What We Know

- The **spirits of the [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Elk clan]]** told us we could not have the Flint Rock relic **without the Golden Stag's blessing**. It is the gate on that mound, and the spirits defer to it.
- We **did not have to hunt it down.** We made camp for the evening and **saw it passing by** — we approached it.
- **It questioned each of us in turn**, and the question was: **what have you done to restore nature's balance?** Everyone answered for themselves.
- **Its verdict: we were found wanting** — but **far enough along the journey** to be worth blessing, **because the breaking of [[The Ordening]] is itself a disruption of that balance.**
- **It blessed us.** The Elk spirits then gave up the relic — **the tip of a mithral spear** — **along with their own blessing.** See [[Gather Ulfgardian Relics]].
- **⭐ The blessing is symbolic.** It carries **no mechanical effect** — it is **a judgement rendered in our favour**, not a power granted. That does not make it small: **a power of nature looked at each of us and decided we were worth backing.**

> **What this establishes:** the Ordening is not only a giant problem or a cosmic one. **A power of nature treats the broken Ordening as a wound in the natural order** — which makes our whole spine ([[Find Hekaton]]) something nature-aligned powers may back.

## What It Wants

- **Nature's balance restored.** That is the only standard it applied to us, and it applied it to each of us individually.

## Open Questions

- **What is it?** A god, a spirit of the mound, the Elk tribe's patron, something older? The notes don't say and **we shouldn't assume.**
- **Does it show up again?** It was **passing by** — it moves. Would it answer at another mound?
- **Should we be reporting back to it?** If it graded us on restoring balance and we're now grading ourselves on relics and airship oil, there is a conversation coming.
- **"Wanting, but far enough along" is credit extended, not a debt settled.** It blessed us for the direction we're pointed in. **What happens when it checks again?**
