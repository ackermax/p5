# Oren

> **Race:** Halfling bard
> **Relation:** Cousin of **[[Ilin Cotton]]**
> **Disposition:** Friendly to the party

## How We Met

[[Anastas Glorybluff]] flagged him in [[Goldenfields]] — wanted an audience with the party. Jaak hoped Oren might be related to Ilin. **He is — they're cousins.**

## What He's Told Us

- "It's been a while" since he's seen Ilin
- Says Ilin was **a bit of a troublemaker**
- **Ilin laid low in [[Waterdeep]]** — something can be done there if you're influential enough — **games and money**
- **Ilin won a place in one of the games**
- About **[[Rulf Cloudfang]]** — "he sucked"

## Why He Matters

- **Best lead on [[Ilin Cotton]]'s whereabouts in [[Waterdeep]]**
- The "games" hint: maybe the **Gambling Den** ("gold pays, luck pays more") or the **Golden Goose** in [[Yartar]]?
- **Misty Beard tavern** is the specific lead — Oren or someone he knows pointed there
