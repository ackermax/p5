# Yamir

> **Profession:** Merchant out of **Calimshan**
> **Status:** **Betrayed by [[Lady Morin Daggerford]]** — current state unknown
> **Disposition:** Was friendly to the party

## What He Was

A Calimshan merchant looking to expand his trade circle. Done business with [[Lady Morin Daggerford]] and **Nobles from Baldur's Gate**. Was in [[Other Locations|Daggerford]] for a few days, staying at the **Northfurrows Inn** in [[Goldenfields]].

## The Betrayal

**Lady Daggerford betrayed Yamir.** What this looks like in practice — and what happened to Yamir — is unclear and an open thread.

## What He Promised

He was expecting to have info for us in a few days, before the betrayal. **What was the info?** That alone is worth tracking.

## Other Calimshan Connection

The notes mention **"Calimshan merchants who want to 'invest' in [Goldenfields] with the Abbot. Abbot thinks they're sketchy."** — separate from Yamir specifically, but worth flagging that Calimshan merchant activity on the Sword Coast is sus right now.

Also **Mistress Khalashite, from Khalim shum** is referenced — another Calimshani contact, context unclear.
