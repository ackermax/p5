# Oversword Vaelia Thann

> **Role:** **Oversword** — the officer who met the party at [[Yartar]]'s guard tower and ran them through the entry check
> **Disposition toward party:** **Professional.** Not hostile — she explained the check rather than enforcing it on us, and we were let through.
> **First met:** [[Session 70 - Var - 2026-08-31|Session 70]] at the **guard tower outside [[Yartar]]**

## What We Know

- **"Oversword" is her rank**, in [[Yartar]]'s guard. We don't know how senior it is or who she answers to — presumably up to the Waterbaron, **[[Nestra Ruthiol]]**.
- She **met us at the guard tower outside the city** and told us what the check was: a **magical ward** stops arrivals, and everyone is **searched for dangerous items in the guardhouse** before entering.
- **We passed the magic checks and were let into the city.** She did not confiscate anything the notes record — and we were carrying **[[Gather Ulfgardian Relics|five giant relics]]**, including a **100 lb giant's skull**.

## Open Questions

- **What is the ward actually screening for?** We passed carrying relics, a **[[Jaaks Celestial War|Sword of Heavens]]**, and a bag of magic items — so it is not a blanket "no magic" gate. **What would have failed?**
- **Why is it there?** A city that screens every arrival for dangerous items expects something to arrive. **Does the guard know about the [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]]?**
- **Is she a contact worth cultivating?** She is the first named authority in Yartar who has spoken to us and she was straight with us.
- **Do the guard's records now show we came in?** If [[Cardon Malefic]] or the cult has eyes in the tower, our arrival is on paper. **[[Var Borgal|Var]] carries a 25,000 gp bounty.**
