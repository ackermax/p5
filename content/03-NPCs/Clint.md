# Clint

> **Role:** Shady merchant — **or several of them.** **Also traded as "Lamin," vendor of exotic flora out of [[Yartar]]** *(same person — confirmed 2026-09-02)*
> **Disposition:** Trades with us. **Not an ally — and now, not neutral either.**

## ⚠️ Clint And "Lamin" Are The Same Man

**Confirmed:** the [[Yartar]] flora vendor our older notes call **"Lamin"** is **Clint under another name.** Everything the raw archive recorded about Lamin belongs here.

**What that means:** **the man who sold [[Sevembra]] the cursed seeds is the man we have been shopping with.** We bought gear off him in [[Everlund]] as recently as **S69**. See [[The Cursed Seeds]].

## What We Know

- A shady salesperson — **or a name several people use.** Only goes by Clint (or Lamin) and sells shady goods.
- **A "Clint" in Red Larch was [[Black Network|Zhentarim]].**
- **As "Lamin":** **vendor of exotic flora** working out of [[Yartar]]. **Wagon full of goods.**

### Appearance & Method *(recorded under the Lamin name)*

- Wears a **fedora and a coat**; **high charisma**; **a bit shady**
- Rides a **red-and-white wagon** marked with a **black dove symbol**
- **Known route:** came from **[[Goldenfields]]**, heading for **[[Everlund]]**, **passing through [[Other Locations|Beliard]]**
- **Known Beliard customers:** the **herbalist**, **some men at the Watchful Knight**, and **a farmer — the father of the stable boy Clinch**

## Dealings

- **Sold the cursed seeds to [[Sevembra]]** in [[Goldenfields]] — five of them, which would have **destroyed the region's crops**. See [[The Cursed Seeds]].
- **S69** — the party **bought gear from him** in [[Everlund]], on the same trip we collected from [[Krowen Valharrow]]. *(Purchases not itemized — [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] logged it as "bought some stuff.")*
