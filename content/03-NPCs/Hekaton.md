# Hekaton

> **Race:** Storm Giant
> **Role:** King of the Storm Giants, keeper of the [[The Ordening|Ordening]]
> **Status:** **Missing**

## Significance

Hekaton's leadership held the [[The Ordening|Ordening]] together — the divine hierarchy that kept the six giant kinds in their proper order. With him gone, the Ordening has shattered, and **this is the cause of the giant attacks** plaguing the Sword Coast.

## What We Know

- He vanished. No one (we've spoken to) knows where.
- His home is the **Maelstrom** — the storm giant capital, somewhere undersea.
- His conch of teleportation can't get us there; conches are bound to giants and won't work for us.
- Multiple parties are investigating: us, [[Harshnag]], possibly [[Klauth]] (who gave us an airship for this).

## The Goal

Find Hekaton and reinstate him as king, ending the giant wars. See [[Find Hekaton]].
