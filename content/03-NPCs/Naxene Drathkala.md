# Naxene Drathkala

> **Role:** Wizard who gave us the [[Goldenfields]] wards quest
> **Disposition:** Ally
> **Notable:** Likes [[Vajra Safahr]] when other archmages don't

## What She Gave Us

A **scroll of Dimension Door** (500 ft teleport, can bring someone with you, 4th-circle spell, DC 14 to use successfully) and the three **wards** to set in [[Goldenfields]].

## The Wards (Completed)

We placed wards in three locations:
1. **Harvest Home Abbey** — close to the Abbot's quarters
2. **Goldengulp's Brewery** — top floor where **[[Strog Thunderblade]]** meets with people
3. **The Gatehouse** — most difficult; required getting in unnoticed; best place was the gatehouse itself or **[[Hantanus Tarm]]'s** office

## Why It Mattered

Wards in [[Goldenfields]] suggest Naxene anticipated **an attack on Goldenfields**. With the [[The Cursed Seeds|cursed seeds]] operation also targeting Goldenfields, this is **the second confirmed prong** of a coordinated attack on the breadbasket.
