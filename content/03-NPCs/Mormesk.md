# Mormesk

> **Type:** Wraith (undead wizard)
> **Location:** [[Wave Echo Cave]]
> **Disposition:** Hostile

## What He Wants

The **[[The Forge of Spells|Forge of Spells]]**. He controls the undead in [[Wave Echo Cave]].

## Why He Matters

Anyone trying to reach the Forge of Spells goes through him. He's the gatekeeper to one of the most powerful magical-crafting sites on the Sword Coast.
