# Rulf Cloudfang

> **Race:** Half-elf
> **Age:** Late 40s (was 47 ~10 years ago)
> **Role:** Fiddler and de facto leader of [[The Traveling Wilmelons]]
> **Disposition:** **Hostile** — actively hunting [[Jaak Greye]]

## Profile

The real power in [[The Traveling Wilmelons]]. Cruel. Was the only member who **opposed taking [[Jaak Greye]] in** as a child; once Jaak was kept anyway, Rulf made sure he was treated as a roadie and kept under his thumb.

Has grown **more erratic and cruel** with time, per Jaak's account.

## Connection To The Cult

Rulf was the one who barged in (with the cult leader) and **cut off the conversation [[Ilin Cotton]] was trying to have with Jaak** — the conversation right before Ilin disappeared. **Strongly suggests Rulf is allied with [[Cult of Cyric]]**, or at least subordinate to its leader.

### Session 57 — Malkyn's Account

About **six months ago**, Rulf visited [[Tavern Misty Beard]] looking for **[[Ilin Cotton]]**:

- **In a black cloak. Shady.**
- **Searching for someone** (Ilin)
- **Concerned that someone was looking at him** — i.e., he believes he's being hunted in turn
- Was **"eager to talk to [[Malkyn Serpentwind|Malkyn]]"** — exact ask unclear, but Malkyn knew about the cult's **abduction of children**
- Told Malkyn he **"had no choice"** and **"didn't know what was at stake"**

### Reframe — Rulf May Be A Puppet

[[Malkyn Serpentwind|Malkyn]]'s read is that **Rulf is a puppet** — not the architect, not even a willing participant in the worst of the cult's work. The "had no choice" language matches.

This doesn't make him less dangerous, and doesn't change that he beats people and abducts children. But it changes the **interrogation** calculus: there's someone (or something) above him with leverage. **That** is the higher-value capture.

## Why Hunt Jaak Now

- Jaak left the troupe humiliated by the heist
- Helping [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] steal her inheritance was a direct attack on the cult, Rulf's patron
- Jaak knows too much about Rulf and the troupe
