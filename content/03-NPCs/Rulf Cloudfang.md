# Rulf Cloudfang

> **Race:** Half-elf
> **Age:** Late 40s (was 47 ~10 years ago)
> **Role:** ⭐ **Current leader of the [[Cult of Cyric]]** *(S74)*. Fiddler and de facto leader of [[The Traveling Wilmelons]]
> **Disposition:** **Hostile** — actively hunting [[Jaak Greye]]. **Killed [[Ilin Cotton]] (S74).**
> **Status (S74):** **Fought at the top of the [[Yartar]] dungeon.** We thought we killed him; **his body dissipated as if he was never there**, and **he vanished into the ether.** **Assume alive.**
> *Var's S74 notes spell him "Rolf" — a guess; **Rulf** is correct (confirmed 2026-10-19). Kept as an alias so Var's spelling still resolves.*

## Profile

The real power in [[The Traveling Wilmelons]]. Cruel. Was the only member who **opposed taking [[Jaak Greye]] in** as a child; once Jaak was kept anyway, Rulf made sure he was treated as a roadie and kept under his thumb.

Has grown **more erratic and cruel** with time, per Jaak's account.

## ⭐ Session 74 — The Leader Of The Cult

At the **top of the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] dungeon** under [[Yartar]] we ran into **a hooded figure**: **Rulf**, leader of [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s old troupe — and **the current leader of the Cult of Cyric.**

What we learned there:
- **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] was being groomed to be the cult's next leader.**
- **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] was only good for his blood** — to **finish whatever ritual they're working towards.**
- **The ritual's goal is bringing [[Cyric]] back.**

**We think we killed him.** But when Jaak went to check the body, **it dissipated as if he was never there at all** — and **Rulf stabbed [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] and killed him** as he disappeared into the ether.

> **So:** the man who scouted Jaak, crippled [[Malkyn Serpentwind|Malkyn]], and hunted Ilin for years **runs the cult now**, and he's **killed Ilin**. **Whether we fought the real Rulf, a double, or an illusion is unknown** — the vanishing body says *something* was off.

## Connection To The Cult

Rulf was the one who barged in (with the cult leader) and **cut off the conversation [[Ilin Cotton]] was trying to have with Jaak** — the conversation right before Ilin disappeared. **Strongly suggests Rulf is allied with [[Cult of Cyric]]**, or at least subordinate to its leader.

### Session 57 — Malkyn's Account

About **six months ago**, Rulf visited [[Tavern Misty Beard]] looking for **[[Ilin Cotton]]**:

- **In a black cloak. Shady.**
- **Searching for someone** (Ilin)
- **Concerned that someone was looking at him** — i.e., he believes he's being hunted in turn
- Was **"eager to talk to [[Malkyn Serpentwind|Malkyn]]"** — exact ask unclear, but Malkyn knew about the cult's **abduction of children**
- Told Malkyn he **"had no choice"** and **"didn't know what was at stake"**

### Reframe — Rulf May Be A Puppet *(S57; challenged S74)*

[[Malkyn Serpentwind|Malkyn]]'s read is that **Rulf is a puppet** — not the architect, not even a willing participant in the worst of the cult's work. The "had no choice" language matches.

This doesn't make him less dangerous, and doesn't change that he beats people and abducts children. But it changes the **interrogation** calculus: there's someone (or something) above him with leverage. **That** is the higher-value capture.

> **S74 cuts against this:** Rulf is now **the cult's current leader.** Either he rose since Malkyn saw him, or the "puppet" was an act. **The god the cult wants to bring back** is, at minimum, still above him.

## Why Hunt Jaak Now

- Jaak left the troupe humiliated by the heist
- Helping [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] steal her inheritance was a direct attack on the cult, Rulf's patron
- Jaak knows too much about Rulf and the troupe
