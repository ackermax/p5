# Doom of the Desert

> **Type:** **Blue Dragon** (lightning-powered)
> **Status:** Active — recently stirred from her lair
> **Disposition:** Hostile to [[Zephyros]] (per [[Krowen Valharrow]])

## What [[Krowen Valharrow]]'s Letter Says

> "The **Doom of the Desert** is among the mightiest of her kind still active in Faerûn — a being of ruin and pride beyond reason. Should she find you unprepared, even your command of the winds may not spare you from her fury."

- She is **female**
- One of the **mightiest dragons still active in Faerûn**
- A "being of ruin and pride beyond reason"
- **Setting her gaze on [[Zephyros]]**
- Her **wrathful pursuit** — Valharrow is investigating *why*

## Confirmed

- **She attacked [[Zephyros]]** — that's where we learn she's "the lightning-powered dragon who attacked Zephyros"
