## About Tressyms (General Reference)

Tressyms are small winged cats — fey-touched, intelligent enough to behave like a familiar but technically not one. They tend to be:

- **Good-natured and curious**
- **Innately able to detect invisibility and magic** in their immediate area
- **Speak with the same range of vocalizations as cats** (no Common, but body language is expressive)

## Why Matey Matters
