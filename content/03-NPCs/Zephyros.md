# Zephyros

> **Title:** **Keeper of the Cloud Spire**
> **Race:** Cloud Giant (wizard)
> **Disposition:** Friendly — isolationist but well-meaning

## Who He Is

A friendly wizard cloud giant. **Lives in a tower that drifts among the northern skies.** Isolationist — spends his time on "observations of clouds and omens" rather than the politics of [[The Ordening|the Ordening]].

## His Current Crisis

He is being pulled in three directions by powerful correspondents:

### 1. [[Countess Sansuri]] — Pressuring Him To Join
Sansuri has been summoning him repeatedly. **He has been ignoring her.** Her most recent letter is a hard ultimatum — she's on the verge of grasping "power beyond our reach" and wants his artifacts and wisdom. See [[Countess Sansuri]] for the full text.

### 2. [[Krowen Valharrow]] — Warning Him To Hide
The Archmage of Silverymoon has written to warn Zephyros that **[[Doom of the Desert|the Doom of the Desert]] has stirred from her lair and may be set on him**. Valharrow urges him to move his tower and conceal his course.

### 3. His Own Agent — Tracking [[Harshnag]]
Zephyros has agents searching for [[Harshnag of Force Grey]] for unknown reasons. The agent's report confirms Harshnag was sighted near [[Goldenfields]] no more than a tenday past, aiding merchants beset by ogres and consulting priests of [[Chauntea|Chauntea]] before vanishing into the northern woods.

## Why He Matters To Us

- He has **artifacts and wisdom** that [[Countess Sansuri]] thinks she needs — possibly tied to fixing [[The Ordening]]
- He is **hunting Harshnag for unstated reasons** — might be ally-finding, might be something darker
- He is a **scholar of clouds and omens** — could be a Sage we consult on cosmology, the [[The Second Sundering]], divine portents
- A potential ally if approached well; a potential target of Sansuri if not

## Open Questions

- Will he heed Valharrow's warning?
- Will he join Sansuri, refuse her, or stall?
- **Why is he tracking Harshnag?**
- Can the party reach him before Sansuri does?
