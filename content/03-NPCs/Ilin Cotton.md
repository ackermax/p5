# Ilin Cotton

> **Race:** Halfling
> **Role / Instrument:** Keytarist — "the only keytar player anyone had ever seen"
> **Affiliation:** **[[Harpers]]** (confirmed session 56)
> **Status:** **Alive and active as of a few weeks ago** — was at [[Tavern Misty Beard]] looking for [[Rulf Cloudfang]] and [[Malkyn Serpentwind]]; has since **departed for [[Other Locations|Yartar]]** to chase a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] lead (per Malkyn, session 57)
> **Disposition:** Ally

## Major Revelation (Session 56)

**[[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] is a [[Harpers|Harper]]** — and was **investigating the [[Cult of Cyric]]** when he vanished.

- His last Harper report mentioned **"something dangerous"**
- He was **discovered** by the cult shortly after
- **Contact was lost** following the discovery
- He was last seen in the **taverns of Waterdeep**, specifically **[[Tavern Misty Beard]]**

This recasts everything we thought we knew about Ilin's disappearance — and about why [[Jaak Greye]] mattered to the cult.

## Update (Session 57 — Misty Beard Lead Closed)

We found his trail at the **[[Tavern Misty Beard]]**, talking to [[Malkyn Serpentwind]] (now the tavern's boss).

- **Ilin was here a few weeks ago.** He was **looking for [[Rulf Cloudfang]]** — to deal with him over the cult.
- He is **alive** and **not in cult captivity** as of that visit.
- He left Waterdeep heading **to [[Other Locations|Yartar]]** — there is a cult lead there.
- He is **looking for [[Harpers]]** (so the discovery/contact-lost period did happen, but he is now trying to re-establish contact, not in hiding from us).
- He frames the cult's current work as **"end of the world stuff."**

So Ilin is no longer missing — he's *ahead of us*, chasing the same enemy.

## Significance To Jaak

The closest thing [[Jaak Greye]] ever had to family. When the [[The Traveling Wilmelons|Wilmelons]] found Jaak on the roadside at 8, Ilin **insisted on taking him in** — over [[Rulf Cloudfang]]'s objection. He secretly taught Jaak the keytar.

## The Disappearance (Now Reframed)

One evening, while the troupe was performing for [[Cult of Cyric]]'s leader, Ilin **pulled Jaak aside in hushed tones**. There was **something urgent** he needed to tell him.

Before he could say it, **[[Rulf Cloudfang]] and the cult leader barged in** and cut the conversation short.

That night, when it was time to perform, **Ilin was nowhere to be found**. He has not been seen since *by the troupe*.

**With the new Harper context:** Ilin was likely already a Harper *during his Wilmelons years*, working undercover with the troupe to get close to the cult. When he was discovered, he extracted himself — or was extracted — and resurfaced in Waterdeep to continue the investigation.

## What This Implies

- **Ilin was protecting Jaak from the cult, not from Rulf personally**
- The "something urgent" he was about to tell Jaak may have been:
  - That Jaak was being scouted as a sacrifice target
  - That Ilin himself was a Harper
  - Both
- The Wilmelons themselves may have been a **Harper cover operation** that went bad — or Ilin was solo, with [[Rulf Cloudfang|Rulf]] only realizing later
