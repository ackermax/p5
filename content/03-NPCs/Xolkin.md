# Xolkin

> **Role:** Card player in [[Yartar]] who put up a **ticket to the Golden Goose** as the stake in a poker game — **and lost it to us**
> **Disposition toward party:** **Helpful, on a trade basis.** He wanted information and paid for it in leads.
> **First met:** [[Session 71 - Var - 2026-09-14|Session 71]] at a tavern in [[Yartar]]

## Appearance

- **A flying snake around his neck.** That's how we knew him before we had his name.

## What We Know

- He was **offering a ticket to the Golden Goose to whoever beat him at cards.** **We won the poker game** — the ticket is ours. See [[Yartar#The Golden Goose|the Golden Goose]].
- **He is looking for information on "Lamin"** — the name [[Clint]] trades under. **We told him where we last saw him.**
- In return he **sent us to Through and Through Investigators** to help with [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s search for **[[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin Cotton]]** — the lead that got us to **[[Garambitio]]**.
- **He told us the [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]] want to talk to us about [[Goldenfields]] and what we were up to there.**

## What They Want

- **[[Clint]] / "Lamin."** Why, he didn't say.

## Relationships

- Hunting [[Clint]]
- Knows what the [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]] want from us

## Open Questions

- **Why is he looking for Clint?** Clint is the man who sold [[Sevembra]] the [[The Cursed Seeds|cursed seeds]] — is Xolkin after him for the same reason we should be, or for something else?
- **How does he know what the Red Mages want?** Passing on a rumour, or closer to them than that?
- **Why stake a Golden Goose ticket on a card game?** The invitations are meant to be hard to come by.
