# Mystra

> **Role:** Goddess of magic
> **Status:** **Killed by [[Cyric]]** — per [[Althariel]]'s account
> **What we were told:** [[Session 68 - Jaak - 2026-08-17|Session 68]] at [[Aasimar Temple|the Aasimar Temple]]

> [!info] Spelling
> [[Session 68 - Jaak - 2026-08-17|Jaak's]] notes write **"Mystral."** The vault spells it **Mystra**.

## What We Know

The **entire** record is what [[Althariel]] told us about [[Cyric]]'s greatest sin:

- **[[Cyric]] killed her**, and in doing so **almost destroyed the entire world.**
- When she died, **all magic ceased to exist.**

That is all. We do not know when this happened, how magic came back, or whether she did.
