# Mystra

> **Role:** Goddess of magic
> **Status:** **Killed by [[Cyric]]** — per [[Althariel]]'s account
> **What we were told:** [[Session 68 - Jaak - 2026-08-17|Session 68]] at [[Aasimar Temple|the Aasimar Temple]]

> [!info] Spelling
> [[Session 68 - Jaak - 2026-08-17|Jaak's]] notes write **"Mystral"**; [[Session 75 - Var - 2026-10-19|Var's S75]] notes write **"Mystril."** The vault spells it **Mystra**.

## What We Know

The **entire** record is what [[Althariel]] told us about [[Cyric]]'s greatest sin:

- **[[Cyric]] killed her**, and in doing so **almost destroyed the entire world.**
- When she died, **all magic ceased to exist.**

That is all. We do not know when this happened, how magic came back, or whether she did.

## Session 75 — "The God Behind The Weave"

- [[Var Borgal|Var]]'s notes call her **the god behind the Weave.**
- **Papers off the cult's "mother"** say **[[Cardon Malefic]] is researching how [[Cyric]] became a god.** Var asks: **is Cardon following Cyric's footsteps — and does he need to kill Mystra to do it?** *(A question, not a finding.)*
- **The second time we've heard of magic leaving the world:** **Karsus** of [[The Netherese|the Netherese]] tried to become **god of magic** with ***Karsus' Avatar***; **it failed, and magic left the world for a time.**

## Open Questions
