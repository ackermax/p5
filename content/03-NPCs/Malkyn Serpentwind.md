# Malkyn Serpentwind

> **Race:** Human
> **Instrument:** Drummer
> **Affiliation:** Former [[The Traveling Wilmelons|Wilmelon]] (Jaak's troupe-era bandmate)
> **Current Role:** **Boss of [[Tavern Misty Beard]]** — son of the owner
> **Disposition:** Reluctant ally — abused by Rulf, weak-willed but not cruel

## Current Status (Session 57)

- **Living in [[Waterdeep]]**, running [[Tavern Misty Beard]] — has been associated with the tavern his whole life, returned a year or two ago
- **In a wheelchair — cannot use his legs.** Also **coughing**.
- **The doctors claim it's trauma.** Jaak's read: **he may be lying** about the cause.
- Was beaten up by **[[Rulf Cloudfang]]** when Rulf returned from [[Other Locations|Neverwinter]] in a bad mood — the leg injury likely traces here, but the "trauma" framing is the cover story he's giving in Waterdeep
- Looked **terrible** when Jaak met him

## What He Told Us (Session 57)

- **[[Ilin Cotton]] was here a few weeks ago** — has since left for [[Yartar]] to chase a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] lead
- **[[Rulf Cloudfang]] stopped by about half a year ago** — in a black cloak, shady, searching for someone, **concerned that someone was watching him**
- Rulf was looking for **Ilin**; Ilin was at the Misty Beard looking for **Rulf** — they kept missing each other
- **Knows about the abduction of children.** Rulf told him he **"had no choice"** and **"didn't know what was at stake."** Malkyn's read: **Rulf is a puppet.**
- Ilin is hunting Rulf to deal with the cult's "end of the world stuff"
- Ilin is also looking for [[Harpers]]

## What He May Still Know

Like [[Anastas Glorybluff]], Malkyn was inside the troupe during Rulf's worst years. He likely knows more about:
- Rulf's [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] dealings
- Where Rulf may have hidden things
- What Rulf was looking for in Neverwinter
- Whether the "trauma" cover story is hiding something specific (a deal? a threat?)
