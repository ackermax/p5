# Sister Garaele

> **Role:** Attendant at the **Shrine of Luck** in [[Phandalin]] (town center, dedicated to **Lady Tymora**)
> **Affiliation:** **[[Harpers]]** — confirmed member
> **Disposition:** Ally

Garaele is openly a Harper. She told us the [[Other Locations|Order of the Gilded Eye]] "answers truthfully" — a useful piece of vouching.

She gave us the **[[Quest For The Spellbook|spellbook quest]]** that led us to [[Agatha]] the banshee.
