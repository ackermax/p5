# Nestra Ruthiol

> **Role:** **The Waterbaron of [[Yartar]]** — the city's ruler
> **Disposition toward party:** **Unknown — we have not met her.** Her city let us in after a search.
> **First met:** **Not met.** Named to us on arrival at [[Yartar]], [[Session 70 - Var - 2026-08-31|Session 70]]

## What We Know

- **She is the Waterbaron of [[Yartar]]** — the title the city's authority runs through. That is the whole of what the notes record.
- Learned on arrival (S70), while the party was being processed at the **guard tower outside the city** by **[[Oversword Vaelia Thann]]**.
- The **magical ward and the mandatory search for dangerous items** at the gate are her city's policy, whoever ordered them.

## Why She Matters

**[[Yartar]] is the convergence point** — [[Ilin Cotton]], the [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]]'s "something big," and [[Cardon Malefic]]'s meeting with that cult all land here. The person who rules the city is either **the party's best lever or the hardest obstacle**, and we walked in without knowing which.

Note also that **Yartar is a [[Harpers]] stronghold** and **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] are [[Harpers|Bright Candles]]** — there may be a Harper-side introduction available that doesn't go through the guard tower.

- **Where does she stand with the [[Harpers]]** — Yartar is their stronghold, and she rules it.
