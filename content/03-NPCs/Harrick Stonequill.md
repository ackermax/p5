# Harrick Stonequill

> **Title:** Giant-Lore Specialist, member of [[Candlekeep]]
> **Currently:** Went to [[Neverwinter]], may be back
> **Disposition:** Cooperative

## Significance To Gotrek

When Harrick touches [[Gotrek Runehammer]], his **runes glow and flux with power**. This is a real datapoint about Gotrek's heritage — Harrick may be the closest thing to a translator we have.

## The Runes (What We Know So Far)

- Written in an **old [[Stone Giant Factions|stone giant dialect]]**.
- Reference [[Skoraeus Stonebones]].
- Contain the **symbol of the [[Stone Giant Factions|Dreamwalkers]]**.
- The leader of the cleft where [[Gotrek Runehammer]] was raised was regarded as a dreamwalker.
- Gotrek's brother was searching for **Thane Kayalithica** — a powerful rockspeaker with revelations.
