# Thane Kayalithica

> **Race:** Stone Giant
> **Title:** **Thane**
> **Faction:** **Stoneshapers** (a.k.a. [[Stone Giant Factions|Rockspeakers]]) — speaks directly into the stones
> **Disposition:** **Hostile** — pursuing a plan that endangers all mortals
> **Status:** Active and operating

## What She's Doing

Per [[Eldrin]]'s report to [[Gotrek Runehammer]] in the dreamrealm:

- She is **trying to unite both sides** of the stone giant schism — Stoneshapers (Rockspeakers) and **Dreamweavers** ([[Stone Giant Factions|Dreamwalkers]]) — under her command
- Her chosen method: **bring the Dreamweavers to heel** rather than reconcile
- She is **doing something vile** — described as such by Eldrin
- **She is trying to destroy the dream world** (the dreamrealm itself)
- If she succeeds:
  - **No mortal will be able to dream**
  - All Dreamweavers could be killed in the process
- She is the one [[Eldrin]] was hunting; she remains [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s brother's quarry

## What She Is

- A **powerful Stoneshaper / Rockspeaker** — sees things others can't
- Has an **unusual connection to the stone** — perceives things "much different" from other stone giants
- Operates from the **Stoneshape clan**'s base — likely the same region the [[Cradle of the Stonegiants]] is in, or near it

## Why She Matters Now

- **Cosmic stakes:** if dreaming is destroyed, every mortal on Toril is affected. This is plot-critical at a Hekaton-level magnitude.
- **Personal stakes:** she is the target of [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s brother's hunt
- **Tactical:** Gotrek needs to **return to the [[Cradle of the Stonegiants|Cradle]]** to act against her
