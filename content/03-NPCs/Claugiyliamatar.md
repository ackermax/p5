# Claugiyliamatar

> **Type:** **Elder green dragon**
> **Location:** [[Other Locations#Kryptgarden Forest|Kryptgarden Forest]]
> **Disposition:** Wary / territorial — not (yet) an enemy
> **First met:** Session 63

## Appearance

A large, elder green dragon. Claims the [[Other Locations#Kryptgarden Forest|Kryptgarden Forest]] as **her realm**.

## What We Know (Session 63)

- Forced down near the **Kryptgarden Forest** by a storm while flying the [[Ignitamis]] to [[Phandalin]], the party **encountered Claugiyliamatar**, who **threatened them for entering her territory**.
- We **talked her down** by explaining **where we got our ship** — i.e. the airship's patron ([[Klauth]], the ancient red dragon). We were then allowed to continue on to [[Phandalin]].
- No hostilities; the party left on uneasy but non-violent terms.
