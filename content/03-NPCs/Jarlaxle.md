# Jarlaxle

> **Race / Class:** Drow
> **Role:** Leader of his own **brigade**; underworld free agent
> **Disposition toward party:** Neutral — **[[Var Borgal|Var]] owes him a like-for-like favor (S76).** Laid-back, hard to read.
> **First met:** [[Session 59 - Var - 2026-06-08]] at the [[Council of the Underworld]] meeting — though [[Var Borgal|Var]] recognized him as **"the pirate from the gambling den"** we'd seen before

## Appearance

A **drow dressed as an admiral / pirate**. **Very laid back** — bored, almost theatrical about it.

## What We Know

- **Commands a brigade** of his own — mercenary muscle he's looking to put to use
- Sits on the **[[Council of the Underworld]]**, the fourth of the bosses to arrive (after [[Thomas]], [[Davil Starsong]], and [[Xanathars Guild|Xanathar]])
- **Bored** — came to the meeting "looking for someone interesting to work with," someone who "could use his brigade." **[[Thomas]] offered to assist** his search.
- **In town on a search of his own** (target unstated)
- **Accepted [[Cardon Malefic]]'s offer** to escort him to [[Yartar]] — but is **afraid of Yartar because of krakens(?)**
- **Full name: Jarlaxle Baenre** *(as [[Var Borgal|Var]] logged it, S75)*
- **S75: [[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] is in [[Yartar]] "with mercenaries of Jarlaxle Baenre"** — per the papers of the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]]'s Great Mother. **He took the escort job.**
- **S76: we ran into him at the docks of [[Yartar]].** **[[Var Borgal|Var]] agreed to a like-for-like favor in exchange for the location of [[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] and [[Doctor Hadol]]: northeast of Yartar.** **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] logged speaking to him too.**
- Var places him as **the pirate seen earlier at the gambling den** (the animal-fights den in [[Waterdeep]]) — so he's been moving in our orbit for a while

## What He Wants

- Something **interesting** to spend his brigade on — he's restless
- Whatever he's privately searching Waterdeep for

## Relationships

- Sits on the **[[Council of the Underworld]]** with [[Thomas]], [[Davil Starsong]], [[Xanathars Guild|Xanathar]]
