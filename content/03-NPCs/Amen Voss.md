# Amen Voss

> **Race:** **Gnome** (whitish-purple hair)
> **Role:** **Curator of magical items** — runs a magic-item shop in [[Waterdeep]]; dispensed the [[The Waterdeep Ultimate Brawl|tournament]] rewards
> **Disposition:** Neutral merchant — cordial, but plays by strict rules
> **First met:** [[Session 61 - Var - 2026-06-22|Session 61]] in [[Waterdeep]]

## Appearance

Gnome with distinctive **whitish-purple hair**.

## What We Know

- He is the **curator of magical items** behind the [[The Waterdeep Ultimate Brawl]]'s prize table. He handed out our rewards from his **shop**, giving us an **amethyst coin** and instructions to an alley where we flipped a coin three times to receive our items.
- He **invited us to return and shop** before we leave [[Waterdeep]] — see Open Threads.
- He previously **sold "Kiki the abomination" to [[Barnaby Baxter]]**. **Kiki is a (mostly friendly) tentacled monster** that Barnaby kept in the circus as an attraction — guests try to stay upright while it tries to topple them over.

## His Three Rules

1. **No refunds**
2. **No haggling**
3. **No deceiving**

## Relationships

- Sold **Kiki the abomination** to [[Barnaby Baxter]]
- Connected to the [[Other Locations|Order of the Gauntlet]], who ran the [[The Waterdeep Ultimate Brawl]]
