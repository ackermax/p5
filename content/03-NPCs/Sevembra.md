## What We Know

- A **druid from [[Goldenfields]]**.
- She was carrying **5 cursed seeds** and was about to plant them outside the gate. They would have **seemed to work at first, then killed every crop** — see [[The Cursed Seeds]].

Fedora and coat, high charisma, **red-and-white wagon with a black dove symbol**. Full profile on [[Clint]]; the thread runs on [[The Cursed Seeds]].
