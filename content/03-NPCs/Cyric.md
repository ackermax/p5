# Cyric

> **Role:** God of **lies and greed**; a **trickster**. The god the [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] serves.
> **Status:** Active
> **What we were told:** [[Althariel]], at [[Aasimar Temple|the Aasimar Temple]] — [[Session 68 - Jaak - 2026-08-17|Session 68]]

## Althariel's Account *(S68)*

The first time anyone has laid out for us **who Cyric actually is**:

- **He is the most dangerous god.**
- **He was a human** who **ascended to godhood** — which is **rare**.
- His domain is **lies and greed**; he was **a trickster**.
- **His biggest sin: he almost destroyed the entire world by killing [[Mystra]]** — and **all magic ceased to exist.**

> *Jaak's notes render the name of the slain goddess "Mystral"; the vault spells it **[[Mystra]]**.*

## Why This Matters To Us

- **A mortal human ascended to godhood.** That is the same thing **[[Jaaks Celestial War|Jaak's kidnapper]]** is trying to do — and Cyric is the proof it can be done.
- **Cyric's cult is the cult that raised [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]** and hunted [[Jaak Greye]] for his blood — see [[Cult of Cyric]] and [[Reckoning With The Cult Of Cyric]].
- Althariel named Cyric in the **same breath as [[Bhaal]]**, who *"tried to use blood of an ancient person to inhabit as a vessel."* Both stories are about **gods and would-be gods reaching for mortal bodies and mortal bloodlines** — see [[Blood of the Ancients]].
- If the [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] is **"risen from the ashes"** and massing at [[Yartar]], it's worth knowing their god has **already tried to unmake the world once.**

## Open Questions

- **Is the cult trying to do for someone else what Cyric did for himself** — walk a mortal up into godhood?
- Was **killing Mystra** a one-off, or the template for what "the age of mortals" ([[The Second Sundering]]) makes possible again?
- Does Cyric know about the **ancient bloodlines**? Does he want [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] for the same reason [[Bhaal]] wanted his vessel?
