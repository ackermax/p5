# Agatha

> **Type:** **Banshee**; once an Eladrin
> **Location:** North of [[Other Locations|Conyberry]] — haunts the area where she died
> **Disposition:** Arrogant, but talks if approached correctly

## Backstory

Originally an **Eladrin** (fey-wilds ancestor of elves, from a place of chaotic magic and beauty; Eladrin live near [[Other Locations|Neverwinter]] and have good relations with other races). Was once an elf and prideful. **Killed near Conyberry; now haunts the area as a banshee.**

[[Other Locations|Daran Edermath]] knows of her.

## The Spellbook Trade

We came to her for the **spellbook of [[Bowgentle]]** — most powerful spellcaster in legend. We brought her an **ornate comb made of silver and jewels** as a gift, as instructed.

**She traded the spellbook to [[Tsernoth]] from the city of [[Other Locations|Iriaebor]].**

This is the lead for the [[Quest For The Spellbook]].
