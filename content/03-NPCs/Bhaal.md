# Bhaal

> **Role:** God of murder
> **Status:** Mostly dead — his cult was defeated in [[Other Locations|Baldur's Gate]] by **[[Jaheira]]** and the [[Harpers]]

A dead-or-dormant deity. His cult was a major threat in Baldur's Gate; that fight is over but cultists of dead gods have a way of resurfacing during the [[The Second Sundering|age of mortals]], when the rules around divinity are loose.

## ⭐ He Tried To Take A Vessel *(new, S68)*

**[[Althariel]] told us Bhaal "tried to use blood of an ancient person to inhabit as a vessel."**

That makes him **precedent, not history.** A god reaching for a mortal body with the **[[Blood of the Ancients|blood of the ancients]]** is exactly the shape of the thing now circling **[[Jaak Greye]]** and **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]** — both of whom carry that blood, and one of whom has **already been kidnapped by someone who wanted it for ascension** ([[Jaaks Celestial War]]).

**Unknown:** whether he succeeded, who the vessel was, or when. **Ask.**

> Reclassified from "closed" to **worth pulling on** — his method is the method being used on our party.
