# Bhaal

> **Role:** God of murder
> **Status:** Mostly dead — his cult was defeated in [[Other Locations|Baldur's Gate]] by **[[Jaheira]]** and the [[Harpers]]

A dead-or-dormant deity. His cult was a major threat in Baldur's Gate; that fight is over but cultists of dead gods have a way of resurfacing during the [[The Second Sundering|age of mortals]], when the rules around divinity are loose.

Worth tracking only if his iconography appears anywhere — otherwise, this is closed.
