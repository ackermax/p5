# Stronmaus

> **Role:** Storm giant god — sky, clouds, weather, joy
> **In our campaign:** Worshipped by **non-evil cloud giants** as their counterpart to [[Memnor]]'s evil "Masked Ones"

> **Spelling note:** In our source notes Stronmaus appears as **"Stromos"** — phonetic capture at the table. Same deity. The vault uses **Stronmaus** as canonical.

## Lore

Member of the **Ordening pantheon** alongside [[Skoraeus Stonebones]] (stone giants), [[Memnor]] (cloud giants), Surtur (fire giants), Thrym (frost giants), and Grolantor (hill giants).

In canon, Stronmaus is **the storm giant god** — second only to Annam in the giant pantheon, very close to [[Hekaton]]'s line. His portfolio is **joy, weather, the sky, the seas** — a high-spirited deity whose worship looks more like celebration than ritual.

## In Our Campaign

The non-evil cloud giants follow Stronmaus rather than [[Memnor]]'s "Masked Ones." This is a notable departure from strict canonical doctrine — but makes thematic sense:

- Cloud giants are rank 2 in [[The Ordening]], directly below storm giants
- With [[Hekaton]] missing and the Ordening collapsing, cloud giants looking *up* the hierarchy for spiritual cover (rather than down to their own god Memnor) is exactly what fractured giants would do
- The split mirrors the [[Stone Giant Factions]] schism in the stone giant tradition

## Why It Matters

- **Cloud giants who follow Stronmaus are presumed friendly** — not part of the [[Countess Sansuri|Masked Ones]] faction
- [[Mulara and Nimbolo]] could be Stronmaus-leaning despite holding a Memnor statue (scholarly interest vs. worship)
- Anyone the party meets who venerates joy/weather/sky in a cloud-giant context probably falls here
