# Yartar

> **Type:** City
> **Region:** Sword Coast North
> **Ruler:** **[[Nestra Ruthiol]]**, the **Waterbaron** *(named S70; not yet met)*
> **Significance:** **[[Harpers]] stronghold**; **[[Ilin Cotton]]'s current destination** chasing a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] lead (S57)
> **Status:** ⭐ **We are here.** Arrived and admitted [[Session 70 - Var - 2026-08-31|Session 70]]; **found [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]]'s inn** [[Session 71 - Var - 2026-09-14|Session 71]]; ⭐ **found Ilin himself, in a [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] torture room under a house**, [[Session 73 - Var - 2026-10-05|Session 73]].

## Why It Matters Now

**Yartar is becoming the convergence point for half the campaign's live threads.** As of [[Session 59 - Var - 2026-06-08|S59]], underworld intel at the [[Council of the Underworld]] revealed the **[[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] has "risen from the ashes," is buying poisons for "something big," and is massing on Yartar** — and **[[Cardon Malefic]] is travelling here in ~3 days to meet the cult**, escorted by [[Black Network|Zhentarim]] and [[Jarlaxle]]. That stacks on the S57 lead:

Per [[Malkyn Serpentwind]] at the [[Tavern Misty Beard]] (S57), **[[Ilin Cotton]] is ahead of us, headed here** on a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] "end of the world" lead. Ilin is also still hunting [[Rulf Cloudfang]] — if we reach Yartar we expect to either meet Ilin or pick up his trail toward Rulf. See [[Find Ilin Cotton]].

**So Yartar in the next few days holds, at once:** Ilin, the Cult of C's "big" play, and Var's hunter Cardon meeting that cult. Walking in means walking into all three.

## ⭐ We Are In The City *(S70)*

**We flew in on the [[Ignitamis]] from [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]] and were admitted.** Everything above stops being a plan and starts being the room we're standing in.

### Getting In — The Gate Is Screened

- **You are stopped outside the town** and sent to **check in at the guard tower**. There is a **magical ward** that halts arrivals.
- **Everyone is searched for dangerous items in the guardhouse** before being let through.
- We were met by **[[Oversword Vaelia Thann]]**, who explained the check rather than enforcing it on us.
- **We went through the magic checks and were let in** — carrying **[[Gather Ulfgardian Relics|five giant relics]]** (including a **100 lb giant's skull**), Jaak's **Sword of Heavens**, and the rest of our kit. **So the ward is not a blanket magic screen** — worth finding out what it *is* screening for.

> ⚠️ **Our arrival is on the record now.** A city that logs and searches every entrant is a city where **[[Var Borgal|Var]]'s 25,000 gp bounty** and **[[Cardon Malefic]]'s interest** have somewhere to look. Assume the guard tower knows we came in.

### The Ship

**We left the [[Ignitamis]] with an older gnome** who is **filling the tank and making repairs.** That is **two open threads closing at once**: the **oil resupply** question S69 raised, and the **repair list** left dangling since S61. **Find out what it costs, how long it takes, and how much range a full tank buys** — see [[Ignitamis]].

## ⭐ First Days In The City *(S71)*

- **A card game at a tavern.** **[[Xolkin]]**, a man with a **flying snake around his neck**, staked a **ticket to the Golden Goose** on poker — **we won it.** He was **looking for information on "Lamin"** ([[Clint]]); we told him where we last saw him, and he sent us to **Through and Through Investigators** for [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin]].
- **We took a room at One Foot in the Boot**, with **[[Joanne]]**.
- **At Through and Through** someone **told us on the side that we were in danger**, and we found **[[Garambitio]]**, the owner: **[[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] is in Yartar, looking for [[Rulf Cloudfang]], and staying at One Foot in the Boot.**
- **Ilin asked [[Joanne]] about [[Rulf Cloudfang]], Through and Through, and [[Cardon Malefic]]** — but **he hasn't been in his room for a week.** **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] got the key**; Ilin's **journal lists houses he was investigating.**
- **The [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]] are looking for us** — all of us, or at least the [[Harpers]] among us. Per [[Xolkin]], they **want to talk to us about [[Goldenfields]] and what we did there.** They are trying to **weaken the West**, and **Goldenfields is the key** — they're **gathering information on it.**
- **A wanted poster** in town: **"Zephros," wanted by "S"** *(as [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] logged it — who either name refers to is unconfirmed)*.
- **We asked [[Laeral Silverhand]] for advice about the Red Mages.** *(Her answer isn't recorded.)*

## ⭐ In Disguise, And Then Underground *(S72)*

- **We woke at One Foot in the Boot.** **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] went out to buy disguise kits** to hide us from the **[[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]]** — **bought** them from **a large shop** (unnamed), **quietly**, and **almost ran into the Red Mages on the way back to the inn.**
- **The Red Mages are walking the streets of this city looking for us**, and they came within a street of our lodging.
- **We disguised ourselves and went to work [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin's list of houses]].**
- **At the house, we fell through a carpet into a dungeon under it** — fought **"spider wolves"**, killed them, and met **"weird monkey creatures"** further down the hall. **Still down there.**

> **Yartar has a level below it.** A private house on a missing Harper's investigation list has **a concealed dungeon full of hostile creatures** under its floor. **That reframes the gate ward** — a city that screens and searches every arrival, with *this* underneath it.

## ⭐ The Dungeon Is The Cult's — And Ilin Was In It *(S73)*

- **Killed the "weird monkey creatures"**, which **dissolved into black goo.**
- **Found [[Ilin Cotton]] in a torture room** in the tunnels. **Freed and healed him.**
- **[[Doctor Hadol]]** (works for **[[Cardon Malefic]]**, building **technology to rival magic**) arrived with a **red-hooded [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of Cyric]] mage** who seems to have **hired him**. **Hadol escaped by technological dimension door**; we knocked out the mage, but **interrogating him got us nothing**, and **he was killed when he tried to attack [[Var Borgal|Var]] during the dealmaking.**
- **Killed "hell creatures" and more cult members** in the next rooms.
- **Ilin is fighting with us**, here to **save children he believes the cult brought here.** **We're still underground.**

> **The [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]]'s "something big" (S59) has a basement.** This is the first cult site we've found in [[Yartar]], and **[[Cardon Malefic]]'s people are working in it.**

### Getting There *(S69)*

**Yartar is a node on the [[Harpers|Harper teleportation-circle network]]** that [[Krowen Valharrow]] opened to us — **500 gp of diamond dust puts six of us in the city**, attendant present. **Relevant now for leaving and coming back**, not for arriving.
