# Yartar

> **Type:** City
> **Region:** Sword Coast North
> **Ruler:** **[[Nestra Ruthiol]]**, the **Waterbaron** *(named S70; not yet met)*
> **Significance:** **[[Harpers]] stronghold**; **[[Ilin Cotton]]'s current destination** chasing a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] lead (S57)
> **Status:** ⭐ **We are here.** Arrived and admitted [[Session 70 - Var - 2026-08-31|Session 70]].

## Why It Matters Now

**Yartar is becoming the convergence point for half the campaign's live threads.** As of [[Session 59 - Var - 2026-06-08|S59]], underworld intel at the [[Council of the Underworld]] revealed the **[[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] has "risen from the ashes," is buying poisons for "something big," and is massing on Yartar** — and **[[Cardon Malefic]] is travelling here in ~3 days to meet the cult**, escorted by [[Black Network|Zhentarim]] and [[Jarlaxle]]. That stacks on the S57 lead:

Per [[Malkyn Serpentwind]] at the [[Tavern Misty Beard]] (S57), **[[Ilin Cotton]] is ahead of us, headed here** on a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] "end of the world" lead. Ilin is also still hunting [[Rulf Cloudfang]] — if we reach Yartar we expect to either meet Ilin or pick up his trail toward Rulf. See [[Find Ilin Cotton]].

**So Yartar in the next few days holds, at once:** Ilin, the Cult of C's "big" play, and Var's hunter Cardon meeting that cult. Walking in means walking into all three.

## ⭐ We Are In The City *(S70)*

**We flew in on the [[Ignitamis]] from [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]] and were admitted.** Everything above stops being a plan and starts being the room we're standing in.

### Getting In — The Gate Is Screened

- **You are stopped outside the town** and sent to **check in at the guard tower**. There is a **magical ward** that halts arrivals.
- **Everyone is searched for dangerous items in the guardhouse** before being let through.
- We were met by **[[Oversword Vaelia Thann]]**, who explained the check rather than enforcing it on us.
- **We went through the magic checks and were let in** — carrying **[[Gather Ulfgardian Relics|five giant relics]]** (including a **100 lb giant's skull**), Jaak's **Sword of Heavens**, and the rest of our kit. **So the ward is not a blanket magic screen** — worth finding out what it *is* screening for.

> ⚠️ **Our arrival is on the record now.** A city that logs and searches every entrant is a city where **[[Var Borgal|Var]]'s 25,000 gp bounty** and **[[Cardon Malefic]]'s interest** have somewhere to look. Assume the guard tower knows we came in.

### The Ship

**We left the [[Ignitamis]] with an older gnome** who is **filling the tank and making repairs.** That is **two open threads closing at once**: the **oil resupply** question S69 raised, and the **repair list** left dangling since S61. **Find out what it costs, how long it takes, and how much range a full tank buys** — see [[Ignitamis]].

### Getting There *(S69)*

**Yartar is a node on the [[Harpers|Harper teleportation-circle network]]** that [[Krowen Valharrow]] opened to us — **500 gp of diamond dust puts six of us in the city**, attendant present. **Relevant now for leaving and coming back**, not for arriving.

## Factions & Powers

- **The Waterbaron, [[Nestra Ruthiol]]** — the city's ruler. **Named to us at the gate (S70); not met.**
- **The city guard** — commanded through officers ranked **Oversword** (see **[[Oversword Vaelia Thann]]**). They run the **ward and the search** at the gate.
- **[[Harpers]]** — Yartar is a Harper stronghold. Plausible point of contact for catching up with Ilin via a local cell. **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] are [[Harpers|Bright Candles]] now** — that is a door we can knock on in our own name.
- **Lion Shield Merchant Empire** — their **leader is based in Yartar**; outpost in [[Phandalin]] (lion-on-shield emblem). **Powerful.** Jack travels with their goods. Listed alongside threats — status unclear.

### The Golden Goose
- **Refined gambling house** — *not* a typical den; nobles, merchants, thrill-seekers
- **Invitation-only** — the rumored **5,000 gp ticket** may be the invitation cost
- Per [[The Waterdeep Wazoo Issue 496|the Wazoo]]: the illustration reads as a **massive multi-deck ship** (or ship-shaped building) — visually a ship, not a building
- Possible lead for [[Oren]]'s "games" hint ("gold pays, luck pays more")
- **S59: we asked [[Thomas]] to get us an invitation** — a possible legitimate way into Yartar's high-society underground just as the cult/Cardon descend on the city
