# Yartar

> **Type:** City
> **Region:** Sword Coast North
> **Significance:** **[[Harpers]] stronghold**; **[[Ilin Cotton]]'s current destination** chasing a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] lead (S57)

## Why It Matters Now

Per [[Malkyn Serpentwind]] at the [[Tavern Misty Beard]] (S57), **[[Ilin Cotton]] is ahead of us, headed here** on a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] "end of the world" lead. Ilin is also still hunting [[Rulf Cloudfang]] — if we reach Yartar we expect to either meet Ilin or pick up his trail toward Rulf. See [[Find Ilin Cotton]].

## Factions & Powers

- **[[Harpers]]** — Yartar is a Harper stronghold. Plausible point of contact for catching up with Ilin via a local cell.
- **Lion Shield Merchant Empire** — their **leader is based in Yartar**; outpost in [[Phandalin]] (lion-on-shield emblem). **Powerful.** Jack travels with their goods. Listed alongside threats — status unclear.

## Notable Places Within

### The Golden Goose
- **Refined gambling house** — *not* a typical den; nobles, merchants, thrill-seekers
- **Invitation-only** — the rumored **5,000 gp ticket** may be the invitation cost
- Per [[The Waterdeep Wazoo Issue 496|the Wazoo]]: the illustration reads as a **massive multi-deck ship** (or ship-shaped building) — visually a ship, not a building
- Possible lead for [[Oren]]'s "games" hint ("gold pays, luck pays more")

## Notable NPCs Here

- **[[Lamin]]** — vendor of exotic flora; sold the cursed seeds to Sevembra ([[The Cursed Seeds]]). Wears a fedora and coat; shady, high-charisma. Wagon is red-and-white with a **black dove** symbol.
- **Lion Shield leader** — name not yet known to us
