# Wave Echo Cave

> **Type:** Cavern / dungeon

## What We Know

- The mine **isn't dry** — riches still to be found.
- Contains the **[[The Forge of Spells|Forge of Spells]]** — used to make magical weapons.
- **[[Mormesk]]**, a wraith, controls undead inside and wants the Forge.
- The **Bear King** is referenced here (context to confirm).

## Approach Plan

## Threats Inside

- Likely traps and other Forge-guardian creatures
