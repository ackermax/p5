### Nightstone
- **A megalith of purple-and-black stone** stands here — a stonehenge that **fell from the clouds** (likely literal — cloud giant origin)
- Named for the megalith
- **[[Velrosa Nandar]]** was the Lady of Nightstone — **died in the attack**
- **The [[Black Network]] attacked Nightstone**, pretending to be allies first
- **Symbol of [[Asmodeus]]** seen connected to the attack — possible second player beyond the Zhentarim
- **Hill giants** were the original reason Daggerford called us in
- **Residents were carted away by goblins**
- We collected the **rod that points to [[Vonindod]]** and the **adamantine ring** here from **Duke Zalto's** people

### Baldur's Gate
