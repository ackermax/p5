# Everlund

> **Type:** City
> **Region:** The Silver Marches (near [[Other Locations#Silverymoon|Silverymoon]])
> **First visited:** [[Session 66 - Gotrek - 2026-08-03|Session 66]]

## Description

A city in the Silver Marches, and a **[[Harpers|Harper]] centre of gravity** — **[[Moongleam Tower]]** stands just outside it. We had been pointed here from two directions for several sessions before we finally arrived in S66, flying in on the [[Ignitamis]] with a dying Ulfgardian chieftain aboard.

## Notable Places Within

- **[[Moongleam Tower]]** — [[Harpers|Harper]] stronghold **just outside the city**; seat of [[Krowen Valharrow]]
- **A [[Harpers|Harper teleportation circle]]** *(S69)* — Everlund is a node on the network Valharrow opened to us
- **[[Clint]]** — shady merchant; we bought gear off him in S69

## Notable NPCs Here

- **[[Krowen Valharrow]]** — Archmage of Silverymoon, **Watcher of the Silver Marches**, and a **High Harper** (confirmed S66). Based at [[Moongleam Tower]].
- **[[Droop]]** *(S66)* — our goblin friend and [[Vajra Safahr]]'s apprentice; met us inside the tower and led us to Valharrow. **Confirmed the same Droop we know from [[Blackstaff Academy]]** — what he's doing here is still unexplained.
- **A hired mage** *(S66, unnamed)* — cast **greater restoration** on [[Arnzan Vashk]] for **1,600 gp**
- **Allerick** — a knight from here

## Events Here

- **S69 — collected on the ghost job.** Reported [[The Everlund Apparitions]] to [[Krowen Valharrow]] and were paid: **access to the [[Harpers|Harper teleportation-circle network]]** — **Everlund is one of its nodes** — plus **a circle for our own [[Phandalin|bastion]]** with **[[Droop]]** as its attendant, and **Bright Candle rank for [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]]**. Also **shopped with [[Clint]]** before leaving. We then went **on foot** to the [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Stone Stand]] mound.
- **S66 — first visit.** Flew in with [[Arnzan Vashk]] to find someone who could cast *greater restoration*. On approach a **glowing wisp** met us — a **communication device** belonging to [[Krowen Valharrow]], who told us to come to him at [[Moongleam Tower]]. Inside, **Droop** brought us to Valharrow, who asked us to look into a **ghost problem northeast of the city** in exchange for something "very valuable" (unspecified) — see [[The Everlund Apparitions]]. We agreed, **paid 1,600 gp** to have Vashk cured, feasted, and flew him home to [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]] before turning back toward Everlund for the ghost job.

- **S67 — the forest northeast of the city.** Landed the [[Ignitamis]] on **[[Minor Contacts#Everlund — The Northeast Forest|Wallace]]**'s farmland, interviewed the lumberjacks, and found **[[Aasimar Temple|the mound]]** — an **aasimar temple or mausoleum** whose dead are **aasimar**. See [[The Everlund Apparitions]]. *(We did not re-enter the city itself.)*

- **S68 — the job done, and heading back to the city.** Cleared [[Aasimar Temple|the mound]]: all four keystones, the portal opened, and **[[The Angel]] destroyed.** **[[Althariel]]'s spirit** gave [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] answers and the **Sword of Heavens**. We told the logging camp the ghosts were busted, took a **long rest at the [[Ignitamis]]**, and **set out for Everlund** — **to report to [[Krowen Valharrow]] and collect.** *(City not yet re-entered.)*

## Hooks & Rumors

- **[[The Everlund Apparitions]]** — Valharrow's job, northeast of the city. **Site cleared (S68); we owe him the report and he owes us the reward.**
- **The High Harper audience** — once we report in, Valharrow wants **[[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] alone, "as a High Harper."** **This is the next thing that happens in Everlund.**
- **[[Track the Red Mages]]** — [[Sister Garaele]] sent [[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] to Moongleam Tower to meet someone here (S63). Valharrow wants to speak with exactly those two **as a High Harper, after the ghost job** — likely the same meeting.
- **[[Gundren Rockseeker]] has been pushing us to come to Everlund** for sessions — his reason is still unstated, and we did not chase it down while here.
- **[[Harshnag]]** was reported to be traveling here to visit Valharrow ([[Harshnags Hunt]]) — he did not come up during the S66 visit.
