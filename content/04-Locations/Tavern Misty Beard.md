# Tavern Misty Beard

> **Type:** Tavern
> **Location:** [[Waterdeep]]
> **Boss / Proprietor:** **[[Malkyn Serpentwind]]** — son of the owner, returned a year or two ago
> **Significance:** [[Ilin Cotton]]'s trail — confirmed alive and on the move (S57); **[[Rulf Cloudfang]]** has also passed through

## The Place

A working tavern with a **menagerie of wait staff** of all kinds of weird species — among them:

- a skeleton
- a tiny dragon
- a mushroom man
- a lizardman

The **barkeep is the purveyor of information** in this room. He makes you sign a **waiver** before he'll feed you the more sensitive tips.

### House Drink

- **The Kraken's Mercy** — a new drink the bartender is testing. **Paralyzes the drinker's legs for ten minutes.** Jaak found this out the unfunny way.

## Why It Matters

The Misty Beard is the **junction point** between the cult-side hunt (Ilin / Rulf) and Jaak's troupe-era past (Malkyn).

### Confirmed Through Here (Session 57)

- **[[Ilin Cotton]]** — was here a few weeks ago; left for **[[Other Locations|Yartar]]** chasing a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] lead
- **[[Rulf Cloudfang]]** — was here ~6 months ago in a black cloak, eager to talk to Malkyn, worried he was being watched
- **[[Malkyn Serpentwind]]** — the boss; in a wheelchair, coughing; doctors call it "trauma" (Jaak suspects a cover story)
