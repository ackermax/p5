# Moongleam Tower

> **Type:** Stronghold — **[[Harpers]]**
> **Region:** just outside [[Everlund]]
> **First visited:** [[Session 66 - Gotrek - 2026-08-03|Session 66]]

## Description

A **[[Harpers|Harper]] stronghold standing just outside [[Everlund]]** (our older notes call it "Moongleam Towers"). It is the seat of **[[Krowen Valharrow]]**, and the destination [[Sister Garaele]] pointed [[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] toward back in S63.

Approaching the city we were met by a **glowing wisp** — not a creature but a **communication device** of Valharrow's, which delivered his invitation to come to the tower.

## Notable NPCs Here

- **[[Krowen Valharrow]]** — Archmage of Silverymoon, **High Harper**; the tower's master
- **[[Droop]]** — met us at the door and escorted us in (S66). **The same Droop we know** from [[Blackstaff Academy]] in [[Waterdeep]], where he apprentices under [[Vajra Safahr]]. Why he's here is an open question.

## Events Here

- **S66 — first visit.** Wisp-summoned, admitted by [[Droop]], and received by Valharrow. He asked us to investigate **ghostly apparitions northeast of Everlund** ([[The Everlund Apparitions]]), promising something "very valuable" in return, and said that **after the job** he wants to speak with **[[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] as a High Harper, not as an archmage.** We accepted, and separately paid **1,600 gp** for the *greater restoration* that cured [[Arnzan Vashk]].
