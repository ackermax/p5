- **The Blackstaff seat was vacant for 60 years.**
- Vajra Safahr is the **7th Blackstaff**. Not well-regarded by the other archmages — they don't believe she's powerful enough. **[[Naxene Drathkala]] likes her.**
- Laeral has **6 sisters**. She's closer to the Weave than most.
