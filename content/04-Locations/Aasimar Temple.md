# Aasimar Temple

> **Type:** Temple or mausoleum, inside a mound
> **Region:** The forest **northeast of [[Everlund]]**, in the Silver Marches
> **First visited:** [[Session 67 - Var - 2026-08-10|Session 67]]

> [!info] "Aasimar Temple" is our working name
> **The site's true name is unknown** — nobody gave us one. We're calling it this because its dead are **aasimar**. Rename when we learn what it was actually called.
>
> *Note on spelling: [[Session 67 - Var - 2026-08-10|Var's]] notes render the word "**Aesir**" and [[Session 67 - Jaak - 2026-08-10|Jaak's]] "Asimaar" — both are the same word, **aasimar**, [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s own race. The vault uses **aasimar** throughout.*

## Description

From outside it doesn't look like a building at all: a **round, strange, unnatural hill, full of foliage**, standing in the forest past the farmland and the lumberjack camps. **[[Minor Contacts#Everlund — The Northeast Forest|Georgie]]** the lumberjack described it before we ever saw it, and the description held.

Inside it is a **temple or mausoleum** with a **main chamber** and an **altar**. It is a **warded** place — something killed the aasimar here, and the wards are what have kept that thing out since. They are **failing now**, and **keystones** are what hold them up.

> Despite the mound shape, **nothing connects this to the [[Ulfgardian Mounds]]**. Different region, different dead, no stated link.

## Notable Places Within

- **The main chamber** — holds the **altar**, and is where the spectrals appeared. After we destroyed the burning portal, **the altar discharged a second wave of radiant energy** on its own.

## Notable NPCs Here

- **The aasimar spectrals** — slim winged humanoids in strange robes, wings decrepit and shedding feathers, faces haunted and sunken-eyed, visibly **sad**. They appear **in the afternoon**. They rose to attack in the main chamber and **[[Jaak Greye]] made them stand down using his own aasimar heritage** — *"for now at least."*
- **[[Altariar]]** — named as **"the last of the aasimar at this base."** Whether Altariar is one of the spectrals or a name out of the site's history is **not established**.
- **The teleporting trickster** *(unnamed, killed)* — sneaky ambusher that **stole one of [[Gotrek Runehammer|Gotrek]]'s weapons**. We recovered the weapon and took **a keystone** off it.
- **The burning portal / "sun"** *(destroyed)* — opened combat by spawning **motes of radiant light**.

## Events Here

- **S67 — first entry.** Landed the [[Ignitamis]] on **[[Minor Contacts#Everlund — The Northeast Forest|Wallace]]**'s farmland, interviewed the lumberjacks, and found the mound — **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] approached it first.** Killed the trickster (**keystone #1**), found **keystone #2** searching the space, talked down the spectrals, and destroyed the burning portal — after which the altar answered with radiant energy. **Whatever killed the aasimar is breaking the defenses down to come back.** Full state on [[The Everlund Apparitions]].
