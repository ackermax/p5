# Aasimar Temple

> **Type:** Temple or mausoleum, inside a mound
> **Region:** The forest **northeast of [[Everlund]]**, in the Silver Marches
> **First visited:** [[Session 67 - Var - 2026-08-10|Session 67]]

> [!info] "Aasimar Temple" is our working name
> **The site's true name is unknown** — nobody gave us one. We're calling it this because its dead are **aasimar**. Rename when we learn what it was actually called.
>
> *Note on spelling: [[Session 67 - Var - 2026-08-10|Var's]] notes render the word "**Aesir**" and [[Session 67 - Jaak - 2026-08-10|Jaak's]] "Asimaar" — both are the same word, **aasimar**, [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s own race. The vault uses **aasimar** throughout.*

## Description

From outside it doesn't look like a building at all: a **round, strange, unnatural hill, full of foliage**, standing in the forest past the farmland and the lumberjack camps. **[[Minor Contacts#Everlund — The Northeast Forest|Georgie]]** the lumberjack described it before we ever saw it, and the description held.

Inside it is a **temple or mausoleum** — the party also calls it **the tomb** — with a **main chamber** and an **altar**. We read it as a warded place keeping something *out*. **We had it backwards** *(S68)*: the site is a **prison**, and the **four keystones** are the **key to the cell**. Set them in the altar and a **portal** opens onto the place where **[[The Angel]]** was held — radiant light pouring off it hard enough to hurt anyone standing near.

> Despite the mound shape, **nothing connects this to the [[Ulfgardian Mounds]]**. Different region, different dead, no stated link.

## Notable Places Within

- **The main chamber** — holds the **altar**, and is where the spectrals appeared. After we destroyed the burning portal, **the altar discharged a second wave of radiant energy** on its own *(S67)*.
- **The altar** — **the lock.** All four **keystones** seated in it **open a portal** *(S68)*. **The keystones are spent afterward** — we still carry them, but they are inert.
- **Beyond the portal — the Angel's cell** *(S68)*. A separate place, and **the Angel controlled it**: something struck us **every round** we were inside. Past the fight is **the spot where it was actually locked up**, where **[[Althariel]]'s spirit** met us. Althariel **remade the portal** afterward and sent us back to the tomb.
- **The rubble from the trickster fight** — **keystone #4** was lying in it the whole time *(S68)*.

## Notable NPCs Here

- **The aasimar spectrals** — slim winged humanoids in strange robes, wings decrepit and shedding feathers, faces haunted and sunken-eyed, visibly **sad**. They appear **in the afternoon**. They rose to attack in the main chamber and **[[Jaak Greye]] made them stand down using his own aasimar heritage** — *"for now at least."*
- **[[Althariel]]** — **the last of the aasimar at this base**, and **the one who brought [[The Angel|the Angel]] here years ago.** It killed him and all his kin; **they are the ghosts.** His **spirit** met us past the portal in S68, thanked us, told us what [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] are, and gave Jaak the **Sword of Heavens**.
- **[[The Angel]]** *(destroyed, S68)* — a **caged one-eyed monster** sealed beyond the altar. Wanted to **"cleanse our world"**; summoned **flying swords**; promised **"the Lord" will return.**
- **The mournful ghosts** *(S68)* — talked down rather than fought. They told us they **angered something very powerful from another dimension**, which is **sending probes into our plane to consume it.**
- **The angry ghosts** *(S68)* — the ones that would not be talked down. **Keystone #3** came off that fight.
- **The teleporting trickster** *(unnamed, killed)* — sneaky ambusher that **stole one of [[Gotrek Runehammer|Gotrek]]'s weapons**. We recovered the weapon and took **a keystone** off it.
- **The burning portal / "sun"** *(destroyed)* — opened combat by spawning **motes of radiant light**.

## Events Here

- **S67 — first entry.** Landed the [[Ignitamis]] on **[[Minor Contacts#Everlund — The Northeast Forest|Wallace]]**'s farmland, interviewed the lumberjacks, and found the mound — **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] approached it first.** Killed the trickster (**keystone #1**), found **keystone #2** searching the space, talked down the spectrals, and destroyed the burning portal — after which the altar answered with radiant energy. **Whatever killed the aasimar is breaking the defenses down to come back.** Full state on [[The Everlund Apparitions]].

- **S68 — the site cleared.** Fought the **angry ghosts** for **keystone #3**, talked down the **mournful ghosts**, and found **keystone #4 in the rubble** of the S67 trickster fight. **All four into the altar → the portal opened.** Through it: **[[The Angel]]**, which broke its cage and fought us with **flying swords** and **round-by-round control of the plane**. **We killed it.** **[[Althariel]]'s spirit** appeared past the fight, **thanked us**, revealed the **[[Blood of the Ancients|ancient bloodlines]]**, pointed us at the **aasimar of the [[Order of the Gauntlet]] at the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]]**, **gave [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] the Sword of Heavens**, and **remade the portal** to send us back. **Short rest in the tomb**, then out — the haunting is over.

- **[[The Angel]] was a probe.** The ghosts named **something from another dimension sending probes to consume our plane**; the Angel promised **"the Lord"** would return. **This site is closed. That thread is not.**
- **[[Blood of the Ancients]]** *(new, S68)* — what this place was really here to tell us.
- **[[Jaaks Celestial War]]** — a site full of dead aasimar that answers to [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s blood, and the aasimar who finally gave him answers.
