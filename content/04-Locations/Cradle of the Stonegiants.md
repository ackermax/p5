# Cradle of the Stonegiants

> **Type:** Stone giant homeland / spiritual center
> **Where:** Among the stone giants — exact location unknown to vault, known to [[Gotrek Runehammer]]
> **First mentioned:** [[Session 56 - Var - 2026-05-18]] — [[Eldrin]] told [[Gotrek Runehammer]] he was sent there

## Why It Matters

- The **cradle** where [[Gotrek Runehammer]] came from — his cleft, his origin
- **[[Eldrin]] sent Gotrek there** through the dreamrealm — meaning this is where the response to [[Thane Kayalithica]]'s plan must begin
- The stone giants here are **different from other stone giants** — described as **more reclusive**

## What Eldrin Told Gotrek

- The Cradle is where Gotrek was sent (in the dreamrealm vision)
- The giants there are **more reclusive** than typical stone giants
- The Cradle is positioned within the **[[Stone Giant Factions|Stoneshapers vs Dreamweavers]]** schism
- Gotrek **needs to return to the Cradle** physically to act against [[Thane Kayalithica]]
