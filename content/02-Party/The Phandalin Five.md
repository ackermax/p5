# The Phandalin Five

![[Phandalin Five Logo.png]]

> **The party's chosen name.** Named for [[Phandalin]], where they all came together.

## Roster

1. [[Var Borgal]] — Human Aberrant Mind Sorcerer
2. [[Gotrek Runehammer]] — Dwarf Barbarian, Path of the Giant
3. [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]] — Human Ranger, Hunter Conclave
4. [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] — Elf Rogue, Arcane Trickster
5. [[Jaak Greye]] — Aasimar Bard, College of Glamour

## Public Profile

Before adopting a name, the [[The Waterdeep Wazoo Issue 496|Wazoo]] described the party as *"an unnamed band of adventurers... the group bears no banner or known name."* Notable deeds attributed to the party while still anonymous:

- **Defended [[Goldenfields]]** from a giant assault
- **Helped repel a Fire Giant attack at [[Other Locations|Triboar]]** — many lives were lost in the battle
- **Rescued villagers near [[Other Locations|Nightstone]]** kidnapped by goblins following a Cloud Giant raid

With "The Phandalin Five" now adopted, those deeds and any going forward can be attributed by name.

|---|---|---|

## Why The Name Works

- **Anchored to a real place** — [[Phandalin]] is where the party formed and still has an active hub (Gundren's manor, Halia's Miners Exchange, Sister Garaele's shrine, Sheriff Sildar's office)
- **Numbered** — easy to remember, scales with reputation
- **Stable** — doesn't reference any single member's gimmick; survives if a player swaps characters
