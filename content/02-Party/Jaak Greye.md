## Backstory

Jaak was **found at age 8 with no memory of his past** — only his name and **fragmented visions of his own death in a great celestial war**. In those flashes:
- A stray attack pierces him
- He's knocked from a chariot
- **Faceless angelic and demonic figures** watch as he falls
- He'd recognize their faces if he saw them again

He was picked up on the roadside by **[[The Traveling Wilmelons]]**, a traveling musical troupe.

### The Wilmelons (At Jaak's Departure)

- **[[Ilin Cotton]]** — halfling keytarist. Took Jaak in over Rulf's objection. Secretly taught him to play.
- **[[Malkyn Serpentwind]]** — human drummer. Weak-willed. **Rulf was cruel to him.** Now in [[Waterdeep]], can't use his legs.
- **[[Anastas Glorybluff]]** — tiefling flutist. Weak-willed. **Rulf was cruel to him.** Now found in [[Goldenfields]] — became a major info source about Rulf.
- **[[Rulf Cloudfang]]** — half-elf fiddler. Cruel leader. Allied with [[Cult of Cyric]].

### The Turning Point — Age 15

The troupe performed for **the Cult of Cyric**'s leader. Jaak met **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]]**, a girl being raised in the monastery. They became fast friends.

One evening **[[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] pulled Jaak aside** with something urgent — *something Jaak needed to know immediately*. **[[Rulf Cloudfang]] and the cult leader barged in** and cut it off. That night, Ilin vanished. Jaak took his place at the keytar, played flawlessly, became the new lead. Said a tearful goodbye to Estelle.

### The Heist — Age 18

Three years later, returned with the troupe. Estelle had learned the full truth of the cult — **it was the [[Cult of Cyric]]**, and **[[Rulf Cloudfang|Rulf]] was scouting young people for them to sacrifice**, with specific qualities. **Jaak was one of those targets** — "I was important to Rulf, and there was something Rulf needed from me." Jaak and Estelle stole Estelle's inheritance and escaped.
