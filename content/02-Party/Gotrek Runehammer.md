## Backstory (From The Campaign Notes)

Gotrek was **raised in a cleft**. The leader of that cleft was regarded as a **[[Stone Giant Factions|Dreamwalker]]** — one of the two stone giant traditions descended from [[Skoraeus Stonebones]]. The Dreamwalker symbol is now burned into Gotrek's runes.

Gotrek has a **brother — [[Eldrin]]** (name confirmed session 56). Eldrin was hunting **[[Thane Kayalithica]]** — a powerful **Stoneshaper (Rockspeaker)**.

## The Dreamrealm Encounter (Session 56)

[[Eldrin]] reached Gotrek **in the dreamrealm**. The conversation:

- **[[Eldrin]] is alive** but **in hiding**
- Gotrek was sent in vision to the **[[Cradle of the Stonegiants]]** — where he came from. The stone giants there are **different — more reclusive**.
- **[[Thane Kayalithica]]** (a Stoneshaper, "from ss" = the Stoneshape clan) is **trying to unite both sides** of the schism — by bringing the Dreamweavers (Dreamwalkers) under her command
- **She is doing something vile** — trying to **destroy the dream world itself**
- If she succeeds:
  - **No mortal will be able to dream**
  - **All Dreamweavers could be killed**
- **[[Gotrek Runehammer|Gotrek]] needs to stop her** and **return to the Cradle** to do so
- [[Gotrek Runehammer]] must unlock his hidden powers in order to face [[Thane Kayalithica]] according to [[Eldrin]]
- His brother is still alive but cannot expose himself

This makes [[Thane Kayalithica]] one of the campaign's headline threats — peer-level to [[Hekaton]]'s disappearance.
