## Backstory

Estelle's mother snuck into a monastery late at night while in labor and **died in childbirth**. No identity, no family found. The monastery became Estelle's home. **The monastery was a [[Cult of Cyric|Cult of Cyric]] front** — god of murder, lies, and tyranny.

She grew up among the dead (catacombs where her mother was buried anonymously) and the living archives (library, monastery stacks). **She has an affinity with the dead and ghosts.**

In her late teens she discovered that **money had been anonymously sent to the monastery for her** for years. The clerics had been **hoarding it for themselves**. She prepared for weeks, **stole her inheritance back** plus jewelry the nuns had been gifted, and **fled in the night**.

> *"After years of deprivations, I have a taste for the finer things in life and intend to claim what is rightfully mine."*
