# Find Rulf Cloudfang

> **Status:** Active — ⭐ **found and fought S74**: he is **the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]]'s current leader.** "Killed," but **his body dissipated** and **he vanished into the ether** — after **killing [[Ilin Cotton]].**
> **For:** [[Jaak Greye]]; secondarily [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] and the broader [[Reckoning With The Cult Of Cyric]] arc

## Why

[[Rulf Cloudfang]] is the [[Cult of Cyric]]'s mobile recruiter. He scouted young people (including Jaak) for sacrifice. He likely killed or arranged for the disappearance of [[Ilin Cotton]]. He beat Malkyn so badly Malkyn can't walk anymore.

## ⭐ Session 74 — Found Him. Lost Him.

At **the top of the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] dungeon** under [[Yartar]], a **hooded figure** turned out to be **Rulf** — **the current leader of the Cult of Cyric.** We learned that **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] was being groomed as the cult's next leader**, that **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] was only ever good for his blood** to finish their ritual, and that **the ritual's goal is bringing [[Cyric]] back.**

**We think we killed him** — but **when Jaak checked the body, it dissipated as if he was never there at all**, and **Rulf stabbed [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] and killed him** as he disappeared into the ether.

> **The quest is not over.** Treat Rulf as **alive and loose**. The "capture him alive" plan below was built for a lieutenant; **he's the head of the cult**, and **he's now killed Ilin in front of us.**

## Last Known Position

**S74: the top of the cult dungeon under [[Yartar]]** — vanished into the ether. Older intel below.

**[[Tavern Misty Beard]] in [[Waterdeep]], ~6 months ago** (per [[Malkyn Serpentwind]], session 57). He was in a **black cloak**, **eager to talk to Malkyn**, and **worried he was being watched / hunted**.

Before that: **Neverwinter** — went there, came back in a bad mood, beat up Malkyn. Anastas reports Rulf was *impatient* — implying he was expecting something from the trip that didn't materialize.

> **⚠️ S69:** **[[Other Locations#Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] is closed off** — it is the one city **excluded** from the [[Harpers|Harper teleportation-circle network]], *"because of Neverember."* If Rulf's trail ever points back there, **we cannot take the short road**, and something is going on in that city that even the Harpers won't route into.

## New Read — Puppet, Not Master *(S57; challenged S74)*

Per Malkyn: Rulf told him he **"had no choice"** and **"didn't know what was at stake"** when admitting to child abductions. **Rulf may be a puppet** — coerced or leveraged by something above him in the cult. This doesn't reduce his danger, but it changes what we want from him: we want **who is pulling his strings**.

> **S74:** Rulf is **the cult's current leader.** If anything is above him now, it's **the god they're trying to bring back.**
