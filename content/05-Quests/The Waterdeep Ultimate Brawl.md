## The Two Trials

### I. The Tournament of Prowess
> *"A contest of mortal skill, where warrior faces warrior, and victory is claimed by strength, discipline, and mastery at arms."*

Two formats:
1. **Bracket Tournament** — paired matches, single-elimination
2. **Free-for-All** — all enter together; strongest, cleverest, or luckiest remain

- **Entry Fee:** 250 gp **per person**
- **Prizes:**
  - 1st place: **5,000 gp**
  - 2nd place: **2,500 gp**
  - 3rd place: **1,000 gp**

> *"Let no contestant complain of bruises, broken pride, or spilled blood, for all who enter do so of their own free will."*

### II. The Monster Arena
> *"For those whose courage is not satisfied by dueling their fellow mortals, the arena shall also host a Monster Challenge, wherein groups of adventurers face a succession of deadly encounters."*

- **Format:** Five challenges in succession, each more dangerous than the last
- **Entry Fee:** 1,000 gp **per team**
- **Rewards by Challenge Completed:**
  - 1st: 500 gp
  - 2nd: One item from **Magic Item Table B**
  - 3rd: Five Potions of Superior Healing
  - 4th: One item from **Magic Item Table C**
  - 5th: *"A prize worthy of champions yet undefeated"*

## Strategic Analysis

**Cost math for our party of 5:**
- All 5 enter the Prowess Tournament: **1,250 gp**
- Party enters the Monster Arena: **1,000 gp**
- **Both:** 2,250 gp combined buy-in

**Worth it?** The Monster Arena ceiling is *very* high — Magic Item Table C is uncommon-to-rare territory (Cloak of Protection, Bag of Holding, etc.), and "a prize worthy of champions yet undefeated" implies *something better than Table C* for the 5th challenge. Potions of Superior Healing alone are 100 gp each — five is 500 gp of consumables. Even completing 2-3 challenges pays for entry.

**Prowess Tournament** is more variable — strong PvP fighter chassis is rewarded, but free-for-all is luck-heavy. [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]] (Hunter Ranger with Greatsword + Shield Master) is the obvious champion candidate.

- The arena **transformed from swamp to a fleshy surface covered in eyes**, with a **giant open mouth full of teeth at the center** — do **not** get dragged into it
- **6–8 eldritch horrors** surround the central maw-pit, **whispering into our heads**
- **The madness mechanic:** starting your turn inside **any monster's circle of influence** drives you mad. We **defeated them all quickly** and moved on.

### Round 3 — Frozen Tundra (✅ Won, Session 60)
- The arena became a **frozen tundra dotted with trees**; we were attacked by **wolves and yetis**
- **Fireball was really good** — cleared them out fast

### Round 4 — Water Arena (✅ Won, Session 60)
- A **flooded arena** against **sharks, squids, and a shark-man hybrid**
- **Tough fight, but we dispatched them**

### Round 5 — Lava Flow (❌ Lost, Session 61)
- The final arena was a **field of lava flow**, with **bridges and rock forms** creating safe footing
- Our final opponent was a **fire dragon elemental** — it **overwhelmed us almost immediately. "Got hammered by the flame dragon, really didn't have a chance."**
- **Lesson (per the table):** we could have **scouted the matchup and prepped** (fire-resistance potions, weapons that bypass its defenses). This was a **deliberate end-game-difficulty fight** — we went in under-prepared.
- We **thanked [[Barnaby Baxter]]** and stayed to watch the singles bracket.
