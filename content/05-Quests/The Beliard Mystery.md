# The Beliard Mystery

> **Status:** Active investigation
> **Location:** [[Other Locations|Beliard]]

## The Situation

Strange things happening in [[Other Locations|Beliard]]. Informed by **Alhaer Mhilshoun**.

- **Merchants disappearing** — cursed-feeling
- All saw the silhouette of a person right before disappearing — **average height, likely male**
- Merchants stayed at a warehouse but spent time at the **Watchful Knight**
- All were alone, lively, and successful before disappearing
- **All disappeared in a bedroom**
- **Maids who entered** included **Lillia**
- Most assume they just left
- **No one likes Alhaer** — not even the stableman **Alzar**
- **Nicks in wood** — someone tried to open a window from outside
- **Boxes stacked** outside, easy exit from a 2nd-floor window
- **Reddish cloth** found by the crate — cotton/linen mix, **likely from a hood**

## Then The Note

This message was recovered from somewhere related to the case:

> *"The girl will be enough, soon we will have enough faces to go around. Breaking and assimilating their memory is difficult, but it won't take long until we have all of them.*
>
> *As for you, keep looking for prospects in [[Other Locations|Beliard]]. Do not do anything to jeopardize your position. I know how eager you are to consume them. But absolutely no killing in Beliard until further notice."*

## What This Means

- **There's a doppelganger / face-stealing creature operating in Beliard.** Eats faces and memories.
- The note's recipient is the field operative ("looking for prospects")
- "**The girl will be enough**" — implies *the Exquisites circus already took someone* whose face will complete a set
- This connects to **[[The Exquisites Investigation]]** — Lisa was a member of the circus, **circus passed through Beliard**, and **after they passed, Alza the herbalist changed** ("eyes a different color, scowl on her face")

## Cast In Beliard

- **Alhaer Mhilshoun** — informant; outbids everyone in town; well-disliked
- **Lillia** — maid; could be found in one of the houses
- **Alzar** — stableman; doesn't like Alhaer
- **Alza** — herbalist; **changed** after the circus came; vanished into the woods; her daughter says her eyes are different and she scowls now; gathers herbs at a place called **Shalamar**
- **Arachar Calatharr** — tavern owner
