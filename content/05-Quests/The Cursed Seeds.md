# The Cursed Seeds

> **Status:** Partial — immediate threat neutralized, source still open

## What We Stopped

[[Sevembra]], a druid outside the gate of [[Goldenfields]], had **5 cursed seeds** ready to plant. They would have **seemed to work at first, then killed all the crops** — devastating [[Goldenfields]], the breadbasket of the region.

**[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] currently holds all 5 seeds on her person.** We should ask the Treent ([[Liferlas]]) about them.

## Source

[[Lamin]] — vendor of exotic flora out of [[Other Locations|Yartar]] — **sold the seeds to Sevembra**.

### Lamin's Profile
- Wears fedora and a coat; high charisma; a bit shady
- Rides a **red and white wagon** with a **black dove symbol**
- Has done business in:

  - Some guys at the **Watchful Knight**
- Came from [[Goldenfields]], heading to [[Other Locations|Everlund]]
