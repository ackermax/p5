# The Cult in Helm's Hold

> **Given by:** **Javen Tarmikos** (Commander, [[Order of the Gilded Eyes]])
> **Status:** Active
> **Location:** [[Helm's Hold]]

## Objective

Defeat a cult operating in or around [[Helm's Hold]]. Tarmikos will speak with us further only **after the cult is defeated**.

## What We Know

- A **spell outbreak** on the road from Helm's Hold to [[Thundertree]] may be related
- The [[Order of the Gilded Eyes]] broke away from the [[Order of the Gauntlet]] — they have their own agenda
- [[The Watcher]] is the local god — cult is likely opposed to that faith

## Open Questions

- Is this the [[Cult of the Dragon]]?
- Is this the **same cult** that raised [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]? See [[Cult of Cyric]] — confirming this would resolve **two arcs in one place**.
- Connected to the **black-sun "we are watching"** messenger?
