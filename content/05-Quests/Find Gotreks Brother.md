# Find Gotrek's Brother *(now: Stop Kayalithica with Eldrin)*

> **Status:** Active and **escalated** — what was "find the brother" is now **a cosmic-stakes operation**
> **For:** [[Gotrek Runehammer]]
> **Brother's name (confirmed session 56):** **[[Eldrin]]**

## Original Objective

Locate [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s brother. Last lead was that he was searching for **[[Thane Kayalithica]]** — a powerful stone giant Stoneshaper/Rockspeaker.

## Current Objective (Session 56)

[[Eldrin]] **contacted Gotrek in the dreamrealm**. He's **alive but in hiding**. The mission has expanded:

- **Find [[Eldrin]]** — second priority now; he is alive, hiding, and reaching Gotrek through dreams
- **Stop [[Thane Kayalithica]]** — *first priority* — she is trying to **destroy the dream world**, which would kill all Dreamweavers and end mortal dreaming everywhere
- **Return to the [[Cradle of the Stonegiants]]** — the staging ground for action against Kayalithica

## What We Know About Kayalithica

- Stoneshaper / Rockspeaker, from the **Stoneshape clan**
- Sees things others can't — unusual depth of perception, deeper connection to stone
- Trying to **unite both sides of the [[Stone Giant Factions|stone giant schism]]** by bringing Dreamweavers under her command
- Her method is to **destroy the dreamrealm**
- See [[Thane Kayalithica]] for the full profile
