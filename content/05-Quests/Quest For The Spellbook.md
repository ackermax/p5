# Quest For The Spellbook

> **Given by:** [[Sister Garaele]] (Harper, [[Phandalin]])
> **Status:** Active — partial progress

## Objective

Recover the **spellbook of [[Bowgentle]]** — the most powerful spellcaster in legend.

## Progress So Far

1. ✅ Talked to [[Agatha]] (banshee, north of [[Other Locations|Conyberry]]) — gave her an **ornate comb of silver and jewels**
2. ✅ Learned the spellbook is **no longer with Agatha**
3. ⬜ **She traded it to [[Tsernoth]] in [[Other Locations|Iriaebor]]**

## Next Step

Find **Tsernoth in Iriaebor** and get the spellbook from him — by trade, by trick, by force.

## Why It Matters

The Harpers want it. That's reason enough. Beyond that, a legendary spellbook in the age of mortals is the kind of object that **major bad actors will compete to acquire** — including the [[Cult of Cyric]] and the [[Cult of the Dragon]].
