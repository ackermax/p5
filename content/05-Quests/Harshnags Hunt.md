# Harshnag's Hunt

> **What the quest is now:** Traveling alongside [[Harshnag]] on his larger hunt — for [[Hekaton]], for the relics needed to bargain with the [[Ulfgardian Mounds|oracles]], and for whatever fragments of the broken [[The Ordening|Ordening]] can be reassembled.
>
> **What it used to be:** A search for Harshnag himself, including the (probably false) salamander rumor that pointed us in his direction.

## Status

- ✅ **Located Harshnag** near [[Goldenfields]] (the "salamander" turned out to be a Behir; rumor still unresolved)
- ✅ Confirmed Harshnag is **actively working the [[Find Hekaton|Ordening problem]]** as part of [[Force Grey]]
- ✅ Learned the **oracles among the [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Ulfgardian mound tribes]] will answer Ordening questions in exchange for giant-relic sacrifices**

- ⏳ We are **meeting up with Harshnag again in three months**
- ✅ **Gate cleared (S58):** [[Thomas]]'s alliance is now secured (see [[The Shanker Job]]), so the Harshnag/Hekaton conversation Thomas was holding back is unblocked

## What This Quest Connects To

Harshnag is the spine that connects three otherwise-separate threads:

- **[[Find Hekaton]]** — the strategic objective (reinstate the Ordening). Harshnag and [[Force Grey]] are the most active allied force working on this.
- **[[Gather Ulfgardian Relics]]** — the tactical operation (collect relics for oracle sacrifice). [[Interitus]], a Force Grey wolf-guardian, is the natural broker here.
- **[[Krowen Valharrow]]** — the Archmage of Silverymoon, currently hosting Harshnag in Everlund; almost certainly a pivotal source of intel on the [[The Second Sundering|age of mortals]] and [[Doom of the Desert|the active dragon threats]].

Without Harshnag, the party would be doing freelance giant-fighting. With him, we are part of the *only coordinated effort* to fix the Ordening.

## Open Sub-Questions

- **Was the salamander real?** We assumed bad intel after finding Harshnag fighting a Behir, but the Goldenfields report specifically described "a monstrosity of fire." Could be:
  1. Bad intel (same creature, wrong identification)
  2. Two separate creatures (Behir handled, salamander still loose)
  3. Metaphor for [[Vonindod|fire giant activity]] in the region
- **Why is [[Zephyros]] tracking Harshnag?** Zephyros's agent confirmed Harshnag's location at Goldenfields. Zephyros has artifacts and wisdom that [[Countess Sansuri]] also wants. Is Zephyros trying to recruit Harshnag, warn him, or use him?
- **What's Harshnag's relationship to [[Memnor]]?** Our notes call Harshnag "Memnor's friend" — that's a strange descriptor for a good-aligned frost giant. Could be old, could be metaphorical, could be a clue.

## Strategic Note

Harshnag is not just an NPC — he is **the closest thing to a "patron" we have for the campaign's main spine.** Treat the three-month gap as the campaign-clock for the Ordening arc.
