# Find Ilin Cotton

> **Status:** Active. ⭐ **Ilin is FOUND (S73)**, freed from a [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] torture room under a [[Yartar]] house and fighting beside us. **The arc continues as a rescue: the children he came for**

## Objective

Find **[[Ilin Cotton]]** — the halfling keytarist who took in Jaak as a child, and (newly confirmed) **a [[Harpers|Harper]] who was investigating the [[Cult of Cyric]]** when he disappeared.

> **Next concrete step:** **work the journal's list of houses.** If he went to one of them and didn't come back, that's where the trail picks up — and **a missing week for a man the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] already discovered once is not good.**

## ⭐ The House Had A Dungeon Under It (Session 72)

We worked the journal, and **the first house on it dropped us into the ground.**

- **We went to the house where Ilin was last seen** — **one of the houses on his journal's list.** *(Confirmed: the journal's addresses and "where Ilin was last seen" are the same information.)*
- **Investigating the house, we fell through the carpet** into **a dungeon or cave beneath it.** The carpet was covering the way down; **we didn't find the entrance, we triggered it.**
- **Attacked immediately by "spider wolves"** — **killed them.**
- **Pushed on down the hall** and ran into **"weird monkey creatures,"** which attacked. **The session ended mid-fight.**

> **On the creature names:** **"spider wolves" and "weird monkey creatures" are all we have** — the DM gave no proper names *(confirmed)*. **The vault uses the party's descriptions.** Don't go looking for a stat block to match.

> **What this means for the search:** **Ilin's journal was right about something.** A house on his list has a **concealed dungeon full of hostile creatures** under it — which is a very good reason a man investigating houses stopped coming back to his room. **He may be down here.** The rest of the journal's addresses just became a list of suspects, not a list of errands.

> ⚠️ **We are underground, mid-combat, in disguise, in a city where the [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]] are hunting us.** Nobody above ground knows where we went.

## ✅ FOUND HIM — In A Torture Room Under The House (Session 73)

**[[Ilin Cotton]] is found.** *([[Session 73 - Jaak - 2026-10-05|Jaak]]: "Found Ilin Cotton.")*

- **We finished the "weird monkey creatures"** from the S72 cliffhanger. **As they died they dissolved into black goo.**
- **Further into the tunnels we found a torture room, and Ilin was in it, worse for wear.** **We released him and healed him.**
- **We had just started talking to him** when **[[Doctor Hadol]]** came in: a doctor building **technology to rival magic**, who **works for [[Cardon Malefic]]**. He was with **a mage in a red hood** who seems to have **hired him as a contractor**. **Hadol escaped with a technological dimension door**; **we knocked out the mage** and found he's **[[Cult of Cyric|Cult of Cyric]]**. **The interrogation got us nothing**, and **when he tried to attack [[Var Borgal|Var]] during the dealmaking, he was killed.**
- **In the next rooms we fought a big battle against "hell creatures" and more cult members, and killed them all.**
- **Ilin is fighting alongside us now.** He's down here to **save children he believes the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]] brought here**, taken by **various methods** — and **the Exquisites circus in [[Waterdeep]] was one of the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]]'s child-kidnapping operations** *(confirmed)* ([[The Exquisites Investigation]]).

> **That's where the missing week went** *(confirmed)*: **Ilin fell through the same carpet trap we did, and the cult captured and interrogated him.** This dungeon is a **[[Cult of Cyric|cult]] site**, and the house on his list was **a trap he walked into the same way we did.** **His journal was right**, and so was the S72 read that **a concealed hostile dungeon is why a man stops coming back to his room.**

> **What we still haven't heard from him:** we were interrupted before he could say much. **Not yet recorded:** **what he's learned, what the cult's interrogators were after, the "something urgent" he never got to tell [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] at the monastery, and whether "Ilin" is an alias** ([[Sister Garaele]], S63). **We have him now, so ask.**

> ⚠️ **The search has turned into a rescue.** Ilin isn't the objective anymore. **The children are.** We're still in the dungeon, and next session we keep investigating.
