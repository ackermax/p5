# Gather Ulfgardian Relics

> **Status:** Active
> **Purpose:** Fuel the [[Find Hekaton|Ordening]] spine — relics are **sacrificed to the mound oracles** in exchange for answers, including how to find and reinstate [[Hekaton]].

## Objective

Acquire giant relics from the nine [[Ulfgardian Mounds]] (each holds at least one), then **sacrifice them to the oracles** for the knowledge we need.

> **The oracle mechanic (confirmed):** the mound oracles trade *answers* for *relic sacrifices*. We are **not** collecting relics to keep or wield — we collect them to spend. (Cross-ref: [[Find Hekaton]], [[The Ordening]].)

> **Where things live:** [[Ulfgardian Mounds]] is the reference for *who's at each mound and how to approach them*. **This page tracks what we have and where we're going next** — don't re-list the mound details here.

## Relics In Hand

- **Statue of [[Stronmaus]]** (cloud-giant relic) — payment from [[Harrick Stonequill]] for rune testing, S57. **Eligible for oracle sacrifice — but flagged "last resort, return if able."** Don't burn this one casually.
- **Shining White relic — a mask** (recovered S64) — dug up over several days at the abandoned Griffin-tribe mound, **in two halves** (a griffin attack hit between finding them); looked like **two halves of a mask**. This is our first relic with no strings attached.
- **The One Stone relic** (recovered S66) — given **freely** by [[Arnzan Vashk]] after we cured him. It is a **magic item**: it can cast **_divination_ or _control weather_, once every 7 days.** No strings attached — but note we'd be **sacrificing a working magic item** if we spend this one on the oracles.
- **The Stone Stand relic — a giant skull** (recovered S69) — **100 pounds**, dug out of the Blue Bears' burial ground while **the tribe's ghosts closed in**. Taken, not given. **We stole it from a haunted graveyard and left by [[Items and References|dimension door]] — assume it is still owed for.**

### Mound Log

- **S64 — Shining White:** searched several days; recovered the relic — **a mask, in two halves** — with a **griffin attack** between them. Before leaving, spotted **fire giants hunting [[Vonindod]] pieces** nearby and slipped away unseen. Departed for **One Stone**; stopped at the mouth of a few rivers, where a **creature attacked while we fished — session-64 cliffhanger** *(probably some giant fish-monster; unconfirmed)*.
- **S65 — en route + One Stone:** the river "creature" was a **giant elder Plesiosaurus** ("Nessy"), lashing out over a **needle stuck in its mouth**; freed it (Jaak's *speak with animals*), and it left peacefully — the needle is a **tattooing needle bearing *dominate beast*** (given to [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]], not a relic). Reached **One Stone** — an apparent **Ulfgardian graveyard** (island in a river off a lake, piled stones); saw **humanoids fleeing west** on approach. At the graveyard, **two [[Black Earth]] tribesmen** attacked claiming the relic, then **Sky Pony Ulfgardians** arrived and readied to fight both sides — **three-way standoff cliffhanger; relic not yet recovered.**
- **S69 — Stone Stand taken.** We walked in **on foot** to the Blue Bears' mound — the one flagged *most evil, possibly extinct, avoid*. **[[Var Borgal|Var]] paid respects to the dead on arrival and got a vision back**: this tribe was **ruthless, killing anyone who came near** — and the read was that **the moment we went for the artifact they'd come for us.** So we didn't go in as a party: **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] and [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]] snuck in alone**, used **a spell to locate the relic**, dug, and had **[[Items and References|dimension door]]** queued to leave the burial ground entirely — the plan being that whatever came up would have nothing to follow. **The ghosts of the tribe rose at the graves and came for the diggers.** After several tense rounds they **got the relic out of the ground, grabbed it, and dimension-doored clear.** **Session ends there.** The relic: **a giant's skull, 100 pounds.**
- **S66 — One Stone cleared.** [[Jaak Greye]] broke the language barrier with **Major Image**, showing himself healing the Sky Pony leader; the Ulfgardians read it instantly and **joined us against the [[Black Earth]]** — we **killed the woman, the man escaped**. Jaak cast *tongues*, and the tribesmen took us to **[[Arnzan Vashk]]**, whose illness Jaak diagnosed as needing **greater restoration**. We flew him to **[[Everlund]]** aboard the [[Ignitamis]], **paid 1,600 gp** to a hired mage to cure him — **deliberately not mentioning the relic until afterward** — then returned him to One Stone and asked. **He gave it freely.** The relic is a **magic item: *divination* or *control weather*, once per 7 days.**
