# Gather Ulfgardian Relics

> **Status:** Active
> **Purpose:** Fuel the [[Find Hekaton|Ordening]] spine — relics are **sacrificed to the mound oracles** in exchange for answers, including how to find and reinstate [[Hekaton]].

## Objective

Acquire giant relics from the nine [[Ulfgardian Mounds]] (each holds at least one), then **sacrifice them to the oracles** for the knowledge we need.

> **The oracle mechanic (confirmed):** the mound oracles trade *answers* for *relic sacrifices*. We are **not** collecting relics to keep or wield — we collect them to spend. (Cross-ref: [[Find Hekaton]], [[The Ordening]].)

> **Where things live:** [[Ulfgardian Mounds]] is the reference for *who's at each mound and how to approach them*. **This page tracks what we have and where we're going next** — don't re-list the mound details here.

## Relics In Hand

- **Statue of [[Stronmaus]]** (cloud-giant relic) — payment from [[Harrick Stonequill]] for rune testing, S57. **Eligible for oracle sacrifice — but flagged "last resort, return if able."** Don't burn this one casually.

## Plan Of Attack (Priority Order)

Mound details (tribes, chieftains, approaches, difficulty) are on [[Ulfgardian Mounds]]. Current priority:

1. [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Shining White]] — mostly abandoned, likely easiest
2. [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]] — help dying chieftain **Arnzan Vashk** for the relic
3. [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Raven Rock]] — help **Ojin Voninsdottir** with her mountain problem (**do NOT kill her**)
4. [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]] — investigate the Elk tribe's unknown state

**Avoid unless ready:**
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Stone Stand]] (Blue Bears) — most evil, may be extinct, will eat us if we die
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Great Worm Cavern]] (Warmblood) — brutal, no info
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Beorunna's Well]] (Red Tigers half) — savage, no diplomacy
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Morgur's Mound]] (Thunder Beast) — **Harthulk Hornspea** undefeated, lizard-like

**Worst-case / last resort only:**
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Grandfather Tree]] (Tree Ghost) — **[[Interitus]] asked us not to go**, and it's a respected place that saved him. Pursue *only* if there is no other option; if we ever must, approach with respect and honor the tree guardians.
