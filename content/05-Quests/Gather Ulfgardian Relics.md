# Gather Ulfgardian Relics

> **Status:** Active
> **Purpose:** Likely tied to [[Find Hekaton|reinstating the Ordening]] and/or arming us against the giantsp

## Objective

Acquire relics from the nine [[Ulfgardian Mounds]]. Each mound holds at least one. **[[Ulfgardian Mounds]]** has the full breakdown.

## Relics In Hand

- **Statue of [[Stronmaus]]** (cloud-giant relic) — payment from [[Harrick Stonequill]] for rune testing, S57. **Eligible for oracle sacrifice — but flagged "last resort, return if able"**. Don't burn this one casually.

## Approach Per Mound

See [[Ulfgardian Mounds]]. Some chieftains will trade for help with their problems; others will only respond to violence; one (Grandfather Tree) requires respect and honoring tradition.

## Current Targets / Priorities

- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Shining White]] (Gryphon tribe) — mostly abandoned, likely easiest
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]] (Sky Ponies) — chieftain Arnzan Vashk is sick; help him for the relic
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Raven Rock]] (Black Ravens + Grey Wolves) — Ojin Voninsdottir has a mountain issue; help her
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Grandfather Tree]] (Tree Ghost tribe) — be respectful; the tree saved [[Interitus]]

## Avoid Unless Ready

- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Stone Stand]] (Blue Bears) — most evil, may be extinct, will eat us if we die
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Great Worm Cavern]] (Warmblood) — brutal, no info
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Beorunna's Well]] (Red Tigers half) — savage, no diplomacy
- [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Morgur's Mound]] (Thunder Beast) — Harthulk Hornspea undefeated, lizard-like
