# Northern Beaches Frost Giants

> **Given by:** [[Thogard]] (Gryphon Riders, [[Lords Alliance|Lord's Alliance]])
> **Status:** ✅ **Completed** ([[Session 58 - Var - 2026-06-01|Session 58]])

## Objective

**Frost giants are rampaging farms on the Northern Beaches.** Stop them. Lords' Alliance quest.

## Resolution (Session 58)

- The morning after [[The Shanker Job]], the party set out to deal with the frost giants
- Following the giants' tracks, we spotted (from a distance) a frost giant **herding captives toward a boat**, with **another giant standing on the boat**
- We **defeated the frost giants** and **freed the would-be slaves**
- Reported the win to the **[[Lords Alliance]]**; [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard|Joseph]] and [[Gotrek Runehammer|Gotrek]] earned **more renown**, working toward the **10 renown** they need to rank up

## What We Learned

- These frost giants weren't just raiding farms — they were **taking slaves** by boat, implying an off-shore destination or buyer
- Frost giants are part of the broader giant chaos triggered by [[The Ordening|the Ordening]] breaking

## Open

- Where were the captives being shipped, and **who was the buyer**?
