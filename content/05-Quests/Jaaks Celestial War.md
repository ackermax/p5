# Jaak's Celestial War

> **Status:** Active — slow-burn cosmic backstory **plus an active threat**
> **For:** [[Jaak Greye]]

## ⭐ Answers At Last — What [[Althariel]] Told Us *(S68)*

The first session that **gave Jaak answers instead of questions.** From the spirit of **[[Althariel]]**, the last aasimar of the [[Aasimar Temple]]:

- **Jaak is of the "Thier" blood** *(spelling unconfirmed)* and **came from Celestia.**
- **Jaak carries the [[Blood of the Ancients|blood of the ancients]]** — and so does **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]**.
- **That blood makes a person strong enough to be an avatar — a vessel of the gods.**
- **[[Bhaal]] tried to use a person of ancient blood as a vessel to inhabit.** It has been attempted before.
- **[[Cyric]]** — the cult's god — **was a human who ascended to godhood**, and **killed [[Mystra]]**, ending all magic for a time.
- **🎯 A group of aasimar at the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]] may be able to help Jaak.** The **[[Order of the Gauntlet]] was created by aasimar.**
- **Althariel gave Jaak the [[Items and References|Sword of Heavens]].**

> **The kidnapping now reads differently.** Someone wanting Jaak's blood "for ascension" isn't collecting a reagent — **Jaak's body is the kind of body a god can wear.** So is Estelle's.

## The Old Question — Who Was Jaak Before Age 8?

Jaak has fragmentary visions of his own death in a "great celestial war":
- A **stray attack** pierces him
- He's **knocked from a chariot**
- **Faceless angelic and demonic figures** watch as he falls
- He would **recognize their faces** if he saw them again

He was found at age 8 with no memory but his name.

## The New Threat — The Kidnapping

> **Jaak was stolen by someone, wants ascension, wants Jaak's blood. Angel blood. Celestia.**

This happened recently (during the [[Waterdeep]] arc). Someone took Jaak. They wanted his **Aasimar blood / celestial blood / Mount Celestia blood** for the purpose of **ascension to godhood**.

This is no longer just "Jaak's backstory mystery." Someone is **actively trying to become a god using Jaak's blood**.

## What This Implies

- Jaak's blood has unusual potency — even by Aasimar standards, this kidnapper thinks it can fuel ascension
- The kidnapper presumably **collected some blood** before Jaak escaped (or was rescued — confirm with DM)
- **The age of mortals ([[The Second Sundering]]) is exactly when this kind of ascension attempt becomes possible** — mortals have more sway
- Possible link to the [[Cult of Cyric]] (their old target) — or a *different* operation that learned of Jaak from them
- Possible link to whoever Jaak was before age 8 — were the celestials who "watched him fall" *waiting* to harvest him at the right moment?
