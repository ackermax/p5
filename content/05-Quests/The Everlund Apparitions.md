# The Everlund Apparitions

> **Given by:** [[Krowen Valharrow]] at [[Moongleam Tower]]
> **Status:** **Site cleared — reporting back.** The cause is dead; the reward is uncollected
> **Reward:** something he says is **very valuable** — he would not say what
> **First mentioned:** [[Session 66 - Gotrek - 2026-08-03|Session 66]] | **On site:** [[Session 67 - Var - 2026-08-10|S67]]–[[Session 68 - Var - 2026-08-17|S68]]

## Objective

Investigate **ghostly apparitions northeast of [[Everlund]]** and **find out what is causing them to appear.**

**Answered (S68):** the apparitions are the **aasimar dead** of the site — [[Althariel]] and his kin — killed by **[[The Angel|the Angel]]**, the thing Althariel had imprisoned beneath the mound. Its cage was failing, which is why the hauntings started. **We opened the cell, killed the Angel, and the site went quiet.**

## What Happened

### The four keystones *(S67–S68)*

| # | How we got it | Status |
|---|---------------|--------|
| 1 | Taken off the **teleporting trickster** that ambushed us | ✅ |
| 2 | Found while **searching the space** | ✅ |
| 3 | Won from the **angry ghosts** we had to fight for it *(S68)* | ✅ |
| 4 | Found **in the rubble where we fought the trickster** *(S68)* | ✅ |

**All four went into the altar**, and a **portal opened** — radiant light pouring out of it that **damaged us just to stand near.**

> **We still have all four keystones**, but they are **spent — inert, and can’t be used again.** Their only remaining value is whatever they’d **fetch as goods**. Worth getting them appraised in [[Everlund]].

### Through the portal *(S68)*

- Inside was a **caged one-eyed monster** — **[[The Angel]]** *(the name from its tooltip; it looked nothing like one)*.
- It talked about **needing to cleanse our world**, **broke out of its cage**, and attacked.
- It **summoned flying swords**, and **controlled the plane itself** — something hit us **every round**.
- **A very tough fight.** As it went down it swore **"the Lord" will return to cleanse our world.**
- Past the fight we reached **the place where it had been locked up**, and **[[Althariel]]'s spirit appeared** — the aasimar who **brought it here years ago**, and whom it **killed along with all his kin.** They are the ghosts.
- He **thanked us**, **told us who [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] really are** (see [[Blood of the Ancients]]), **gave Jaak the [[Items and References|Sword of Heavens]]**, and **remade the portal** to send us home.

### Out *(S68)*

- Back to the tomb, **short rest**, then out to the **logging camp to tell them the ghosts have been busted.** They thanked us.
- **Long rest at the [[Ignitamis]]**, then **on the road to [[Everlund]]** — to [[Krowen Valharrow]].

## The Other Dimension

The **mournful ghosts** — the ones we talked down instead of fighting — told us that they **angered something very powerful from a different dimension**, and that it is **sending probes into our plane to consume it.**

Put next to the Angel's dying words about **"the Lord,"** the read is: **the Angel was one of the probes**, and **the thing that sent it is still coming.** Killing it closed a haunting, not a war. See [[The Angel]].
