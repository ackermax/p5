# The Everlund Apparitions

> **Given by:** [[Krowen Valharrow]] at [[Moongleam Tower]]
> **Status:** active — **on site, mid-dungeon.** Two of four keystones in hand
> **Reward:** something he says is **very valuable** — he would not say what
> **First mentioned:** [[Session 66 - Gotrek - 2026-08-03|Session 66]] | **On site:** [[Session 67 - Var - 2026-08-10|Session 67]]

## Objective

Investigate **ghostly apparitions northeast of [[Everlund]]** and **find out what is causing them to appear.**

**As of S67 it is no longer a haunting job.** The apparitions are the **dead of the site itself**, and something that killed them is **breaking back in**. Gathering the **keystones** is now the actual task.

## What We Know

- **The site:** a **round, unnatural, foliage-covered hill** in the forest — see [[Aasimar Temple|the Aasimar Temple]]. Inside it is an **aasimar temple or mausoleum** with a **main chamber and an altar**.
- **The dead are aasimar.** Slim winged humanoids in strange robes, wings decrepit and missing feathers, sunken haunted faces, sad. They appear **in the afternoon**. **[[Altariar]]** is named as *"the last of the aasimar at this base."*
- **Timeline:** apparitions began **a little over a week ago**; the lumberjacks **stopped working about a week ago**. *(Matches the S66 brief that woodcutters "disturbed" something.)*
- **Something killed the aasimar**, and it is now **breaking down the site's defenses to return**. **The last two keystones are what stops it.**
- **[[Jaak Greye]]'s aasimar heritage works on them.** In the main chamber he used his heritage plus pure charisma to make the spectrals disperse rather than fight — **"for now at least."**
- **The altar is active.** After we killed the burning portal, a **second wave of radiant energy** came off the altar in the main chamber. Unexplained.
