# Track the Red Mages

> **Status:** Active — assigned Session 63
> **Giver:** [[Sister Garaele]] ([[Harpers]])
> **For:** [[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]

## Objective

[[Sister Garaele]] tasked Jaak and Estelle with investigating **the Red Mages**, a group whose movements the [[Harpers]] have picked up across the Sword Coast.

## What Garaele Told Us (Session 63)

- The **[[Cult of Cyric|Cult of C]] is too well hidden** to track directly right now — so she's redirecting us onto a group that's *moving in the open*.
- **Red Mages have been spotted in numbers across the Sword Coast.** They are **looking for "curious relics"** and **for important people**.
- **Identifying marks:** **bald, with tattoos on their heads**; **many are necromancers**.
- **The ask:** understand what they're doing — **get papers, documents, and similar items; find their hideouts.**

## Where

**[[Everlund]] — meet at [[Moongleam Tower]].** Someone at the Harper stronghold there wants to speak with Jaak and Estelle; that's the next step on this trail. *(Resolved per Jaak's account — [[Var Borgal|Var]]'s notes had misremembered this as [[Yartar]]. Yartar remains the cult/Cardon convergence, but the Red Mages errand points to Everlund.)*

### S66 — We reached the tower

We arrived at [[Moongleam Tower]] and met **[[Krowen Valharrow]]**, confirmed a **High Harper**. **The Red Mages were never discussed.** Instead he gave us [[The Everlund Apparitions]] and said that **afterward** he wants to speak with **[[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] as a High Harper.** Since that's the same pair [[Sister Garaele]] sent, we assumed **Valharrow was the contact she meant.**

### ⚠️ S69 — That Assumption Did Not Hold

**The audience happened, and it was a promotion.** Valharrow made Jaak and Estelle **[[Harpers|Bright Candles]]** and read them into the **[[Harpers|teleportation-circle network]]** — **and that was the whole audience.** He is not holding a further conversation.

**Across two meetings, he has never once mentioned the Red Mages.**

So the S66 read was wrong, and this trail is **still where [[Sister Garaele]] left it**. Two readings, and we can't tell them apart from here:

1. **Garaele meant somebody else at [[Moongleam Tower]]** — the tower is a Harper stronghold with more than one Harper in it. **We never asked for anyone by name.**
2. **The contact never materialised** — in which case the thing to do is **go back to Garaele in [[Phandalin]] and ask her who she actually meant.**

> **Next concrete step:** ask by name. Either at the tower, or of Garaele. **[[Phandalin]] is about to be one teleport hop from everywhere** — going back to ask her is cheap now.

## ⚠️ S71 — The Red Mages Are Looking For *Us*

The trail stopped needing a contact. In [[Yartar]]:

- **The Red Mages are looking for all of us** — [[Var Borgal|Var]]'s notes hedge **"or at least the [[Harpers]] in our group"** ([[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]]).
- **[[Xolkin]] says they want to talk to us about [[Goldenfields]]** — **and what we were up to there.**
- **Their aim: to weaken the West. The key to that is [[Goldenfields]]**, and **they are gathering information about it.**
- **At Through and Through Investigators, someone told us on the side that we were in danger** — possibly the same threat.
- **We asked [[Laeral Silverhand]] for advice regarding the Red Mages.** *(How we reached her, and what she said, isn't recorded.)*

> **What this changes:** the question is no longer *where do we find them* — they're coming to us. **Goldenfields is the breadbasket, and we already stopped one attack on its crops ([[The Cursed Seeds]]).** Whether that's "what we were up to there" is **not confirmed**.

## ⚠️ S72 — We Chose To Hide, And They Are Walking The Streets

We answered the "talk or trap?" question by **not answering it**: we went into disguise instead.

- **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] left the inn to source disguise kits** specifically **to hide us from the Red Mages**.
- She **bought the supplies from a large shop** — **paid for them** *(confirmed)*, but did it **"sneakily,"** keeping the purchase quiet. *(The shop is unnamed.)* **So there's no theft on our record here** — the only trace is a shopkeeper who remembers the sale. *(How many kits is unrecorded.)*
- **On the way back she almost ran into the Red Mages.** They are **out in the streets of [[Yartar]], in the open, close to our inn.**
- **We got the kits and disguised ourselves well** — the party's read is the disguises held.
- **We then went to work [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin's houses]] in disguise** and **fell into a dungeon**, where we still are.

> **What this tells us:** the Red Mages aren't waiting for a meeting — **they are searching the city for us on foot**, and they got within a street of the inn we sleep in. **The disguises are the only thing between them and us**, and **[[Joanne]]'s inn is a known address** — [[Garambitio]] gave it to us, so it can be given to them.

> **Still undecided: talk or trap.** Hiding buys time; it does not answer the question.
