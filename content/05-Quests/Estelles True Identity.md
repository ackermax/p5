# Estelle's True Identity

> **Status:** Active — personal arc
> **For:** [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]]

## Objective

Discover who Estelle really is — her family, her mother's identity, and the **anonymous benefactor** who has been sending her money since infancy.

## Known Facts

- Mother arrived at [[Cult of Cyric|the monastery]] late at night while in labor
- Mother **died in childbirth** — Estelle survived
- Mother's identity was unknown to the monastery; no family could be traced
- Estelle was buried anonymously in the monastery catacombs
- **Someone has been anonymously sending money** to the monastery for Estelle, ever since
- The clerics hoarded the money instead of using it for her
- Estelle's surname is **"Moonglow Platinum"** — possibly chosen, possibly real, distinctively un-common-sounding

## Open Questions

- Was the mother running from someone? Why a strange monastery, late at night?
- Was the cult **involved** in her mother's situation, or did the mother just stumble in?
- Why did the benefactor pick that monastery? Did they know she'd end up there?
- Why anonymous? Are they hiding from the cult, from the mother's family, from the law?
- **The surname.** "Moonglow Platinum" reads like an *elven house name*, possibly noble. If real, Estelle may have living family.

## Leads To Pursue

- The monastery's records — what little was written down about the mother that night
- Any record of a noble or wealthy elven family by the name "Moonglow Platinum"
- The mother's anonymous grave — could Estelle's affinity with the dead reveal anything?
- The benefactor likely left a paper trail in the money's origin — a banker or courier who knows where it came from

## Connections

- This is tied to [[Cult of Cyric]] — the cult took the money meant for her
- If the cult **knew** who her mother was and lied, this becomes a much darker quest
