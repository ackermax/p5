# Reckoning With The Cult Of Cyric

> **Status:** Active — personal arc shared by [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] and [[Jaak Greye]]; expanding into a major campaign thread
> **For:** Both of them — and arguably for [[Var Borgal]] (magic-cult-purge sympathies) and [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]] (Lord's Alliance interest)

## Objective

Bring down the **[[Cult of Cyric]]** and its leadership. They:
- Raised [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] in deprivation while hoarding her inheritance
- Allied with [[Rulf Cloudfang]] to silence [[Ilin Cotton]] — **and Rulf now leads them, and killed Ilin (S74)**
- **Are working to bring [[Cyric]] back** *(S74)*, using **children's blood** and needing **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]]'s blood to finish** — **and Jaak himself as Cyric's vessel (S75)**
- **Scouted [[Jaak Greye]] (and others) for sacrifice** — Jaak was a high-value target
- **Kidnapped Jaak for his blood** *(confirmed)* — see [[Jaaks Celestial War]]
- Have been hunting both since the heist
- Ran the [[The Death Knight Ritual]] in [[Kryptgarden Forest]] (party stopped this cell)
- Are *likely* the cult [[Other Locations|Javen Tarmikos]] wants destroyed at [[Helm's Hold]]

## Session 59 — The Cult Resurfaces At Yartar

Underworld intel from the [[Council of the Underworld]] meeting:
- The **Cult of C has "risen from the ashes,"** is gathering **merchandise and poisons**, is **"trying something big,"** and is **converging on [[Yartar]].**
- **[[Cardon Malefic]] is meeting the Cult of C at Yartar in ~3 days** — escorted by [[Black Network|Zhentarim]] and [[Jarlaxle]]. The cult now has a confirmed dealing with a powerful, anti-magic-wielding outsider.
- This **stacks on [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin's lead]]** (he's chasing the cult to Yartar, calls it "end of the world stuff"). **Yartar is the next venue for this whole arc.**

## Session 63 — Cult Gone to Ground; Chase the Red Mages Instead

Back in [[Phandalin]], [[Sister Garaele]] told Jaak & Estelle the **Cult of C is now too well hidden to track directly.** She redirected them onto **[[Track the Red Mages|the Red Mages]]** — bald, head-tattooed necromancers massing across the Sword Coast, hunting "curious relics" and important people — as a group that *is* moving in the open. Whether the Red Mages are working with the cult or on their own hunt is unknown; the Red Mage trail may be the current back-door to this arc.

## Session 73 — We Found A Cult Site, Under Yartar

The dungeon under the house on [[Find Ilin Cotton|Ilin's list]] (S72) **is the cult's.**

- **A torture room held [[Ilin Cotton]]**, the [[Harpers|Harper]] who'd been investigating them. **Freed and healed.**
- **A red-hooded mage** we judged **Cult of Cyric** was down there with **[[Doctor Hadol]]**, a contractor who **works for [[Cardon Malefic]]**. **Hadol escaped; the mage was knocked out and interrogated, gave us nothing, and was killed when he tried to attack [[Var Borgal|Var]] during the dealmaking.**
- **We killed "hell creatures" and several more cult members** in the rooms beyond.
- **Ilin believes the cult has brought children here.** **Confirmed: the Exquisites circus in [[Waterdeep]] was one of the cult's child-kidnapping operations** ([[The Exquisites Investigation]]) — **we broke one of their supply lines without knowing it.**
- **Ilin had fallen through the same carpet trap we did**, and the cult **captured and interrogated him.**

> **Garaele said in S63 that the cult was too well hidden to track. We fell into one of their sites through a carpet.** This is **the first hard contact with the cult's [[Yartar]] operation**, and **the first time we've seen Cardon's deal with them in practice.**

## ⭐ Session 74 — The Leader, The Goal, And What They Wanted With Us

- **Flesh golems were burning bodies** east of where we were. **Destroyed.** Then **more cultists** to the north.
- **A book** showed that **the young people brought here had their blood extracted for some sort of ritual.** **The oldest is 15.**
- **At the top of the dungeon: [[Rulf Cloudfang|Rulf]], the cult's current leader.**
- **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]] was being groomed to be the next leader of the cult.**
- **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] was only good for his blood** — to **finish whatever ritual they're working towards.**
- **The goal: bringing [[Cyric]] back.**
- **We thought we killed Rulf;** his **body dissipated** when Jaak checked it, and **he stabbed [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]] to death** as he vanished into the ether.
- **Ilin's belongings:** a **picture of [[The Traveling Wilmelons|Jaak's troupe]]** and **his notes about the cult and his investigation.** We're carrying his body and **searching the rest of the dungeon for children still alive.**

> **This is the biggest single reveal of the cult arc.** We have its **leader**, its **goal**, and **why it wanted Estelle and Jaak** — and the two people it wanted most are **standing in its dungeon.** See [[Blood of the Ancients]] for what Althariel said that blood can do.

## ⭐ Session 75 — The Mother, A Death, And Cardon's Papers

- **[[Gotrek Runehammer|Gotrek]] threw [[Ilin Cotton|Ilin]]'s body over his shoulder** and we went looking for the children.
- **A prison block, guarded by the anti-magic golem [[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] gave the cult** — it **seemed bound to the space with some sort of mission.** **We didn't want the fight and backed out.**
- **A large room, and an older woman waiting: the cult's "mother."** **She seemed to have affection for [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]].** **Estelle would be the leader, and [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] was being set up to be [[Cyric]]'s vessel** *(confirmed)*.
- **Fight, then an ambush by more cultists.** **We barely made it out alive — Estelle died as she killed the last enemy.** **[[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard|Sir Joseph]] took the *revivify* scroll she was carrying and, with Var walking him through it from one of his necromancy books, brought her back.**
- **Var took papers off the mother's corpse** and read them while we hid for a short rest:
  - **Cardon and [[Doctor Hadol]] gave the cult the anti-magic golem as a "gift"** to help **expand the cult's reach.**
  - **Cardon was looking for information on how [[Cyric]] became a god.** **Next clue: [[The Netherese|the Netherese]]** — **Karsus** and ***Karsus' Avatar***, which **failed and caused magic to leave the world for a time.**
  - **Cardon is here with mercenaries of [[Jarlaxle|Jarlaxle Baenre]].**

> **Cardon isn't just doing business with the cult — he's arming it**, and **studying the one thing the cult's god did that no one else managed: becoming a god.** The cult wants **Cyric back**; **Cardon wants to know how Cyric got there.** **Those may not be the same goal.**
