# Vonindod — The Adamantine Colossus

> Fire giant superweapon. Once destroyed. Possibly being rebuilt.

## History

The **fire giants gathered adamantine** and built a colossus **80 feet tall** to fight dragons. The plan worked — **too well**. Vonindod began to **destroy everything**, indiscriminately. The giant kinds **banded together** to defeat it.

## The Threat Now

**Fire giants ARE rebuilding it — confirmed.** Duke Zalto is leading the effort. We recovered:
- **The rod** (from Nightstone) — **points to Vonindod**
- **The adamantine ring** — also from Zalto's people at Nightstone
- **Duke Zalto kept orcs as slaves** to harvest adamantine

Implications:
- A rebuild explains unusual fire-giant activity
- Could be connected to **the salamander rumor / Behir** [[Harshnag]] was investigating (fire monstrosity in the region) — see [[Harshnags Hunt]]
- Is one of the levers tied to the collapse of [[The Ordening]] — Zalto attempting to climb the broken hierarchy

## Why The Rod Matters

If the rod points to Vonindod, **we have a tracker for an apocalyptic adamantine colossus.** This is potentially the most decisive single intelligence asset the party currently holds. The right move depends on whether we want to:
- Confirm location and report to [[Lords Alliance|Lord's Alliance]] / [[Klauth]]
- Strike preemptively
- Use the rod as bait to draw Zalto out
