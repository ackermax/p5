# The Black Network

> **Also known as:** **Zhentarim** (canonical name) — the Black Network is the same organization.
> Crime syndicate / mercenary guild. **Sworn enemies of the [[Harpers]].**

## Identification

The Zhentarim are one of the major factions of the Sword Coast — a mercenary network with a respectable public face and a crime-syndicate operational core. Our source notes captured the name in several spellings (Zentherin, Zantherin, Zantarin) before we standardized to **Zhentarim**. All those spellings refer to this organization.

## What They've Done To Us

- **Attacked Nightstone** — pretended to be allies, then attacked Nightstone Castle. We convinced them to stop fighting.
- **Now dominate [[Other Locations|Womford]]** — once a merchant hub; people are disappearing there; "Womford bat" rumors among survivors in Silverymoon
- **Hidden caches** — they hide things in trees; we found one with a flying snake inside
- **High-level infiltration suspected** — "Lord's Alliance has bad people high up?" per the notes

## Known Operations / Associates

- **The "Flying Snakes"** — a Zhentarim hallmark; the snake we found in the cached tree was one of theirs
- Active in the **Sword Coast underground**, especially [[Waterdeep]]'s lower tiers

## Why They Matter

- Direct enemies of the [[Harpers]], which means **direct enemies of [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] and [[Jaak Greye]]** by extension
- Possibly infiltrating the [[Lords Alliance|Lord's Alliance]] — if true, [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]] is exposed
- May be coordinating with — or competing with — the [[Cult of the Dragon]] and [[Cult of Cyric]] on opportunistic basis
