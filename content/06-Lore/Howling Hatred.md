# Howling Hatred

> Another cult active on the Sword Coast. **Going around looking for allies.**

## What We Know

- Active in the region
- **Soliciting alliances** with other powers — likely a sign of weakness, or of a coordinated multi-cult plan
- Canonically (Forgotten Realms): the **Cult of the Howling Hatred** is one of the four Elemental Evil cults, devoted to **Yan-C-Bin**, prince of evil air. They fly, they ambush from the sky.

## Strategic Implications

The Sword Coast right now has at least these cults active simultaneously:
- [[Cult of the Dragon]] (largest, hidden, "grand thing")
- [[Cult of Cyric]] (active, Estelle/Jaak personal arc)
- **Howling Hatred** (Elemental Evil — air)
- *Possibly:* the **fake servants of [[Bahamut]]** we fought in [[Other Locations|Kryptgarden Forest]]

If even two of these are coordinating, the Sword Coast is in worse shape than the [[Lords Alliance|Lord's Alliance]] thinks. **A cult looking for allies is the kind of cult that finds them.**
