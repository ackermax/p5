# Stone Giant Factions

> The two ways stone giants commune with **aman** — and the war between them. Directly relevant to [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s runes.
>
> **Naming note:** Two name-pairs are in use for the same two traditions:
> - **Rockspeakers** / **Stoneshapers** — the stone-speaking tradition
> - **Dreamwalkers** / **Dreamweavers** — the dream-peering tradition
>
> The vault uses both. Search and links resolve to this note via aliases.

## The Division

[[Skoraeus Stonebones]] — **demigod, son of Amand, not on this plane** — divided the means by which stone giants would connect to aman, creating two traditions. Those who **represent Skoraeus's will** are called **Danes**.

### Rockspeakers / Stoneshapers
- Speak **directly into the stones** / shape the stones with their will
- Dwarves can do something similar (stonecunning is a faint echo)
- More literal, more grounded
- **[[Thane Kayalithica]]** is from the **Stoneshape clan** — and is the most dangerous Rockspeaker currently active
- [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s brother [[Eldrin]] was hunting Kayalithica

### Dreamwalkers / Dreamweavers
- Peer **not into the earth, but into the dreams of the land**
- More mystical, more interpretive
- [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s runes carry the **Dreamwalker symbol**
- The leader of the cleft where Gotrek was raised was regarded as a Dreamwalker — **possibly a Dane**

## The War

The division between the two traditions triggered a war. The two stone giant factions **split into clans**. The conflict shaped the modern stone giant tradition. It is the oldest stone giant schism.

## The Current Threat — Thane Kayalithica (Session 56)

**[[Thane Kayalithica]]** is a powerful **Stoneshaper** trying to **unite both sides of the schism** — by bringing the **Dreamweavers under her command**.

The method she has chosen is **vile**: she is **trying to destroy the dream world itself**. If she succeeds:

- **No mortal will be able to dream**
- **All Dreamweavers could die in the process** (their tradition relies on the dreamrealm)

She perceives differently than other Stoneshapers — sees things others can't, has an unusually deep connection to stone. **She must be stopped.**

## Why This Matters For Gotrek

[[Gotrek Runehammer]] carries Dreamwalker runes. His brother [[Eldrin]] (now confirmed alive, in hiding) hunted a Stoneshaper (Kayalithica). Originally framed as "the brothers are on opposite sides of the oldest schism" — but now Eldrin is the one acting against the *Stoneshaper* threat, putting them effectively on the *same* side against Kayalithica.

Gotrek needs to **return to the [[Cradle of the Stonegiants]]** to act against Kayalithica's plan.

[[Stonadar]] belonged to one of these two clans — confirming which one is a near-term research priority. [[Harrick Stonequill]] is the best-positioned scholar.
