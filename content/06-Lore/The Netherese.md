# The Netherese

> **An ancient civilization — a race of incredible magic.** Everything we know comes from **papers [[Var Borgal|Var]] took off the corpse of the [[Cult of Cyric|cult]]'s "mother"** in the dungeon under [[Yartar]] — [[Session 75 - Var - 2026-10-19|Session 75]]. **The papers are about [[Cardon Malefic]]'s research**, so this is *his* trail we're reading.

## What The Papers Say *(S75)*

- **[[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] was looking for information on how [[Cyric]] became a god.**
- **The next clue is with the Netherese** — the ancient civilization of the incredible magic race.
- **Karsus**, who was part of this people, **wanted to be the god of magic.**
- He **created a spell called *Karsus' Avatar*** that would make him **the god of magic**.
- **It failed**, and **caused magic to leave the world for a time.**

## Why It Matters

- **Cyric climbed from mortal to god** ([[Althariel]], S68) — and **killed [[Mystra]]**, the goddess of magic, after which **all magic ceased to exist.** **Karsus tried to *become* the god of magic**, and **magic left the world.** Two stories, **one shape**: a mortal reaches for the godhood behind magic, and **magic breaks.**
- **This is what [[Cardon Malefic|Cardon]] is studying** — the man who raised [[Var Borgal|Var]] to run an inquisition that would **purge magic users**, who walks inside an **anti-magic field**, and whose people build **[[Doctor Hadol|technology to rival magic]]**.
- [[Var Borgal|Var]]'s questions, as he wrote them *(questions, not answers)*:
  - **Is Cardon trying to follow in Cyric's footsteps** — kill **the god behind the Weave** ([[Mystra]]) and **become a god himself**? **Does he need to kill Mystra to do that?**
  - **Or is he looking to replicate it without the godhood part?**

## Open Questions

- **Who or where are the Netherese now?** Var's notes call them **ancient**. Is there anything left to find — ruins, texts, survivors?
- **What exactly is the "clue" Cardon expects to find with them?**
- **How did magic come back** — after Karsus, and after Mystra's death? *(See [[Mystra]].)*
- **Does Cardon know about the [[Blood of the Ancients|blood of the ancients]]?** The cult he's backing wanted [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] for his blood.
