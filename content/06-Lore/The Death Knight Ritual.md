# The Death Knight Ritual

> Necromancer's journal recovered after the party killed him in [[Kryptgarden Forest]]. Cult of Cyric operation — confirmed by content. **The party stopped this before completion.**

## The Necromancer's Plan

> Verbatim from the journal — full text preserved below for evidentiary purposes.

The necromancer aimed to forge a **Death Knight** — not the usual cheap kind, but one anchored by hundreds of bound souls. To do this he needed:
- A **body worthy** — battle-hardened, not a common corpse
- **Souls in bulk** — dozens to hundreds, suppressed and stripped of identity, woven into a single obedient force
- **A lair beyond scrutiny** — a forgotten mausoleum in [[Kryptgarden Forest]]
- **A steady supply of victims** — the **Triboar–Red Larch road** corridor (Dessarin road traffic)

## Where We Found It

The body was already chosen — "a strong subject... battle hardened, fit for my goal" — name redacted/smudged in the journal. Bodies harvested for bones. Souls drawn out at the moment of fear and pain (because terror strengthens them). The Death Knight body was being preserved with oils and sigils against rot.

By the time we arrived, **the ritual was at the "near completion" stage**:
- Body already moved once
- Souls had merged into a single muted chorus
- Only "a last harvest" needed for final energy
- "I can feel the Death Knight waiting, restrained only by the final words I have yet to speak"

[[Interitus]] asked the party to kill this necromancer. We did — **before even meeting Interitus**. Convenient timing.

## Why It Matters Now

- **Travelers on the Triboar–Red Larch road were being ambushed for months.** This is real-world bad — many missing people unaccounted for.
- The bound souls in the lattice — *where do they go now that the ritual is stopped?* Did they release? Are they still bound somewhere?
- The body — was it destroyed? Is the proto-Death Knight still preservable?
- The smudged name of "the strong subject" the necromancer found — **who was he**?
- The journal links the operation back to the [[Cult of Cyric]] doctrinally (souls, sacrifice, the focus on terror and obedience all match)
- **Other cells of this cult are likely doing the same thing elsewhere.** This was one ritual; the playbook can be reused.

## Full Journal Text

### Entry I — Resolution
A Death Knight. A weapon of flesh and soul, bound not by rituals of the common necromancer but by the very essence of war itself. No mere corpse will serve. No lone soul will be sufficient. I must forge something greater, something that endures, something that commands fear and discipline.

This will require more than the simple raising of the dead. It will require souls—many souls—and a body that will not reject the burden.

I have decided. I will raise a Death Knight.

### Entry II — The Plan
To create a Death Knight, one must have a body worthy of it. Not the decayed remains of some common soldier, but the corpse of one who has known violence, whose muscles and bones were shaped by battle and survival. Even then, the body alone will fail unless sustained—anchored—by something greater.

Souls must serve as that anchor. Not one, nor a handful. Dozens. Hundreds. Bound together, suppressed, stripped of identity, and woven into a single, obedient force. The will must be broken before it can be shaped.

I will require a place to work unseen, far from scrutiny, where the screams of the living and the whispers of the dead will go unheard.

### Entry III — The Lair
I have found such a place deep within the [[Kryptgarden Forest]]—a forgotten mausoleum, ancient and neglected, its purpose long abandoned. The stone still stands firm, and the earth beneath it is heavy with death.

My undead servants have labored without rest. They have cleared collapsed corridors, reinforced chambers with bone and mortar, and sealed entrances that once welcomed mourners. The air is thick with death, and the walls remember every corpse laid within them.

I also found a strong subject while wandering, he was <smudges>, the subject is battle hardened, fit for my goal.

### Entry IV — The Harvest
The roads between [[Other Locations|Triboar]] and [[Other Locations|Red Larch]] provide all that I require. Travelers, messengers, hunters, merchants—those who wander the Dessarin roads between those towns. My servants ambush them in silence, binding them and dragging them back to the lair while they still breathe. Here, I observe. I test. I learn.

When they are no longer useful, I take what I need. Their souls are torn free at the height of fear and pain, stronger for the terror that precedes death. Their bodies are repurposed as the ritual demands.

### Entry V — The Ritual
Bodies are harvested for bones. The souls are drawn from their vessels and siphoned into prepared conduits. The air vibrates with their resistance, their anguish, their desperation.

Some souls fight harder than others. Those are the most valuable. I break them slowly, carefully, forcing their essence into the growing lattice that will form the Death Knight's core. Their suffering strengthens the bond.

The host body is treated relentlessly. Oils preserve the flesh. Sigils reinforce the bone. Necromantic force replaces what decay would claim. The corpse no longer rots. It endures.

### Entry VI — Near Completion
The gathering of energy is almost complete.

It already moved once, but the energy is still not enough. The souls have merged, their voices blending into a single, muted chorus beneath my control. The body accepts them. It no longer rejects the burden.

The ritual is nearly complete. I only need a last harvest so I'll have enough energy and then I can proceed with the last convergence of soul and flesh, the sealing of will.

I can feel the Death Knight waiting, restrained only by the final words I have yet to speak.

Soon, I'll have a power beyond imagining.
