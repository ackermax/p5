# Cult of the Dragon

> Rising power on the Sword Coast. Hidden, deliberate, preparing something.

## Current Status

- **Gaining power on the Sword Coast**
- **Very well hidden** — as if preparing something **grand**
- **Now publicly named in the press** ([[The Waterdeep Wazoo Issue 496|Wazoo]] reports them as *the* prominent cult)
- **Recruiting in isolated settlements and among desperate populations**
- **Specifically targeting children and the young/impressionable** — "recruiters often prey upon the young and impressionable"
- **Seeking ancient dragons and dark powers**
- **[[Tiamat]] is active again** per the same intel
- **Bands of dragons** emerging — see [[Doom of the Desert]]
- **[[Other Locations|Elturel]] vanished into Avernus** — the most extreme manifestation of cosmic-evil activity on the Sword Coast in living memory
- Connected to **[[Other Locations|Alturel]]** (same town, alternative spelling in our notes) — sank into **Avernus**

## Active Threats Possibly Linked

- **The "we are watching" messenger** with a **scar on his mouth** and a **black sun symbol** — could be Cult of the Dragon, could be something else
- **[[The Cult in Helms Hold|The cult in Helm's Hold]]** — same cult? Probably same as [[Cult of Cyric]] instead, given Estelle/Jaak's story
- **[[Cult of Cyric]]** — could be a Cult of the Dragon cell, or one of the smaller cults absorbed under its umbrella
- **[[Howling Hatred]]** — also actively recruiting allies; could be coordinating
- **The spell outbreak** on the road from [[Helm's Hold]] to [[Thundertree]]
- **[[Doom of the Desert]]** — among the mightiest dragons still active in Faerûn, recently stirred from her lair

## Why It Matters Now

A second major antagonistic force on the Sword Coast **at the same time as [[The Ordening]] is collapsing** is too much to be coincidence. Likely either:
- The Cult is **exploiting** the giant chaos
- The Cult **caused or accelerated** the giant chaos (e.g., is [[Hekaton]] their prisoner?)
- Both crises share a deeper cause
