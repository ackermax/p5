# The Second Sundering

> Cosmological event. The reason the gods feel further away. **Average people don't know this happened.**

## What Happened

Something happened to the gods. **Less connection with the gods overall.** New rules for divine power. Mortals have more sway over the world than before. The **age of mortals has begun.**

It's quiet, not catastrophic. No fireballs in the sky. Most ordinary people have no idea this event occurred — they may have noticed clerics complain about reduced communion, but few would name it as "the Sundering."

## Why It Matters For This Campaign

- **The collapse of [[The Ordening]]** happens during the age of mortals — these may not be unrelated. The cosmic hierarchies (giant *and* divine) are loosening at once.
- **[[Cult of the Dragon]] active again, [[Tiamat]] active again, bands of dragons** — divine-tier evils stirring as if they sense an opening
- **[[Cult of Cyric]] ascendant** — Cyric is the Dark Sun, god of murder and lies; if the gods are weaker, his cult's earthly operations may matter more
- **[[Cult of the Dragon|The Cult of the Dragon's "grand thing"]]** is plausibly an attempt to capitalize on divine weakness
- **[[Jaaks Celestial War|Jaak's kidnapper "wants ascension"]]** — *ascension to godhood* is something a mortal might actually attempt during an age of mortals. The Second Sundering is the doctrinal opening for that.

## Other Crises Of The Age

- **[[Cult of the Dragon]] active again**
- **[[Tiamat]] active again**, bands of dragons emerging
- **Troubles in [[Other Locations|Elturel]]** — and ultimately the entire town **vanished**, sunk into **Avernus** per other notes
- **Troubles with the Thai theocracy** (Thay — the Red Wizards)
- **[[Lords Alliance|Lord's Alliance]] and [[Other Locations|Order of the Gauntlet]] have their hands full**
- **Things look bleak**

## Strategic Implications

We are operating during one of the worst stretches in Faerûnian history in recent memory. **Multiple cults, multiple cosmic threats, weakened divine response, fractured giant hierarchy, and a major fiendish event already occurring in Elturel.** Whoever the party is doing the big lifting for — [[Klauth]], [[Harshnag]], the [[Harpers]] — knows the stakes are that high.
