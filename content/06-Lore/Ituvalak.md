# Ituvalak

> **Stub** — one mention, S66. **Filed as a faction, but that's a working guess, not established.** Expand when we learn more.

**[[Arnzan Vashk]] told us about Ituvalak** during the S66 visit to [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]]. The only thing recorded about it is the phrase:

> **"Children of the forest"**

That is the entire note. Whether Ituvalak is **a people, a place, a spirit, or a title** is not established, and neither is why Vashk raised it or what he wanted us to do about it.

**Working assumption: it's a faction** — a group of some kind, given "children of the forest" reads as a people. **This is a guess made to file the note, not something we were told.** If it turns out to be a place, this moves to `04-Locations`.

## Open Questions

- **What is it?** People, place, or something else? *(Filed as a faction on a hunch.)*
