# The Ordening

> The divine hierarchy of giants, holding the six kinds in their proper places. **Currently broken.**

## The Hierarchy (Highest → Lowest)

1. **Storm Giants** — top of the order. Led by [[Hekaton]] from the **Maelstrom**.
2. **Cloud Giants** — follow [[Memnor]] ("Masked Ones") or [[Stronmaus]] (sky/joy)
3. **Fire Giants** — created [[Vonindod]] long ago; may be rebuilding
4. **Frost Giants** — see [[Harshnag]] (atypical), and the [[Northern Beaches Frost Giants|Northern Beaches raids]]
5. **Stone Giants** — divided by [[Skoraeus Stonebones]] into [[Stone Giant Factions|Rockspeakers and Dreamwalkers]]
6. **Hill Giants** — lowest; [[Guh]] at [[Grudd Haug]] is rallying them

## Why It Matters

The Ordening was **held together by [[Hekaton]]**, but **Hekaton is missing**. With him gone, the Ordening is no more. **This is the cause of the giant attacks** plaguing the Sword Coast — each kind of giant is rising in its own way, trying to claim a higher rung than the order assigned them.

## Our Job

Find Hekaton. Reinstate him. End the war. See [[Find Hekaton]].

## Related History

- **[[Gurt]]** — once one of the strongest giants ever; a frost giant defeated by humans. Sworn enemy of **Ulfguard**. They killed each other in final combat, and **Ulfguard became the god of strength**.
- **[[Vonindod]]** — the adamantine colossus the fire giants built to fight dragons. It went wrong. Giants banded together to destroy it. Fire giants **may be rebuilding it**.
- **[[Ulfgardian Mounds|Ulfgardians]]** — barbarian clans descended from Ulfguard's followers. Watch over against the giants.
