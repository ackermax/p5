# The Doom Raiders

> A **retired adventuring band** that the [[Harpers]] consider were "not decent people." Built ties to the **[[Black Network|Zhentarim]]** before disbanding.

## What We Know

- **Retired** — no longer adventuring together
- **Members were "not decent people"** — per Harper assessment
- **Created ties to the [[Black Network|Zhentarim]]** during or after their adventuring career
- **[[Skeemo Weirdbottle]] is a former member** — currently runs Weirdbottle Concoctions in [[Waterdeep]]
- **The [[Harpers]] are watching them** — believe they are still operating quietly, especially Skeemo

## Why They Matter

- **A retired adventuring band of high-level operators** still in the Sword Coast is itself a high-impact resource — they have contacts, gear, capabilities, and history
- **Their ties to the Zhentarim** mean any operation against the Black Network may have to navigate them
- **At least one (Skeemo) is also working with the [[Cult of Cyric]]** — implying the Doom Raiders' contacts are now serving multiple bad actors

## Open Questions

- Who are the other former Doom Raiders?
- Where are they now?
- Was the group originally Zhentarim-aligned, or did they slide into it after retirement?
- Are they coordinating, or has each member gone solo with their old contacts?
