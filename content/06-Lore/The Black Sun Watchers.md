# The Black Sun Watchers

> Mysterious thread. One messenger. One threat. No name yet.

## What We Know

A messenger delivered a message saying **"we are watching"** — accompanied by a **black sun symbol**. The messenger had a **scar on his mouth**.

## Working Theories

### Tier 1 — Strong Candidates
- **[[Cult of the Dragon]]** — possible, but their style is usually more cloaked
- **[[Cult of Cyric]]** — has motive (hunting Estelle and Jaak); "black sun" isn't documented in their iconography but cults stack symbols
- **[[The Beliard Mystery|The face-eater handler]]** — the note recovered ("the girl will be enough... faces to go around") is signed by *somebody* who runs a network. They are *literally* watching. Could be the same operation.

### Tier 2 — Worth Considering
- **[[Cardon Malefic]]'s inquisition** — chasing [[Var Borgal]]. Black sun doesn't fit his political style though.
- **[[Black Network]]** — Zhentarim watch everybody; the message style fits their intimidation pattern
- **The cloaked glowing man** who burned down [[Var Borgal]]'s family and later awakened his sorcerous powers
- **Whoever wrote [[Gotrek Runehammer]]'s [[Stone Giant Factions|Dreamwalker]] runes** — clearly watching long-term
- **Whoever kidnapped [[Jaak Greye]] for [[Jaaks Celestial War|ascension blood]]** — they're *still watching* Jaak now

### Tier 3 — Cosmic Long Shots
- **[[Jaaks Celestial War|Jaak's faceless watchers]]** — angels and demons who watched him die in the celestial war. They're *literally watchers*. If they exist in this world, "we are watching" is exactly what they'd say. The black sun is also a real eclipse / cosmic-judgment symbol.

## What To Watch For

- The **black sun symbol** — anywhere it appears
- Scar-mouth messengers
- Anyone using the phrase "we are watching"
- Anyone watching, period
