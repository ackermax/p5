# The Red Brands

> **Status:** Defeated (party arc — Phandalin chapter)
> Mercenary gang that ran [[Phandalin]] before the party arrived.

## What They Were

- 15-20 strong, mostly **mercenaries**
- Led by **[[Glasstaff]]** (the wizard [[Iarno Albrek]] under an alias)
- Based out of **Tresendar Manor**, south of town
- Stayed at the **Sleeping Giant Tavern**
- Ran a protection racket — shaking down [[Phandalin]] residents
- **Garret** was a frontline lieutenant who refused to admit he was the leader
- **[[Halia Thorton]]** was the *only* notable resident not intimidated by them

## The Bigger Picture

- **Answered to "the Spyder"** — a note from the Spyder was on [[Glasstaff]]'s desk
- The Spyder is **[[Nezznar]]** — drow, of the [[Society of the Drow|Loyalists of Lolth]]
- Their drow connections were investigating beneath Phandalin (Tresendar Manor's basement led down and to the side; rot smells; sword buried in orchard)

## Outcome

Defeated and dispersed. Glasstaff (Iarno Albrek) was *the* missing person [[Sildar Hallwinter]] had been sent to find. Tresendar Manor cleared. **[[Mirna]]** was rescued from the manor — she's from [[Thundertree]], looking for an heirloom (alchemist's house, plague).

## Loose Ends

- **The orchard sword and the rot smell beneath Tresendar Manor** — was this fully investigated? The connection to the drow under-passages and [[Old Owl Well]]'s Underdark link is suggestive.
- **[[Mirna]]'s heirloom** in [[Thundertree]] — quest still open
- Did all 15-20 Red Brand mercenaries actually get accounted for, or did some flee?
