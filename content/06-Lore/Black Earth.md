# Black Earth

> **Stub** — encountered S65; expand as we learn more.

A rival group hunting Ulfgardian relics. First met at **[[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]]** (S65): **two Black Earth members** were at the graveyard and **attacked the party**, claiming the **relic was theirs** — the fight kicked off after [[Jaak Greye]] miscalled them "Black Ground." A round in, **Sky Pony Ulfgardians** arrived and squared up too, leaving a **three-way standoff** (unresolved).

Possibly connected: a band of **humanoids seen fleeing west** from One Stone as the party approached *(unconfirmed)*.

## S66 — The Fight Resolved

Once [[Jaak Greye]] convinced the **Sky Ponies** we were healers, the Ulfgardians **fought the Black Earth alongside us**. We **killed the woman; the man escaped.** So the Sky Ponies are clearly *not* allied with them — the Black Earth are rivals to the mound tribes as well as to us.

**[[Jaak Greye]] records them as "cultists"** rather than a tribe. *(Gotrek's notes just call them Black Earth; the "cult" reading is Jaak's — unconfirmed either way.)*

## Open Questions

- Who are they and what do they want the relic for? **Tribe, or cult?** Jaak calls them cultists.
- **The man who escaped** — he knows our faces and knows we took the One Stone relic. Where did he go, and who does he report to?
- Are the fleeing humanoids the same group?
- They claimed the relic **was theirs**. On what basis?
