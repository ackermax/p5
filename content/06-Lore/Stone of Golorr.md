# Stone of Golorr

> An object that **[[Xanathars Guild|Xanathar]] is hunting** — and the real reason the beholder wants **[[Commander Hawkwinter]]** dead. **Learned from [[Laeral Silverhand]], [[Session 62 - Var - 2026-06-29|Session 62]].**

## What We Know

- **Xanathar wants the Stone of Golorr.** This is what's behind the contract on [[Commander Hawkwinter]]'s life (S59: Xanathar wanted Hawkwinter dead for "stealing from him" / being "in his way").
- **It ties back to [[Commander Hawkwinter]]** — most likely the thing Hawkwinter has, took, or is keeping out of Xanathar's reach.
- We do **not** have it, and don't yet know **what it does** or **where it is now**.

## Open Questions

- **What is the Stone, and what does it do?** Why is a beholder crime-lord willing to offer a Council-rank promotion to kill for it?
- **Does [[Commander Hawkwinter]] physically hold it?** Or merely know where it is / stand between Xanathar and it?
- Does this intersect any of our other [[Waterdeep]] threads — the [[Council of the Underworld]], [[Jarlaxle]]'s search, [[Force Grey]]?
