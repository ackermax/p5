# The Waterdeep Wazoo — Issue 496

> **Tagline:** "The Least Questionable News Source in the Sword Coast"
> **Special Edition:** Rumblings in the North
> **Price:** 2 cp

![[Waterdeep Wazoo Issue 496.png]]

## Front Page Articles

### Giants on the Move — Is the Ordning Broken?

> Reports of giant attacks across the Sword Coast have increased dramatically in recent months. Settlements across the **Dessarin Valley**, including **[[Goldenfields]], [[Other Locations|Triboar]], and [[Other Locations|Nightstone]]**, have all suffered attacks from various giant clans.
>
> Scholars now speculate that the ancient hierarchy of giantkind — known as **[[The Ordening|the Ordning]]** — may have been broken.
>
> *"If the Ordning truly has fallen,"* explains giant-lore specialist **[[Harrick Stonequill]]**, *"then every giant lord will seek to prove dominance. We would see giants moving across the land, raiding settlements, seeking relics, wealth, or glory."*
>
> Travelers are advised to remain vigilant, particularly in rural areas and along open roads.

**This is now public knowledge.** Harrick is on record. The party can speak openly about the Ordning to anyone reading the Wazoo.

### Mysterious Adventurers Defend the North

> An unnamed band of adventurers has been sighted across the Dessarin Valley aiding towns under attack. The group reportedly helped defend [[Goldenfields]] from a giant assault and later **assisted the people of [[Other Locations|Triboar]] in repelling a Fire Giant attack**, **though many lives were lost in the battle**. The same adventurers were also seen near [[Other Locations|Nightstone]], where they rescued villagers kidnapped by goblins following the devastation left by a Cloud Giant raid. Though the group bears no banner or known name, tales of their deeds are quickly spreading across the North.

**This is us** — [[The Phandalin Five]], reported here before we'd adopted the name. Worth noting:
- Our **Triboar Fire Giant fight is now in the public record** with a "many lives lost" framing — somber, not heroic. **Confirmed by DM (2026-05-29)** — and it's where we recovered the **adamantine ring** from the Fire Giants (see [[Vonindod]], [[Items and References]]).
- The Wazoo had to describe us as "unnamed" because we hadn't picked a name yet. Future editions will be able to use **The Phandalin Five** by name.

### Giant Castles Seen in the Sky

> Multiple travelers report sightings of **massive castles drifting among the clouds** across the North. Officials advise citizens to watch the skies and report unusual cloud formations or shadowy structures drifting overhead. *Floating fortresses spotted over the Sword Coast. Beware the clouds!*

These are the **cloud giant castles** — [[Mulara and Nimbolo]]'s flying castle, [[Zephyros]]'s Cloud Spire, and presumably **[[Countess Sansuri]]'s castle** as well.

### Elturel in Flames!

> Witnesses claim **the great city has been seen engulfed in fire**, though details remain scarce and conflicting. Whether the disaster was caused by war, magic, or something darker remains unknown. *City engulfed in fire, Disaster in the south!*

Our earlier intel said **Elturel/Alturel completely disappeared, sunk into Avernus**. The Wazoo's "engulfed in fire" framing is different — the public may not know *yet* that it's a planar event. The fall is happening *now* in the timeline. See [[Other Locations|Elturel]] and [[Cult of the Dragon]].

## Inner Page Articles

### Skyship Crash Recently Spotted East of Baldur's Gate

> Merchants traveling east of **[[Other Locations|Baldur's Gate]]** report seeing the wreckage of what appears to be a **skyship that crashed somewhere in the wilderness**. The origin of the vessel remains unknown. Treasure hunters and opportunists are already rumored to be searching for the wreckage.

**DM Easter egg — Baldur's Gate 3 reference.** Not a real campaign lead. The Nautiloid crash that opens BG3 happens east of Baldur's Gate. Leave it alone.

### Yartar Booming — The Golden Goose Draws Crowds

> The city of [[Yartar]] continues to grow in wealth and popularity thanks in part to the mysterious gambling hall known as **the Golden Goose**. Known for its luxurious games and extravagant prizes, the Golden Goose has become a destination for nobles, merchants, and thrill-seekers alike. However, **entry is strictly by invitation**, making the establishment one of the most exclusive attractions in the North.

Confirms the invitation-only nature of the **Golden Goose** in Yartar. The illustration shows it as a **massive multi-deck ship** (or ship-shaped building) — visually it reads like a ship, not a building. **The "5,000 gp ticket" we'd heard about elsewhere may be the invitation cost.**

### Cultists on the Loose!

> Authorities warn that **cult activity has increased throughout the Sword Coast**. The most prominent among these groups appears to be the **[[Cult of the Dragon]]**, whose members are reportedly **recruiting new followers in isolated settlements** and among desperate populations. Rumors claim these cultists seek **ancient dragons and dark powers**. Parents are advised to remain vigilant and **keep watch over their children, as recruiters often prey upon the young and impressionable**.

Confirms:
- Cult of the Dragon is *the* public-facing cult threat right now
- They **target children and the young/impressionable** — matches the [[Cult of Cyric]]'s playbook (they too scouted young people via Rulf)
- "**Isolated settlements**" is the recruiting ground — directly relevant to [[The Beliard Mystery]] and [[The Cursed Seeds]] (both isolated settlement plays)

### Dwarven Entrepreneur Seeks Fortune

> A dwarf identifying himself as **[[Gundren Rockseeker]]** is recruiting able-bodied people in the town of [[Phandalin]]. Rockseeker claims to have discovered a promising new mining venture and promises good pay and a share in the profits to those willing to assist in the operation. Interested parties are encouraged to seek him out for details.

Gundren is **publicly advertising for new hires** — this is the same mining venture context as [[Wave Echo Cave]] / the Forge of Spells. He's not done. The two new dwarves at the Miners Exchange (the man and the woman with spectacles) make more sense with this context — he's staffing up.

## Editorial

### From the Editor

> Giants move, cults conspire, and pirates prowl. Many settlements have been reduced to fear and ash. Yet brave adventurers still rise to face the dangers that threaten our world. The Waterdeep Wazoo is here to report on the stories others fear to print. Stay vigilant.

**"Pirates prowl"** — a small addition that doesn't appear in any of our other intel. **Moonshay Isles treasure** rumor (the one we overheard) suddenly fits a bigger pattern.
