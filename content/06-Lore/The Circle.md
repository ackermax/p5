# The Circle

> The **inner ranks of the [[Cult of Cyric]]** — referenced in the letter recovered from [[Skeemo Wierdbottle]]'s back room.

## What We Know

The recovered letter signs from "**The Cult of C**" and refers to "**The Circle**" twice:

> *"The **Circle** turns, and the hour we spoke of draws near."*
>
> *"Fail us, and the **Circle** will remember."*

## What This Implies

- The Circle is a defined body — not just a general term for "the cult"
- It has institutional memory ("the Circle will remember") — meaning it persists across time
- It convenes / "turns" — suggesting either a rotating leadership, a cycle of rituals, or a recurring meeting
- The author of the letter speaks with the Circle's authority — they are *of* the Circle, not pleading to it

## Working Theories

- **Inner ranks / priesthood** of the [[Cult of Cyric]] — the higher initiates who actually direct operations
- **A specific council body** within the cult — possibly the cell that operates in [[Waterdeep]]
- **A ritual structure** — "the Circle turns" could be literal, a ritual phase the cult is moving through
- Connection unknown to the Cyric cell at [[Helm's Hold]] (see [[The Cult in Helms Hold]])

## Open Questions

- Who sits at the Circle?
- Is the Cyric cell at the monastery (where Estelle was raised) part of the Circle, or a different cell?
- Does "the Circle turns" refer to a specific event — a scheduled action — that we should be watching the calendar for?
- The earlier "**we are watching**" message with the **black sun** symbol ([[The Black Sun Watchers]]) — could the watchers be the Circle?
