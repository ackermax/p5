# Order of the Gauntlet

> **Type:** Faction — militant, righteous, *"too straightforward in their approach"* (an old note, presumably a Harper or [[Force Grey]] read)
> **Status:** Friendly / allied
> **Seat we know of:** **The Halls of Justice**, [[Waterdeep]]

> [!important] **Founded by aasimar** *(new, S68)*
> [[Althariel]] told us the **Order of the Gauntlet was created by aasimar**, and that **a group of aasimar at the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]] may be able to help [[Jaak Greye|Jaak]].** This turns a background faction into **an active lead on [[Jaaks Celestial War]] and [[Blood of the Ancients]].**

## What We Know

- **Created by aasimar** *(per [[Althariel]], S68)*.
- **A group of aasimar sits at the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]]** — the people to ask about Jaak's blood.
- **Phandalin presence** via **Daran Edermath** — investigating **undead activity** *(early notes; also written "Darren Endermath")*.
- **They ran the [[The Waterdeep Ultimate Brawl|Waterdeep Ultimate Brawl]]** *(S59)* — we signed their waivers and fought in their tournament.
- **The [[Other Locations|Order of the Gilded Eye]] broke away from them** — glove-with-an-eye symbol, based in [[Helm's Hold]], own agenda. See [[The Cult in Helms Hold]].
- Listed among the **allied factions** alongside the [[Lords Alliance]] — see [[Factions]].
