# Harpers

> Secretive organization opposing tyranny and evil. **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] and [[Jaak Greye]] both carry Harper pins** — strong evidence they are affiliated (or being recruited).

## Ranks

1. **Harp Shadow**
2. **Bright Candle** — **[[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] and [[Jaak Greye]] currently**, promoted by [[Krowen Valharrow]] in **S69** as the payoff of the audience he'd been holding since S66
3. **High Harper** — [[Krowen Valharrow]]

## The Teleportation Circles *(S69)*

**Harper secret.** Its existence is known **only to Bright Candles and High Harpers** — which is why the promotion and the reveal happened in the same conversation.

[[Krowen Valharrow]] granted the party **access to the network**, and **a circle of our own in the [[Phandalin|bastion]]** (still needs materials gathered), with **[[Droop]] as the resident mage who attends it**.

## Iconography In The Party's Possession

- **Harp pin** — both Estelle and Jaak have one
- **Harp bow** — Estelle has one
- **Armor with the Harpers symbol** — found by Sir Joseph
- **Silver Harper Pin** – When you attune to this pin, you can devise a general line of thought of 25 words or fewer. While you wear this pin, a creature that reads your thoughts detects your preprogrammed line of thought instead. A creature that takes the if Study action while reading your thoughts makes a DC 13 Intelligence (In Investigation) check. On a successful check, it becomes aware that the detected thoughts are preprogrammed.

## Known Members & Contacts

- **[[Sister Garaele]]** — at the **Shrine of Luck** in [[Phandalin]]; openly confirmed Harper. Praying to Lady Tymora.
- **[[Jaheira]]** — based in **Baldur's Gate**; helped defeat the **Cult of Bhaal** there; still doing Harper work
- **[[Krowen Valharrow]]** — Archmage of Silverymoon, "Watcher of the Silver Marches" — **confirmed a HIGH HARPER (S66)**, and holds **[[Moongleam Tower]]** outside [[Everlund]]. The highest-ranking Harper we've met face to face. **He wants to speak with [[Jaak Greye]] and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] "as a High Harper, not as an archmage"** once we finish [[The Everlund Apparitions]]. **S69: he paid out [[The Everlund Apparitions]] with *access* — he promoted Jaak and Estelle to Bright Candles and introduced us to the teleportation circles** (above), including one for our own bastion.
- **[[Laeral Silverhand]]** — much more connected to Harpers than publicly admitted; should be asked
- **[[Ilin Cotton]]** — **confirmed Harper** (session 56); was investigating the [[Cult of Cyric]] before he was discovered. Currently missing — last seen at [[Tavern Misty Beard]] in [[Waterdeep]]. See [[Find Ilin Cotton]].

## Open Questions

- **What does a High Harper want with two Bright Candles?** *(S69)* Valharrow paid a mercenary job in **rank and infrastructure**. He did not buy a service; he **kitted out two members** — and then said nothing further. **The promotion was the entire audience.**
- **Who was [[Sister Garaele]]'s [[Moongleam Tower]] contact?** *(S63)* She promised Jaak and Estelle a meeting there about the [[Track the Red Mages|Red Mages]]. Valharrow has now met them twice and **never raised the Red Mages.** **Someone else in that tower, or a lead we still haven't collected.**
- **Are Estelle and Jaak knowingly Harpers, or being moved into position by someone above them?** The pins didn't come from nowhere. **S69 settles half of it — they are formally *in*, at rank 2 of 3.** It does not settle who put them on the ladder.
- **Did their childhood escape involve Harper assistance?** Helping two kids steal back stolen wealth from a Cyric cult is *exactly* Harper work.
- **Were they given the pins recently, or have they had them since the escape?**
- **Who handed out the pins?**

## Strategic Notes

- Working theory: the Harpers have a longer-running interest in **at least one of our PCs** — likely Estelle (Cult of Cyric escapee) or Jaak (Aasimar, target of [[Cult of Cyric]] sacrifice)
- The Harpers' enemy list (Black Network, Cult of the Dragon, evil cults) overlaps heavily with our active enemies. We are functionally allied whether or not we've been formally inducted.
