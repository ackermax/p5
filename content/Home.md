## 📖 Previously, in the Aasimar Temple, Northeast of Everlund — [[Session 68 - Var - 2026-08-17|Session 68]] (2026-08-17)

We fought a pack of **angry ghosts** for **keystone #3** and found **keystone #4** lying in the rubble where the trickster ambushed us last session — and in between, a set of **mournful ghosts** we talked down instead of fighting told us they had **angered something very powerful from a different dimension**, which is now **sending probes into our plane to consume it**. All four keystones went into the **altar**, a **portal** tore open pouring radiant light that hurt just to stand near, and on the far side we found a **caged one-eyed monster** — **[[The Angel]]** — which broke out talking about **cleansing our world**, summoned **flying swords**, bent the plane against us **every round**, and went down hard **swearing that "the Lord" will return to finish the job**. Past the fight, in the cell where it had been held, **the spirit of [[Althariel]]** met us: the **last aasimar of this base**, the one who **brought the Angel here years ago** and was **killed with all his kin** for it — **they are the ghosts**. Then he answered questions nobody has ever answered for us: **[[Jaak Greye|Jaak]] is of the "Thier" blood, out of Celestia, and carries the [[Blood of the Ancients|blood of the ancients]] — and so does [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum|Estelle]]** — blood that makes a person **strong enough to be an avatar, a vessel of the gods**, the very thing **[[Bhaal]]** once tried to inhabit; and **[[Cyric]]**, he said, is **the most dangerous god of all** — a **human who ascended**, who **killed [[Mystra]]** and stopped **all magic**. He pointed Jaak at **a group of aasimar at the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]]** — the **[[Order of the Gauntlet]] was created by aasimar** — **gave him the Sword of Heavens**, and remade the portal to send us home. We short-rested in the tomb, **told the logging camp the ghosts were busted**, took a long rest by the [[Ignitamis]], and **set out for [[Everlund]]** — where [[Krowen Valharrow]] owes us **a reward and an audience**.
