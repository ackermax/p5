# Session 58 - Var — 2026-06-01

> **In-game date:** 
> **Location:** [[Waterdeep]]

## Recap (One Paragraph)

We finished Thomas's job — fought through the rest of the [[Shankers|Shanker]] hideout (past some steam machinery I still can't make sense of), shut down the valve they'd rigged to flush rats back at us, and pulled his locket off the Shanker boss after he bombed himself and his own men rather than hand it over. Back at his base, Thomas came clean: he sits on the [[Council of the Underworld]], and there's a meeting tomorrow evening about *my* situation — and about [[Cardon Malefic]]. He's going to disguise us and walk us in. After handing the rest of the stolen goods to the [[Lords Alliance]] and resting at the Yawning Portal, we spent the next day running down the frost giants on the Northern Beaches, caught one loading captives onto a boat, killed them, and freed the prisoners — more renown for Joseph and Gotrek toward their rank-up.

## Key Events

- We continued to fight Shankers as we kept going through the sewers
- We also saw some steam machinery in the tunnels, but so far could not determine specifically what they do
- We killed a lot and noticed an emergency valve to flush out the rats into the first room. We shut one of the valves and stopped it from being able to be used against us
- We retrieved the locket after the Shanker boss blew himself and his lackeys up with a bomb.
- We also collected other stolen goods from the Shanker hideout
- Headed back to [[Thomas]] and handed him the locket. He informed us he is a member of the [[Council of the Underworld]], and there is a meeting tomorrow evening to discuss my situation and meet with [[Cardon Malefic]]
- He offered to disguise us and take us to the meeting tomorrow night, which we accepted
- We handed the stolen goods off to the [[Lords Alliance]] and headed back to the Yawning Portal to spend the night
- We woke up the next day to take care of the Frost Giant problem, after converting our currency to an easier to carry around amount
- We started following their tracks and eventually from a distance saw a frost giant taking what looked like slaves to a boat with another giant on top.
- We defeated the frost giants, freed the soon-to-be slaves, and reported it to the [[Lords Alliance]], where our two members were told they received some more renown and are working towards the 10 renown they need to rank up.
