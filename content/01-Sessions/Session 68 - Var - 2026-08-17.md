## Key Events

- We kept searching the area and fought some angry ghosts to get the third keystone.
- We found some mournful ghosts who we convinced to not fight us and told us they angered something very powerful from a different dimension who is sending probes into ours to consume the plane.
- We found the last keystone near the rubble where we fought the trickster teleporter that stole one of Gotrek's weapons last session
- We placed the keystones into the altar and a portal appeared, with radiant light emanating from it and damaging us.
- We stepped inside the portal and saw a caged one-eyed monster.
- It said some stuff about needing to cleanse our world, broke out of its cage, and started a combat with us
- It summoned some flying swords to attack us, and seems to have control over this plane to affect us every round
- It is called "Angel" in its tooltip
- As we take it down after a very tough fight, it claims the "lord" will return to cleanse our world, which I assume is its leader, a god
- We make it to the place where it was locked up and the spirit of Althariel, another Aasimar appears
- Altharial thanks us and says he is the one who brought the Angel here years ago before he and his kin were killed, becoming the ghosts we saw on the other side of the portal.
- He says the [[Jaak Greye]] is a celestial, which we knew, but that he has the blood of their
- He also says Jaak and [[Estelle Moonglow Platinum]] both also have ancient bloodlines
- Aasimar may be able to assist Jaak in the Halls of Justice in [[Waterdeep]]
- Aasimar created the order of the gauntlet
- Jaak is given the Sword of Heavens and we are sent back through the remade portal to the tomb, where we short rest to regain our health, and continue back to the logging camp to tell them the ghosts have been busted.
- They thank us, we take a long rest by the airship, and head towards Everlund
