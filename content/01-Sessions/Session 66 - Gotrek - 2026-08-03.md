## Key Events

- Started session in combat with [[Black Earth]] and surrounded by [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Ulfgardians]], likely Sky Ponies lead by [[Arnzan Vashk]]
- Ulfgardians speaking unknown language, we cant communicate
- [[Jaak Greye]] used Major Illusion to display him healing the Ulfgardians leader to convince them that we are friends, and it works beautifully.
- [[The Phandalin Five]] and the [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Ulfgardians]] team up against the [[Black Earth]]
- We killed the female from the [[Black Earth]] and the man escaped
- [[Jaak Greye]] cast tongues and spoke with the tribesman and they took us to mee [[Arnzan Vashk]]
- Jaak evaluated his health, discovering a bad disease needing Greater Restoration.
- We convinced [[Arnzan Vashk]] to come on our sky ship to head to [[Everlund]] to search for somebody to cast that spell.
- We neglected to mention our need of his relic, deciding to wait until after he is healed
- We flew to [[Everlund]] and were greeted by a glowing wisp, belonging to [[Krowen Valharrow]] at [[Moongleam Tower]] just outside the city. It was a communication device and he told us to come to him
- Upon entering the tower, we find [[Droop]] who takes us to [[Krowen Valharrow]]
- Valharrow wants us to help the farmers to the northeast with a ghost issue, and in return he has something to help us that is very valuable (unknown)
- We agree to help, and we pay for Greater Restoration services for 1600g.
- The mage we hired heals [[Arnzan Vashk]] and he is happy and wants to know why we did it, but we decide to have a feast and speak in the morning about it instead (because we had exhaustion and no inspiration)
- Returned to [[Ulfgardian Mounds|One Stone]] and convinced [[Arnzan Vashk]] to give us his relic, which he was happy to do. We found out it was a magical item that lets us cast divination or control weather once every 7 days. 
- Returned back towards [[Everlund]] to take a look into the ghost issue
