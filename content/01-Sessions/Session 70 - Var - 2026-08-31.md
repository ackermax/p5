## Key Events

- We make it back to the airship with the giants head and make our way to Flint Rock.
- When we get there, we do not notice much and make it to the top of the rock where incense is burning
- We remove the altar and find a passage below, where the relic lays
- We are approached by spirits of the Elk clan who ask us our intentions
- We are told in order to receive the relic we need to get the blessing from the Golden Stag
- We make camp for the evening and as we do see the Golden Stag passing along the way.
- We approach it and it asks us questions about how we have worked towards restoring nature's balance
- We each answer, and though it finds us wanting, believes we are far enough on our journey to bless us as the breaking of the Ordening is something that can disrupt that balance
- We camp for the night and make it back underneath the altar, where the mythral spear is
- It is only the tip of the spear, however
- We receive the relic along with the blessing of the spirits of the Elk Tribe
- We make our way to Yartar
- Once we make it there we are stopped outside the town to go and check in at the guard tower
- We are stopped by a magical ward and are to be checked for dangerous items in the guardhouse
- We are met by Oversword Vaelia Thann who lets us know about the check
- Nestra Ruthiol is the Waterbaron of Yartar
- We go through our magic checks and make our way into the town, leaving our sky ship with an older gnome who will fill up the tank and make repairs

## NPCs Met

- **The spirits of the Elk clan** — the dead of [[Ulfgardian Mounds|Flint Rock]]. **They asked our intentions instead of attacking** — the opposite of the Blue Bears — and set the Golden Stag's blessing as their condition
- **[[The Golden Stag]]** — the power whose blessing the Elk spirits required. **Questioned each of us on what we had done to restore nature's balance**, found us **wanting but far enough along**, and blessed us
- **[[Oversword Vaelia Thann]]** — the officer who met us at [[Yartar]]'s guard tower and explained the entry check
- **[[Nestra Ruthiol]]** — **the Waterbaron of [[Yartar]]**; named to us, **not met**
- **An older gnome** — took the [[Ignitamis]] to **fill the tank and make repairs**. **He never gave a name.**
