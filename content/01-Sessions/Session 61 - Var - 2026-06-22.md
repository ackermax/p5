# Session 61 - Var — 2026-06-22

> **In-game date:** 
> **Location:** [[Waterdeep]] — the [[The Waterdeep Ultimate Brawl|arena]]

## Recap (One Paragraph)

We fell to the **flame dragon** in Round 5 of the Monster Arena pretty quickly, thanked [[Barnaby Baxter]], and stayed to watch the singles bracket — [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]] won it and [[Gotrek Runehammer]] went out in the third round, with Joseph earning a **magic tattoo** that can force a creature to roll a 1 (three times per long rest). **[[Amen Voss]]** gave us an **amethyst coin** and sent us to an alley to flip it three times for our rewards; each of us walked away with two magic items, and I picked up a **Robe of Useful Items** and a set of **Sending Stones**. Voss invited us back to shop before we leave [[Waterdeep]] — no refunds, no haggling, no deceiving.
