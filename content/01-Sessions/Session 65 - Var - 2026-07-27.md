## Key Events

- We started where we left off, about to be attacked by a giant water creature in the river.
- It turned out to be a giant (and elder) Plesiosaurus
- We discovered it was lashing out because it had something uncomfortable in its mouth.
- We pulled it out without doing much damage to it, and it thanked us ([[Jaak Greye]] used speak with animals to communicate with it) and left us to keep fishing.
- It turned out to be a tattooing needle with the dominate beast spell, which we gave to [[Sir Joseph Gordon Die Hard]]
- We continued on the way to the One Stone
- On the way, Sir Joseph saw some humanoids heading west away from the one stone, took no heed of them, and continued on our way to One Stone.
- We arrive at One Stone.
- It seems like an island surrounded by a river stemming from a lake. There are stones piled in various places.
- We believe this is an Ulfgardian graveyeard
- As we make it to the graveyard, we encounter two members of the [[Black Earth]] tribe
- [[Jaak Greye]] called them Black Ground instead of Black Earth and they decided to attack us, saying the relic was theirs.
- After the first round of the fight, what seemed to be the Ulfgardians of the Sky Pony tribe, who say something in a language we don't understand and seemingly get ready to fight us and them, not knowing who to trust.
